## Global Agencies: Why Compare This Category?

Global agencies typically serve students from multiple countries and handle applications to several destinations. They may suit you if you are considering Australia alongside other countries, or if you want a single point of contact for a multi-country application strategy. However, their global reach does not guarantee local expertise or consistent service quality across every office. Before engaging any global agency, verify in writing which specific countries, institutions, and services are included in your agreement. Also confirm whether the agency is listed as a registered education agent on the official website of each institution you are applying to, as required under the ESOS framework. The Australian government notes that institutions must record their education agents in PRISMS, but PRISMS is not a public search tool for students. Therefore, you should check institutional websites directly.

## Domestic Service Networks: Why Compare This Category?

Domestic service networks are agencies that operate primarily within a single country, often China, and cater to local students and families. They may offer in-person consultations, Chinese-language support, and a more localized understanding of the application process. This category may suit you if you prefer face-to-face communication or need guidance in your native language. However, the quality and coverage of physical offices can vary, and not all networks have publicly verifiable office lists. Before choosing a domestic network, verify which cities and service teams are actually available, and confirm the scope of services and fees in writing. Also check whether the agency is a registered education agent for your target institutions.

## Local Specialists: Why Compare This Category?

Local specialists are agencies based in Australia, often with deep knowledge of a specific city or region. They may suit you if you want on-the-ground support, local expertise, and a team that understands the Australian education system from the inside. However, their geographic focus can be a limitation if you are applying to institutions outside their area. Always verify that the agency can handle applications to your chosen institutions, and confirm whether they provide any services beyond application execution, such as accommodation or visa advice. Note that education agents typically cannot provide regulated immigration legal advice, so for visa questions you may need to consult a registered migration agent.

## FAQ

### How do I verify that an agency is registered with Australian institutions?

The Australian government requires institutions to record their education agents in PRISMS, but PRISMS is not a public search tool. Instead, you should visit the official website of each institution you are applying to and look for their list of registered education agents. If the agency is not listed, it may not be authorized to represent that institution.

### What should I ask an agency before signing a contract?

Ask for a written breakdown of all fees and services, including which institutions and countries are covered, what each stage of the application includes, and whether there are any additional charges. Also ask about the agency’s policy on refunds and what happens if your application is unsuccessful. The Study Australia website advises comparing service fees and getting a written agreement that specifies what you are paying for.

### Can an education agent provide immigration advice?

Education agents can help you compare courses and process applications, but they typically cannot provide regulated immigration legal advice. For visa-related questions, you may need to consult a registered migration agent. Always check the credentials of anyone giving you immigration advice.

### Do free tools cover all application costs?

No. Free tools and platforms may offer free application assistance, but this only covers their own services. You will still be responsible for school application fees, government visa fees, and any other third-party charges. Always read the terms to understand what is included.
