Bangkok offers some of the best-value student housing in the world. Modern condo studios with pools, gyms, and 24-hour security can be found for the price of a shared room in London. The BTS Skytrain and MRT subway make commuting easy, and the food is legendary.

### Chulalongkorn University (Siam area)

**Siam Square & Sam Yan**: The immediate area around Chula. Modern condo buildings mixed with older apartment blocks. Walking distance to campus. Condo studios ฿8,000–15,000/month (USD 230–430).

**Phaya Thai & Ratchathewi**: 1–3 BTS stops from Siam. More residential, excellent street food. ฿6,000–12,000/month.

**Ari**: 3 BTS stops north. Bangkok's hip neighborhood — craft coffee, vintage shops, and Michelin-recommended street stalls. ฿7,000–14,000/month.

### Thammasat University (Tha Phra Chan — Riverside)

**Banglamphu & Khao San Road area**: The old city. Thammasat's riverside campus is surrounded by temples and the backpacker zone. Older apartments with character. ฿5,000–10,000/month.

**Rattanakosin & Phra Nakhon**: Bangkok's historic centre. Quieter than the tourist strips, short walk to campus. ฿5,000–9,000/month.

### Mahidol University (Salaya — Nakhon Pathom)

Mahidol's main campus is in Salaya, about 25 km west of central Bangkok. On-campus dorms from ฿3,500–7,000/month. Nearby apartments and condos in Salaya from ฿4,000–8,000/month. The new SRT Light Red Line connects Salaya to Bang Sue in 30 minutes.

## Cost of Living

Monthly student budget in Bangkok:

- **Condo studio**: 6,000–12,000
- **Food (street + restaurants)**: 5,000–8,000
- **BTS/MRT transport**: 1,000–2,000
- **Phone + Internet**: 500–1,000
- **Total**: **12,500–23,000**


That's USD 360–660/month — among the cheapest in the world for a capital city.

## FAQ

**Do condos require a long lease?** Most require a 12-month lease, but 6-month and monthly options exist at a premium. International students often negotiate successfully.
**Can I manage without Thai language?** In student areas near Chula/Thammasat, yes. English is widely spoken. Lease contracts will be in Thai — have a friend or agent translate.
