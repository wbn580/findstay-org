Hong Kong is one of the world's most densely populated cities, and its housing market reflects that — small spaces, high prices, and incredible efficiency. For students at HKU, CUHK, CityU, PolyU, and HKUST, university accommodation is the first and best option, but private alternatives exist.

## University Halls

HK universities offer on-campus or nearby halls that are heavily subsidized.

- **HKU**: Residential halls in the Western District, including the new Jockey Club Student Village. HKD 10,000–25,000/semester.
- **CUHK**: 23 undergraduate and postgraduate halls on campus in Sha Tin. HKD 8,000–18,000/semester.
- **CityU**: Student Residence in Kowloon Tong. HKD 12,000–20,000/semester.
- **PolyU**: Hung Hom Halls, steps from campus. HKD 10,000–20,000/semester.

## Private Rentals

### HKU (Western District / Kennedy Town)
- **Kennedy Town**: The new MTR extension has transformed this area. 10 min to HKU. Rooms HKD 6,000–12,000/month.
- **Sai Ying Pun**: Between HKU and Central. Trendy with coffee shops and galleries. Rooms HKD 7,000–14,000/month.

### CUHK (Sha Tin / Tai Po)
- **Sha Tin**: Near campus, major shopping at New Town Plaza. Rooms HKD 5,000–9,000/month.
- **Tai Po**: Further north, cheaper. Rooms HKD 4,000–7,000/month. Great cycling infrastructure.

### CityU / BaptistU (Kowloon Tong)
- **Kowloon Tong**: Premium residential area. Rooms HKD 7,000–15,000/month.
- **Sham Shui Po**: Working-class neighborhood with legendary street food markets. 10 min MTR. Rooms HKD 4,500–8,000/month.

## Co-Living Spaces

Hong Kong has seen a co-living boom. Operators like Dash Living, Weave Living, and The Nate offer furnished rooms with weekly cleaning and social events. HKD 8,000–15,000/month. Ideal for international students who want a turn-key solution.
