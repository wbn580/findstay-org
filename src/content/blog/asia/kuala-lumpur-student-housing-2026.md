Kuala Lumpur offers some of the best-value student housing in the world — modern condo studios with infinity pools and gyms for less than the price of a London room share. Universiti Malaya (Malaysia's top university), UKM, Taylor's, and Monash Malaysia create a diverse international student community.

### Universiti Malaya (Lembah Pantai)

**Section 17 Petaling Jaya**: The main UM student area. Walkable to campus, established housing stock from the 1970s-90s, excellent hawker food. Rooms MYR 400–800/month (USD 90–180). Among the cheapest university-adjacent housing anywhere.

**Bangsar South**: Modern condo towers 5 min drive from UM. Linked by the LRT. The Sphere mall has grocery stores and restaurants. Studios MYR 1,200–1,800/month. Higher-end but still cheap by global standards.

**Pantai Dalam**: Between UM and the LRT station. Older apartments, popular with budget-conscious students. Rooms MYR 300–600/month.

### Taylor's University, Sunway University & Monash Malaysia (Bandar Sunway)

The education hub of Malaysia. Sunway City hosts multiple university campuses in one planned township. Modern condo buildings with resort-style amenities (pools, gyms, even bowling alleys). Sunway Pyramid mall is one of Malaysia's largest. Rooms MYR 600–1,200/month. The BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) connects to the LRT network.

### The Condo Standard

Malaysian student condos have amenities that would cost 3–5× more in the West: infinity pools, fully equipped gyms, function rooms, 24-hour security, and sometimes even badminton courts and karaoke rooms. All included in rent.

### Foreign Ownership Rules

International students can rent freely — no special restrictions. The process is straightforward: find a unit on PropertyGuru or iProperty, contact the agent (typically WhatsApp), view, and sign. Most leases are 12 months with 2+1 month deposit (2 months rent + 1 month utilities deposit).

### FAQ

**Is KL safe?** Yes, especially in the student and expat areas (Bangsar, PJ, Sunway). Standard urban awareness applies.
**Do I need a car in KL?** It's a driving city, but the LRT/MRT network has improved dramatically. University areas near stations (UM, UKM) are doable without a car. Sunway has the BRT.
**Monthly budget?** MYR 1,500–3,000/month (USD 340–680). This includes rent, food (eating out is cheap), transport, and entertainment. Possibly the best student-value city in the world.
