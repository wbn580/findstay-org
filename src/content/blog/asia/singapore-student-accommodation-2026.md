Singapore is Asia's education powerhouse — home to two universities in the global top 20 (NUS #8, NTU #15, QS 2026) and a booming international student population. Housing is expensive compared to other Asian cities, but the quality, safety, and convenience are exceptional.

## On-Campus Housing

### NUS (Kent Ridge)
NUS guarantees on-campus housing for first-year international students. Halls of Residence (catered) cost SGD 500–800/month. Residential Colleges (RCs) integrate living and learning with seminar rooms and dining halls — SGD 700–1,000/month.

### NTU (Jurong West)
NTU has 24 Halls of Residence, the largest on-campus housing system in Singapore. Rooms from SGD 265–605/month depending on room type (single/double, with/without aircon). Apply through NTU's housing portal.

### SMU (City Campus)
SMU's city centre location means limited on-campus housing. Prinsep Street Residences offer 255 rooms at SGD 700–900/month.

## Off-Campus Housing

### HDB Flats
Singapore's public housing covers 80% of the population. Renting a room in an HDB flat is the most affordable off-campus option.

- **Clementi & Dover** (near NUS): SGD 700–1,200/month for a room
- **Jurong West & Pioneer** (near NTU): SGD 600–1,000/month
- **Bras Basah & Bencoolen** (near SMU): SGD 900–1,500/month

### Private Condos
Higher-end option with pools, gyms, and security. Rooms SGD 1,200–2,000/month.

## Cost of Living

Monthly budget for an international student in Singapore:

- **Rent (HDB room)**: 700–1,200
- **Food (hawker centres)**: 300–500
- **Transport (MRT/bus)**: 60–120
- **Phone + Internet**: 30–60
- **Total**: **1,090–1,880**


## FAQ

**Is it easy to get on-campus housing at NUS?** First-year international students get priority. After year one, you'll likely need to find off-campus housing, which is competitive but manageable.

**What's the cheapest way to eat in Singapore?** Hawker centres. A full meal costs SGD 3.50–6.50. Chicken rice, laksa, char kway teow — world-class food at budget prices.
