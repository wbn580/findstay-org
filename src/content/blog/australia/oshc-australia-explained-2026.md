If you're an international student heading to Australia, OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover) isn't optional — it's a visa condition. Every Subclass 500 visa holder must maintain OSHC for the entire duration of their stay. But not all OSHC policies are created equal. Here's what you need to know before buying.

## What OSHC Covers

OSHC is basic health insurance designed to cover:
- **Doctor (GP) visits** — 100% of the MBS (Medicare Benefits Schedule) fee at bulk-billing clinics
- **Hospital treatment** — shared ward accommodation in a public hospital
- **Ambulance** — emergency transport (critical — ambulance rides cost AUD 400–6,000 without cover)
- **Prescription medicines** — up to AUD 50 per item, capped at AUD 300/year for singles

## What OSHC Does NOT Cover

- Dental (except emergency hospital dental)
- Optical (glasses, contact lenses, eye exams)
- Physiotherapy, chiropractic, psychology
- Pre-existing conditions (usually a 12-month waiting period)
- Pregnancy (12-month waiting period for obstetric services)

Most students add **extras cover** for dental and optical — typically AUD 15–30/month extra.

## When to Buy OSHC

You need OSHC from the day you arrive in Australia. Most universities include OSHC in your Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) package, but you can choose your own provider — just make sure the coverage start date matches your arrival.

**Pro tip**: If your university arranges OSHC for you, you can still switch providers after arrival. The policy is yours, not the university's.

## FAQ

### Can I buy OSHC after arriving in Australia?
You must have OSHC from day one of your visa. Buy it before you travel. You can switch providers after arrival.

### What happens if my OSHC expires?
Your visa compliance is at risk. Immigration (Home Affairs) can cancel your student visa if you don't maintain OSHC.

### Is OSHC the same as travel insurance?
No. [SafetyWing](https://safetywing.com/?affiliate_id=17632) and other travel insurers cover different things (trip cancellation, lost luggage, COVID quarantine). Some students buy both OSHC + travel insurance for full coverage.

### Can I use OSHC for dental and optical?
Only hospital dental is covered. For regular check-ups, glasses, and contact lenses, you need extras cover.
