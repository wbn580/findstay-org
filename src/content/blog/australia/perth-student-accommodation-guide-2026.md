Perth is Australia's most underrated student city. It has beautiful beaches, a growing tech sector, and housing costs that make Sydney and Melbourne students weep with envy. The 2026 rental market is competitive but far more accessible than the east coast capitals.

## Perth Housing Market 2026

The median rent for a one-bedroom apartment in central Perth is AUD 420/week — the lowest of any Australian capital. The vacancy rate is 1.8%, slightly better than Sydney and Melbourne. Combined with lower grocery and transport costs, Perth offers the best overall value for budget-conscious international students.

![Perth skyline from Kings Park](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1598928506311-c55z7bef91d5?w=1600)

## UWA — Crawley & Nedlands

The University of Western Australia sits on a stunning campus along the Swan River, surrounded by Perth's most expensive suburbs.

### Crawley & Nedlands
The closest suburbs. Mostly large family homes with granny flats or shared arrangements. Rooms AUD 200–320/week. Expensive by Perth standards but excellent lifestyle.

### Subiaco
Trendy, walkable, and on the train line. Subiaco has a great market, cafes, and a 10-minute bus ride to UWA. Rooms AUD 220–340/week.

### Claremont
Further west but on the Fremantle train line. Claremont Quarter is a premium shopping centre. Rooms AUD 200–300/week.

## Curtin University — Bentley

Curtin is in Bentley, about 6 km southeast of the CBD.

### Bentley & Wilson
The immediate suburbs. Older houses, large blocks, and a high concentration of student sharehouses. Rooms AUD 170–260/week — some of the cheapest near-campus housing in Australia.

### Victoria Park
"The Vic Park strip" on Albany Highway is packed with restaurants and bars. 5-minute bus to Curtin. Rooms AUD 180–280/week.

### Cannington
Home to Westfield Carousel (one of Perth's biggest malls) and on the Armadale train line. Rooms AUD 160–240/week.

## ECU — Joondalup & Mount Lawley

### Joondalup
ECU's northern campus is 25 km from the CBD but self-contained. Joondalup has Lakeside Shopping Centre, a hospital, and affordable housing. Rooms AUD 150–230/week.

### Mount Lawley
ECU's arts campus near the city. Trendy suburb with Beaufort Street's cafe strip. Rooms AUD 180–280/week.

## Murdoch University

### Murdoch & Bateman
Southern suburbs with affordable sharehousing. Close to Fiona Stanley Hospital (useful for nursing students). Rooms AUD 160–250/week.

## Why Perth Makes Financial Sense

A monthly budget comparison (shared housing):

- **Sydney**: AUD 1,100 · AUD 200 · AUD 500 · AUD 1,800
- **Melbourne**: AUD 960 · AUD 175 · AUD 450 · AUD 1,585
- **Perth**: **AUD 760** · **AUD 150** · **AUD 420** · **AUD 1,330**


Perth saves you roughly AUD 470/month vs Sydney — that's AUD 5,640/year, or two return flights home.

## FAQ

### Is Perth too isolated for international students?
Perth is geographically isolated but well-connected. Direct flights to Singapore (5 hours), Bali (3.5 hours), and Dubai (11 hours). The timezone (GMT+8) works well for students from Asia.

### Do I need a car in Perth?
Not essential if you live near campus. The Transperth train network is good, but buses can be infrequent in outer suburbs. A car helps for weekend beach trips.

### Is OSHC mandatory in Perth?
Yes — [OSHC is required](https://agents.flywire.com/services/Unilink/oshc-au?referrer=0df195ef-7f4d-4faf-82e2-1878faa84597) for all international students in Australia, including Perth.
