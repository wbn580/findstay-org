Dublin combines world-class universities (Trinity College ranks in the global top 100) with legendary craic and a booming tech sector. The student housing market mirrors the city's overall housing crisis — expensive and competitive, but far from impossible with the right approach.

## Trinity College Dublin (City Centre)

**Trinity Hall (on-campus)**: Trinity reserves rooms in Trinity Hall in Dartry (Rathmines area) for first-year students. €800–1,100/month including utilities. 20 min walk or short bus.

**Rathmines & Ranelagh**: South of the Grand Canal. Trendy, excellent food and pubs. Rooms €700–1,100/month.

**Phibsborough**: North of the Liffey, near the new TU Dublin Grangegorman campus. Up-and-coming. Rooms €600–900/month.

## UCD (Belfield)

UCD's campus is in the southern suburbs in Belfield.

**UCD Residences**: On-campus housing for first-years and international students from €800–1,100/month.

**Clonskeagh & Donnybrook**: Walking distance to UCD. Quiet, leafy, expensive. Rooms €700–1,100/month.

**Stillorgan & Dundrum**: 15–20 min bus. Dundrum Town Centre is Ireland's largest shopping mall. Rooms €600–900/month.

## DCU (Glasnevin)

**DCU campus accommodation**: From €700–950/month.

**Glasnevin & Drumcondra**: Walking distance. Traditional Dublin suburbs. Rooms €600–900/month.

**Ballymun**: North of DCU. Cheaper but rougher reputation (though improving). Rooms €500–750/month.

## "Digs" — The Irish Solution

In Ireland, "digs" means renting a room in a family home, often with meals included. It's a uniquely Irish housing type — more personal than a flatshare, less institutional than a dorm. €150–200/week including bills and some meals. Find them on Daft.ie and HostingPower.ie.

## FAQ

**Why is Dublin housing so expensive?** High demand from tech workers (Google, Meta, Stripe all have large Dublin offices) competing with students. Limited construction during the post-2008 crash created a supply deficit that still exists.
**What's the best time to search?** May–July for September start. August is chaotic.
**Insurance needed?** Non-EU students need private health insurance. EU students use EHIC. [SafetyWing](https://safetywing.com/?affiliate_id=17632) covers interim periods.
