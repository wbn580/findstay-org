Student feedback highlights a mix of strong staff support and facility concerns that prospective residents should consider when choosing housing.

These reviews suggest that while the university’s administrative support and ranking are positive, on-campus facilities such as bathrooms and the cafeteria need improvement. For students planning to live off campus, the condition of university facilities may influence how much time they spend on campus versus at their accommodation.

## City Context: Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi is the capital of the United Arab Emirates, a modern city with a mix of urban districts and suburban developments. The university is situated in Zayed City, a developing area along Al Ain Road, approximately 30–40 minutes by car from the city centre (depending on traffic). The surrounding neighbourhood includes residential compounds, some commercial services, and limited public transport options.

Public buses operate on major routes, but frequency and coverage are sparse compared to the city centre. Most students rely on private cars, shared taxis, or ride-hailing services (e.g., Uber, Careem). For international students without a vehicle, proximity to campus and access to daily essentials (supermarkets, pharmacies, eateries) becomes a key housing factor.

The UAE’s rental market is typically priced in UAE dirhams (AED) and leases are often annual. Prices vary significantly between shared villas, studio apartments, and one-bedroom flats in compounds. Because the campus is located on the outskirts, accommodation in nearby compounds (such as Al Reef, Al Ghadeer, or Zayed City itself) tends to be more affordable than downtown.

## Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

**Walk to campus:** Accommodation within a 15-minute walk of the university is limited to a few immediate residential blocks on Al Ain Road. Most housing options are in gated compounds that require driving. If you find a unit within walking distance (rare), you save on transport costs and time, but the immediate neighbourhood offers few amenities (shops, cafes). The school address is “9H77+CRH - Al Ain Rd - Zayed City - MZ39 - Abu Dhabi.” Walking distance is defined as roughly 1 km.

**Transit / drive commute:** The vast majority of students rely on a car or taxi. A 10–20 minute drive opens up compounds like Al Ghadeer (about 15 minutes) or Al Reef (about 20 minutes), which have supermarkets, parks, and gyms. Public bus routes (e.g., route 44 or 45) run along Al Ain Road but may have 30–60 minute headways. The cost of regular taxi rides (AED 10–20 per trip one way) adds AED 300–600 per month if commuting daily.

**Trade-off:** Living closer to campus means less commuting time but fewer lifestyle options. Living farther (10–20 km) gives better shopping and recreation but adds monthly transport costs. For students who value a lively environment, the drive is usually worth it. For those focused purely on study, a walkable apartment (if available) is more efficient.
