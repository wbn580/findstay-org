### City Context: Washington, D.C.

American University is located at 4400 Massachusetts Ave NW, in the northwest quadrant of the city. This area – Tenleytown and neighboring Friendship Heights – is residential, quieter than downtown D.C., and served by the Red Line metro. Students frequently commute to internships on Capitol Hill, in Dupont Circle, or in the central business district.

D.C.’s cost of living is higher than the U.S. national average. Groceries, dining out, and utilities all reflect the capital’s premium. However, the campus itself offers reasonably priced dining (as noted in reviews), and the surrounding neighborhoods provide a mix of chain supermarkets, local cafés, and convenience stores within walking distance.

Public safety is an important consideration. The Tenleytown area is known for lower crime rates relative to other parts of the city, and many students feel comfortable walking during daylight hours. Shuttle services and ride‑share options are widely used for late‑evening returns.

### Budget Bands

Housing costs near American University vary significantly by distance from campus and amenity level. The following are indicative bands for the 2026‑2027 academic year – confirm actual prices with each operator before committing.

- **Economy (shared spaces / longer commute):** Rooms in shared apartments or houses located 30–45 minutes away by metro (e.g., along the Red Line toward Glenmont or Shady Grove) can be found in the lower range. Expect to share a bathroom and kitchen with 2–4 other tenants.
- **Standard (private room, 15–25 minute commute):** A private bedroom in a shared apartment or a studio within 1–2 miles of campus typically falls in the mid‑range. Many purpose‑built student residences near Tenleytown or Friendship Heights offer single rooms with shared common areas.
- **Premium (walk to campus / private unit):** Studios or one‑bedroom apartments within a 10‑minute walk of the main gate command the highest rates. These units often include in‑unit laundry, fitness facilities, and concierge services.

*All prices above are indicative and subject to change – confirm directly with the housing operator for exact 2026‑2027 rates.*

### Walk vs Transit Trade‑off

American University’s main campus is not immediately adjacent to a metro station; the closest stop is **Tenleytown‑AU (Red Line)**, about a 20‑minute walk or a short shuttle ride. The university operates a free shuttle connecting the campus to the metro station during term time.

- **Walking distance (under 15 minutes):** Apartments along Massachusetts Ave NW, near the intersection with Nebraska Ave, or in the immediate Tenleytown neighborhood allow true walk‑to‑campus convenience. Trade‑off: you pay for that proximity, and the immediate retail offering is limited to a few restaurants, a grocery store, and some shops.
- **Transit reliant (metro + shuttle or bus):** Living farther along the Red Line (e.g., Cleveland Park, Woodley Park, Bethesda) reduces rent significantly. The trade‑off is 30–50 minutes of combined travel time per direction. During peak hours the shuttle can be crowded; allow an extra buffer.
- **Bike‑friendly:** D.C. has a Capital Bikeshare station near campus, and many streets have bike lanes. A 15‑minute bike ride can widen your housing options to areas like Cathedral Heights or Van Ness.
