### City Context: Kuala Lumpur and Taman Teknologi Malaysia

APU is located at Jalan Teknologi 5, Taman Teknologi Malaysia, 57000 Kuala Lumpur. This area is part of a technology park in the southern Kuala Lumpur region, near suburbs like Bukit Jalil and Sri Petaling. It is a purpose-built commercial and educational zone, so immediate surroundings are more business-oriented than residential. Most student housing is concentrated in neighbouring residential suburbs.

Kuala Lumpur is a dense, affordable city by global standards. Public transport includes the LRT (Light Rail Transit), MRT, buses, and ride-hailing services like Grab. The nearest LRT station to APU is about a 10–15 minute drive or bus ride; walking from the station to campus is not always practical due to limited pedestrian infrastructure in the tech park. Many students rely on shuttles, private transport, or living within walking distance of campus.

### Budget Bands

Student housing near APU falls into three broad categories. Prices are indicative – always confirm with the operator for 2026–2027.

- **Affordable (shared rooms or basic on-campus dorms):** These options typically provide a bed and shared facilities. On-campus residences are managed by the university. If you are comfortable with shared living, this can be the most cost-effective choice. Monthly costs generally range from RM 500 to RM 800 per person.
- **Mid-range (private room in a shared apartment or condo):** Many students rent rooms in nearby condominiums such as those in Taman Teknologi or Bukit Jalil. These offer more privacy and often include amenities like a pool or gym. Expect to pay RM 1,000 to RM 1,800 per month for a room.
- **Premium (studio or 1-bedroom apartment):** Quiet, self-contained units are available within a 15-minute drive. These are ideal for couples or those who prefer solitude. Monthly rent is typically RM 2,000 to RM 3,500.

All figures are indicative. The actual price depends on the exact location, facilities, and lease terms. Always confirm directly with the landlord or residence operator.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

Choosing between walking distance to campus and using transit depends on your priorities.

**Walk to campus:** The only way to walk easily from home to APU is to live inside the Taman Teknologi Malaysia park itself. A few apartments and townhouses exist within the park, but availability is limited. The advantage is zero commute time and no transport cost. The downside is that these units may be more expensive per square foot, and the park itself has limited grocery shops or restaurants – you would still need transport for weekly shopping.

![Asia Pacific University of Technology and Innovation (APU) Malaysia](https://img.unistay.net/housing/p/uh-s00479746.jpg)

**Transit commuting:** Most students choose to live in nearby suburbs like Bukit Jalil, Sri Petaling, or Bandar Tasik Selatan. These areas are well-served by LRT and buses. The trade-off is a commute of 20 to 40 minutes each way, depending on traffic and the exact route. However, you gain access to more affordable housing, more dining options, and a livelier neighbourhood. Many students use a combination of Grab or shuttle to cover the last kilometre from the LRT station to campus.

The optimal choice depends on your budget and tolerance for commuting. If you prefer to walk, start searching early for the limited on-campus or park residences. If you want more housing choices and lower costs, accept the daily commute.

### Shortlist Logic for Nearby Residences

When evaluating specific housing options, consider these factors in order of priority:

1. **Distance to campus (walk or shuttle).** Check if the building offers a free shuttle to APU. Many condos in Bukit Jalil run shuttle buses. If not, calculate the combined time for walking to the nearest LRT, riding, and then waiting for a connector.
2. **Safety and security.** Look for 24-hour security, CCTV, and good lighting. The Taman Teknologi area is generally safe, but individual buildings vary. Read recent Google reviews of the apartment complex itself.
3. **Amenities and nearby shops.** A grocery store, pharmacy, and eating outlets within a short walk or one LRT stop can save time. Bukit Jalil has an Aeon mall, while Sri Petaling has many street food options.
4. **Lease terms and utilities.** Most landlords require a one-year lease. Check if water, electricity, and internet are included. Student housing near APU often charges separate utility bills.
5. **Roommates.** If you want to share, find roommates through university notice boards or social media groups before signing a lease. This can reduce individual rent by 30–50%.

A practical shortlist would include:

- On-campus residences (first choice if available and within budget – confirm directly with APU accommodation office)
- Condominiums along Jalan Teknologi within the tech park (e.g., certain serviced apartments – check current listings)
- Bukit Jalil apartments such as those near LRT station (balance of cost and convenience)
- Sri Petaling townhouses or low-rise flats (more local character, often cheaper)

Always visit the property in person or do a live video tour before committing. The negative review about the Open Day experience suggests that not everything is as advertised – apply the same caution to housing.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Does APU offer on-campus housing?**  
Yes, APU has student residences on or near the campus. They are managed directly by the university. Availability can be limited, especially during the start of semesters. Contact the APU accommodation office for 2026–2027 room options and indicative rates.

**2. How far is the city centre from the campus?**  
APU is located about 20 km south of Kuala Lumpur’s city centre (KLCC). By car or Grab the trip takes 25–40 minutes depending on traffic. The closest LRT line connects from Bukit Jalil to KL Sentral in about 25 minutes, but a feeder bus or shuttle is needed to get from the station to campus.

**3. What is the average monthly rent for a room in a shared apartment near APU?**  
Indicative rent for a private room in a shared condo in Bukit Jalil or Sri Petaling ranges from RM 1,000 to RM 1,800. For a bed in a shared dormitory, expect RM 500–800. These figures are for mid-2026 – you must confirm with the landlord or operator as prices fluctuate.
