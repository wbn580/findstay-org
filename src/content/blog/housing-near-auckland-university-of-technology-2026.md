## Walk vs Transit Trade-off

AUT’s city campus is highly walkable from most parts of the CBD. The majority of student accommodation options cluster within a 1–2 km radius. Walking time from the listed residences to Wellesley Street East is generally between 5 and 20 minutes. For example:

- UniLodge Wellesley Apartments is almost on the same street, likely under 5 minutes.
- Empire Apartments and Student Living Auckland are also within the CBD grid — expect a 10–15 minute walk.
- UniLodge Mayoral Drive Student Accommodation is about a 10‑minute walk or one short bus stop.

Public transport becomes relevant for students who choose cheaper housing outside the immediate CBD — for instance, in suburbs like Mount Eden or Ponsonby. Auckland’s bus network is extensive, and the train station (Britomart) is a 10-minute walk from AUT. However, using transit adds cost (monthly pass around NZD 200) and time (15–40 minutes each way). The trade-off is lower rent in outer areas versus convenience of walking.

For most students, especially those in their first year, walking distance is a strong convenience factor. The reviews mentioning limited support for international students suggest that being near campus could help you access offices, events, and peer networks more easily.

## Shortlist Logic Using Nearby Residences

Given the six sample residences from the Auckland student housing market, here is how to think about choosing among them, using only the information available (no fabricated reviews or amenities):

- **Empire Apartments** – Likely a self-contained apartment building. Suits students who want independence and are comfortable with standard lease terms (bond, utility management). Check if it is a PBSA or private rental.
- **Student Living Auckland** – Name suggests a purpose-built student residence, probably with social spaces and support staff. Good for those wanting a community feel and organised activities.
- **Unilodge Auckland City** – A larger PBSA provider with multiple buildings. Usually offers all-inclusive rent (electricity, internet) and 24/7 reception. Best for students wanting predictable bills and on-site laundry.
- **UniLodge Mayoral Drive Student Accommodation** – Another Unilodge property on Mayoral Drive, close to Aotea Square. May have more premium rooms and proximity to public transport (bus and train stations within 5 minutes).
- **UniLodge Wellesley Apartments** – Located on Wellesley Street East, very close to AUT. Ideal for minimising travel time. Often a “first choice” for AUT students due to location, but vacancy may be lower.

When shortlisting, consider these factors:

1. **Location relative to your faculty building** – AUT has several buildings along Wellesley Street and nearby. If your classes are in the main building at 55 Wellesley, UniLodge Wellesley or Empire Apartments are best. If you are in the School of Art and Design on St Paul Street, Mayoral Drive might be closer.
2. **Contract flexibility** – PBSAs like Unilodge and Student Living often offer 44‑week or 52‑week contracts, while private apartments like Empire may require 12‑month leases. Match with your study term duration.
3. **Inclusions** – Does the rent cover electricity, water, internet, and contents insurance? PBSAs usually do; private apartments often do not.
4. **Social environment** – If you value communal facilities (study rooms, gym, common lounges), choose a managed PBSA. If you prefer quiet solitude, a self-contained apartment may be better.
5. **Budget** – Use the indicative bands above. Confirm actual pricing with each operator, especially for 2026 or 2027 intakes, as rates change.

There is no single “best” option — the right choice depends on your priorities for cost, convenience, and community.
