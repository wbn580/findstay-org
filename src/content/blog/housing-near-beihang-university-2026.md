## What Campus Reviews Reveal About Life at Beihang University

## City Context: Haidian District and Beijing

Beijing is China’s capital and a major global city. Beihang University sits in Haidian District, the city’s academic hub, home to numerous universities and tech companies. The area is well-connected by metro lines (Line 10, Line 13, and Line 15 intersect near the campus), buses, and ride-hailing services.

Key characteristics of the neighbourhood:

- **Student population**: Dense with Chinese and international students, creating a lively, study-oriented atmosphere.
- **Amenities**: Plenty of affordable eateries, convenience stores, bookshops, and parks (e.g., Yuyuantan Park within walking distance).
- **Safety**: Haidian is considered a safe area, with low street crime.
- **Weather**: Beijing has four distinct seasons – hot summers (peaking at 35°C) and cold, dry winters (as low as -10°C). Housing with good heating and air conditioning is important.

**Band 1: Budget (shared accommodation)**

- Shared room in an apartment or student dormitory-style building: 2,500–4,000 RMB per month
- Typically includes utilities, sometimes meals (in canteen-style setups)
- Location: 10–20 minutes by metro from campus

**Band 2: Mid-range (private room in shared flat)**

- Private room in a 2–3 bedroom apartment: 5,000–8,000 RMB per month
- Usually includes basic furniture, kitchen access, and utilities (except sometimes electricity)
- Location: 5–15 minutes by metro or 15–30 minutes walking

**Band 3: Upper-mid (studio or small one-bedroom)**

- Studio or 1-bedroom apartment near campus: 7,000–12,000 RMB per month
- Often includes air conditioning, heating, and basic appliances
- Location: within 1–2 km of campus (walkable)

**Band 4: Premium (larger 1-bedroom or 2-bedroom)**

- 1-bedroom or 2-bedroom apartment in newer buildings: 10,000–18,000 RMB per month
- May include gym, security, and concierge services
- Location: usually within 1 km of campus or a short metro ride

Note: Rent can increase during peak enrolment periods (August–September). Always negotiate the final price and read the contract carefully, especially regarding deposit and notice period.

## Walk vs Transit Trade-off

### Walking

Living within a 15-minute walk of Beihang University means easy access to classes, libraries, labs, and social events. The main campus is compact, and many lecture halls are near the east and south gates.

- **Advantages**: No commute costs, no schedule dependency, more time for study and rest.
- **Disadvantages**: Rent per square metre is higher within this circle. Apartments tend to be older or in traditional “hutong” neighbourhoods. Noise from traffic or street vendors can be common.

### Public Transit

A 5–10 minute metro ride opens up cheaper and more varied housing options. Metro Line 10 (terminals at Xitucheng and Zhichunli stations) and Line 13 (at Wudaokou station) serve the area.

- **Advantages**: More affordable rent, newer buildings (e.g., near Liudaokou or Wudaokou), larger living spaces, quieter surroundings.
- **Disadvantages**: Monthly transit cost (around 200–300 RMB for frequent travel), time spent commuting (15–25 minutes door-to-door), weather exposure.

**Typical trade-off**: Renting a private room 15 minutes away by metro may cost 30–40% less than a similar room within walking distance.
