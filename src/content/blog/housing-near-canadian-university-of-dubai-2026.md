### Walk vs Transit Trade-off

**Walking distance** from campus is ideal but expensive. City Walk itself has limited affordable student housing — most apartments are luxury or short-term holiday homes. Walking is possible if you live in Al Wasl or Jumeirah 1, but rents there are high. Students who can afford AED 4,000+ per month for a studio may find options within a 15-minute walk.

**Transit** is the realistic choice for most. The Dubai Metro Red Line and numerous bus routes connect City Walk to cheaper areas. Key transit hubs: Dubai Mall Station and Business Bay Station. From those, a short bus or taxi ride (5–10 minutes) gets you to campus. Monthly metro passes cost around AED 250–350, and taxis from the station to City Walk cost about AED 15–25.

Given the parking nightmare described in reviews, driving is strongly discouraged unless you have a reserved spot. If you must drive, choose a residence with included parking (e.g., Al Sufouh Suites may have parking).
