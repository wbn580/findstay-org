## City Context: Campus and Surrounding Area

Chandigarh University is located on the NH-05 highway, about 10–12 km from the city centre of Mohali (also spelled Mohali, part of the Chandigarh Tricity region). The area is a mix of educational institutions, residential colonies, and developing commercial zones. The campus itself is self-contained, with academic blocks, hostels, libraries, labs, sports facilities, and food courts.

Most students live either in on-campus hostels (operated by the university) or in private accommodations in nearby villages and townships such as Gharuan, Kharar, and Landran. The region is well-connected by local buses, auto-rickshaws, and ride-sharing services. For students without personal vehicles, walking or cycling can be an option only if they live within a comfortable distance from the campus gates.

## Budget Bands for Student Housing

![Chandigarh University](https://img.findstay.org/housing/p/uh-s01591621.jpg)

Housing costs near Chandigarh University vary depending on distance, amenities, and whether meals are included. Prices are indicative — confirm with the operator.

- **On-campus hostels (shared rooms)**: Typically INR 60,000 – 1,00,000 per academic year (includes meals and utilities). These are the most convenient option, located within the campus boundaries.
- **Private PG accommodations (paying guest)**: INR 5,000 – 12,000 per month per person. Basic PG rooms are at the lower end; air-conditioned or single rooms with attached bathrooms cost more. Many include Wi-Fi and electricity charges up to a limit.
- **Shared apartments or flats**: INR 8,000 – 15,000 per month per person for a room in a 2BHK or 3BHK. These are usually in multi-story buildings in Gharuan or Kharar. Security deposits typically equal 1–2 months’ rent.
- **Independent houses or villas (rare)**: INR 15,000 – 25,000 per month for a small house, suitable for groups of 3–4 students sharing.

Note: Prices in 2026 may have shifted due to inflation and demand. Always confirm the total monthly cost including electricity, water, and maintenance with the landlord or PG operator.

## Walk vs Transit: The Daily Commute Trade-Off

**Walking distance** (0–1 km from campus): Very few residential options are within a comfortable walking distance from the academic buildings. The immediate surroundings of the university are mostly undeveloped land or the campus itself. Students who live in on-campus hostels can walk to classes, libraries, and dining halls in 5–10 minutes. If you choose to live off-campus, the closest villages are Gharuan (approx. 1.5–2 km) – walking is possible but not ideal in hot or rainy weather.

**Transit-dependent** (1–5 km): Most off-campus housing is in this range. Students use:

- **Shared auto-rickshaws**: INR 20–50 per trip; frequent service along the main road.
- **Local buses**: Operated by Punjab Roadways and private operators. Bus stops near the university are on NH-05.
- **Private vehicles**: Scooters or bicycles are common among senior students. The campus has parking, but traffic on the highway can be heavy during peak hours.

**Long-distance commuter** (5–15 km): Some students stay in Kharar or Mohali city, where rents are slightly lower and more amenities (shops, restaurants, gyms) are available. Commuting by bus or shared taxi takes 20–40 minutes each way. This option saves on rent but consumes time and energy.

**Trade-off summary**: Proximity to campus means less travel time and more participation in late-night events or library study sessions. However, on-campus housing often requires a semester commitment and may have stricter rules (curfew, guest policies). Off-campus housing gives more independence but adds a commute cost of INR 1,000–3,000 per month.

## Shortlist Logic: How to Choose Housing Based on Nearby Options

When evaluating housing near Chandigarh University, consider these factors in order of priority for most students:

1. **Distance to campus**: Shortlist properties within 2 km of the university main gate. Walking distance under 20 minutes is ideal. If walking is not feasible, ensure a direct auto route is available.
2. **Safety and security**: Look for female-only PG options if applicable. Gated communities with CCTV and night guards are more common in developed colonies like Landran. Ask about nighttime accessibility — many PG owners restrict entry after 10 PM.
3. **Meals provided**: On-campus hostels and some PG accommodations offer three meals. If you cook, a shared kitchen in an apartment reduces monthly food costs to around INR 3,000–5,000 (indicative — confirm with the operator).
4. **Internet and electricity**: Most PGs include Wi-Fi in the rent, but speed may be shared. Check if air-conditioning is metered separately. In hostels, electricity usage beyond a cap may be charged extra.
5. **Group size**: Single rooms cost more per person. Sharing a room or apartment with 2–3 friends brings down the rent to INR 4,000–7,000 per person in a PG or apartment.
6. **Lease flexibility**: On-campus hostels are annual commitments. Off-campus PGs often allow monthly or semester-wise payments, making them easier to leave if needed.
7. **Amenities**: Laundry, gym, and common room are standard in higher-end PGs. If you value these, prioritize properties closer to Kharar township, where newer buildings offer more facilities.

**Example decision flow**:

- If you want to fully immerse in campus life and don’t mind shared bathrooms → choose on-campus hostel.
- If you prefer privacy and a quiet environment → rent a single room in a PG 1–2 km away.
- If you have a vehicle and want lower rent → look in Gharuan village (approx. 1.5 km) for shared flats.
- If you need a 24/7 study space and flexible food options → shortlist apartments in Landran area with a mini-market nearby.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is it better to live on-campus or off-campus for a first-year student?**  
A: Many first-year students prefer on-campus hostels because they are close to classes, libraries, and social events. The university manages meals, security, and basic amenities. Off-campus options can be cheaper but require you to arrange transport and food. If independence is a priority, consider off-campus after the first semester once you are familiar with the area.

**Q: How much does a shared PG room cost near Chandigarh University in 2026?**  
A: Indicative monthly rent for a shared PG room (double or triple sharing) ranges from INR 5,000 to 8,000 per person. This usually includes electricity up to a limit, Wi-Fi, and sometimes breakfast/dinner. Confirm the exact inclusions with the operator, as prices vary by location and season.

**Q: Are there any student housing options with reliable internet for online classes?**  
A: On-campus hostels provide campus-wide Wi-Fi, which is generally fast enough for streaming and assignments. Off-campus PGs in Gharuan and Kharar often include Wi-Fi; ask about the speed and data cap before moving in. For heavier requirements, consider buying a portable dongle or using a local fiber-optic connection (available in most apartment buildings).
