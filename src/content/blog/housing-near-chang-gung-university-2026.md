## Housing Near Chang Gung University: What Campus Reviews Reveal

Chang Gung University (CGU) is located at No. 259, Wenhua 1st Rd, Guishan District, Taoyuan City, Taiwan. With a QS ranking of 668, it is a medium-sized institution closely linked to the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital system. The university holds a Google rating of 4.2 from 879 reviews, but the written feedback tells a more nuanced story that can influence housing decisions.

### What Student Reviews Tell Us About Campus Life

These reviews highlight two recurring issues: steep campus staircases and inconsistent faculty communication. The “250 plus steps” complaint suggests that the campus sits on a slope or multi-level terrain. If you plan to live off-campus, proximity to entrances without heavy elevation change may matter. Meanwhile, the negative experiences with teaching attitudes may push some students to seek housing that reduces time spent on campus (e.g., a home with a good study space), though this is a personal preference.

On the positive side, a summer pre-med student (Kfroi A, rating 5, 11 months ago) praised the program depth while suggesting lectures could be more engaging. This indicates that despite some friction, the academic rigor is appreciated.

### City Context: Taoyuan’s Guishan District

CGU sits in Guishan District, a suburban area in Taoyuan City, about 30 minutes by car or scooter from Taoyuan’s main railway station. The district is less dense than central Taipei, with a mix of residential blocks, small shops, and industrial-zoned areas. The university itself is near the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital complex, which is a major employer and transport hub.

Public transport options include bus routes connecting to Taoyuan HSR (high-speed rail) station, Taoyuan Airport MRT (A7 Sport University Station is about 2 km away), and local buses. Many students use scooters or bicycles for short trips. Walking distance to campus is limited, as the immediate surroundings are mostly hospital buildings and a few convenience stores.

  Shared rooms in older apartment buildings within a 20–30 minute walk or short bus ride. Often includes basic furniture, shared bathroom, and utility costs split. Some near-campus “student villages” (min-su) offer bunk-bed rooms in this range.
- **Mid-range: 8,000–12,000 NTD**  
  Private studio or one-bedroom units in purpose-built student housing or newer apartment complexes within 1–2 km of campus. These usually have a private bathroom, small kitchenette, and air conditioning. Many are located along Wenhua 1st Rd or on streets off Jianguo Rd.
- **Premium: 13,000–20,000 NTD**  
  Larger apartments or serviced suites near the Taoyuan Airport MRT stops (e.g., A7 or A8). These offer more space, gyms, and faster access to Taipei. Commute to CGU is 15–20 minutes by scooter or bus. Some foreign graduate students choose this option.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

**Walking**  
Residences within a 15-minute walk of the main gate (around the corner of Wenhua 1st Rd and Fuxing Rd) are scarce. Most available units are inside the hospital staff residential blocks or older low-rise buildings. A few private boarding houses exist on the slopes behind the sports complex. Walking is feasible if you do not mind the hilly terrain and limited late-night street lighting. For students who rely on walking, checking the exact elevation change between the unit and the classroom building is wise — the stairs mentioned in reviews may extend beyond campus boundaries.

**Transit**  
The majority of student housing lies along bus routes 601, 603, and 606, which run from Guishan to Taoyuan Station. The Taoyuan Airport MRT (line) has stops at A7 (Sport University) and A8 (Chang Gung Hospital). A7 is about a 10-minute bus ride from CGU; A8 is closer (a 5-minute bus or 15-minute walk). Housing near these MRT stations tends to be newer and more expensive but offers a direct line to Taipei Main Station (35–40 minutes). For students who frequently travel to Taipei for internships or leisure, transit-oriented housing may save time overall, even if the commute to campus takes 20–25 minutes.

### Shortlist Logic: How to Choose Among Available Residences

Because the data does not provide a specific list of nearby residences, the following selection criteria can help you evaluate any option you find through local platforms or referrals.

![Chang Gung University](https://img.findstay.org/housing/p/uh-s01524825.jpg)

1. **Proximity to a flat entrance**  
   Given the stair reports, a residence with level ground or ramp access to the building and a short walk to campus may improve daily comfort. Avoid units that require climbing additional steps after a day of stairs.
2. **Distance to bus stop or MRT**  
   Reliable public transport reduces dependence on scooters, which can be costly and require a local license. Prioritize stops within 300 metres if you plan to commute.
3. **Utility and contract terms**  
   Many rentals in Taiwan charge electricity per kilowatt-hour (often above market rate in student housing). Ask for a breakdown of water, electricity, internet, and management fees. Aim for leases that are 6–12 months to match the academic calendar.
4. **Noise and study environment**  
   The review about a teacher shouting suggests some interactions can be intense. A quiet home environment may help buffer stress. Ground-floor units facing main roads can be noisy; top floors may have better air quality but less lift access.
5. **Security and maintenance**  
   Look for buildings with 24-hour security (common in newer complexes) or a resident manager. Older houses may have flimsy doors or shared entrances; check for functioning locks and fire alarms.
6. **Roommate compatibility**  
   For shared flats, request a trial period or a video tour. Cultural and language differences can affect study habits. Some housing platforms allow you to filter by language preference.

### Final Housing Strategy for CGU Students

- **First-year students**: Consider applying for on-campus dormitories (if available) to reduce the search burden and get oriented. Dorm fees are typically 3,000–6,000 NTD per semester, much cheaper than off-market options. However, availability may be limited for international students; check the university housing office directly.
- **Returning students**: Move off-campus after learning the local area. Many find flats through LINE groups or notices on campus bulletin boards. Start searching 1–2 months before the semester begins.
- **International students**: Budget for a scooter rental (~2,000–3,000 NTD per month) or an MRT pass (~1,280 NTD per month for Taoyuan routes). English-speaking landlords are uncommon; bring a Chinese-speaking friend or use a bilingual agent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Does Chang Gung University offer on-campus housing for international students?

Yes, CGU provides dormitories primarily for undergraduate and graduate students. Eligibility for international students depends on the quota and academic level. Dorm rooms are usually shared (2–4 persons) with communal bathroom facilities. The cost is roughly 3,000–6,000 NTD per semester, which is significantly less than private off-campus options. Contact the university’s Office of International Affairs for the latest application process for the 2026–2027 academic year.

### 2. What is the average monthly rent for a private apartment near CGU?

For a single-person studio within a 20-minute commute, the indicative range is 8,000–12,000 NTD (confirm with the operator). Shared rooms cost 4,000–7,000 NTD. Premium units near the MRT stations (A7/A8) range from 13,000–20,000 NTD. These prices are for 2026–2027 and may vary by season and negotiation.

### 3. How do I commute from the Taoyuan Airport MRT to campus?

Take the MRT to **A8 Chang Gung Hospital Station**. From the station exit, you can walk (15 minutes) or transfer to bus routes 601, 603, 606, or the free hospital shuttle. The bus ride to CGU’s main gate takes about 5 minutes. Alternatively, **A7 Sport University Station** is slightly farther — take a 10-minute bus (route 606) or a 5-minute scooter ride. MRT frequency is every 6–8 minutes during peak hours.
