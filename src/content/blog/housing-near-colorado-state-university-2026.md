## Housing Near Colorado State University: What to Know Before You Arrive (2026–2027)

Colorado State University (CSU) sits in Fort Collins, a mid-sized city at the base of the Rocky Mountains. With a Google rating of 4.2 from 445 reviews, the campus gets consistent praise for its setting and academic rigor, but some students also note concerns about cost and administrative processes. If you are planning to move to Fort Collins for the 2026–2027 academic year, understanding the local housing landscape will help you make a practical decision.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Current and former students often highlight the campus’s natural surroundings and the quality of education. One five-star reviewer, Dariusz Prokopowicz, described CSU as “majestically sprawling beneath the watchful gaze of the Rocky Mountains” and called it “an absolute paragon of modern research and sustainable innovation.” Aaron Decker, another five-star reviewer, noted: “Colorado State University in Fort Collins, Colorado is a beautiful campus. The Rocky mountains have the backdrop and Northern Colorado weather, getting any better? Great football stadium.”

Jeremy Janus, a 2011 graduate in Food Science, gave a five-star review focusing on the life-changing impact of his education: “The education in the classroom and in life that I received while I attended university was life changing. I met some of the best people I have had in my life and I pursued my love for nutrition and food science while I was there.”

Not all experiences are positive. A one-star reviewer, Aliyah Mottram, wrote in 2025: “Honestly with the inflated cost of college now days I expect the admission process to be smooth. I submitted my application they added a school that I never attended to it, when I reached out to my admissions counselor she told me ‘unfortunately you added that school when you applied’.” This feedback points to potential friction points with administrative systems, which can also affect housing-related paperwork such as lease verification or roommate matching.

Overall, the reviews suggest that CSU attracts students who value the outdoors, academic community, and a campus that feels connected to nature. The main drawbacks are rising costs and occasional bureaucracy.

### City Context: Fort Collins

Fort Collins is home to roughly 170,000 residents and sits about 65 miles north of Denver. The city is known for its bike-friendly streets, craft breweries, and access to outdoor recreation. Average temperatures range from 15°F in January to 85°F in July, with 300 days of sunshine per year.

The local economy is anchored by CSU, agriculture, technology, and tourism. For students, the cost of living in Fort Collins is moderate compared to nearby Boulder or Denver. Housing near campus tends to be slightly more expensive, but options become more affordable if you are willing to commute by bus or bike.

### Budget Bands (Indicative Prices for 2026–2027)

Housing costs in Fort Collins vary by distance from campus, unit type, and amenities. Based on listings available for the 2026–2027 academic year, you can expect the following indicative ranges (all prices are indicative — confirm with the operator):

- **Shared rooms / budget-friendly rooms in multi-bedroom apartments:** from around $650 to $800 per month per person
- **Studio or one-bedroom apartments within 1 mile of campus:** $950 to $1,300 per month
- **Two- or three-bedroom apartments (split among roommates):** $1,200 to $1,800 total per month ($600–$900 per person)
- **Premium housing (furnished, near campus, with gym/study lounges):** $800 to $1,100 per person for a shared unit

The sample listing price floor is $659 per month, which generally reflects a room in a shared apartment close to campus. Always check what is included (utilities, internet, parking) because these can add $100–$200 per month.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

Walking to CSU’s main campus (the Oval and the Lory Student Center) takes roughly 10–20 minutes from most housing options within a half-mile radius. Properties such as **State**, **The District At Campus West**, and **Uncommon Ft. Collins** are within a comfortable walking distance — about 5 to 15 minutes on foot. Walking saves time and transit cost, but rents in this zone are typically higher.

Going further out, rent drops. For example, **The Outpost** and **Yugo Fort Collins Grove** are about 1–2 miles from campus, making them more affordable but requiring a bus or bike ride. Fort Collins’ public transit system, Transfort, offers several routes that serve the university. A semester bus pass is included with CSU tuition, so riding the bus is free for students. Buses run about every 15–30 minutes during weekdays. Cycling is also very popular — the city has over 300 miles of bike lanes and trails, and many rentals come with bike storage.

If you plan to bike, aim for properties within 3 miles of campus; the ride takes about 15 minutes. If you rely on the bus, check that the stop is within a 5-minute walk from your apartment.

### Shortlist Logic: How to Choose Among Nearby Residences

The sample properties available for the 2026–2027 academic year offer a range of price points and distances. Here is a factual breakdown of what each option generally provides (detailed amenities and exact pricing must be confirmed directly with the operator):

- **The Outpost** – Located slightly west of campus, often a 15–20 minute walk or a short bus ride. Units are typically furnished with in-unit laundry. Usually aimed at upperclassmen and graduate students. Indicative price range: low $700s per person for shared apartments.
- **State** – A newer development very close to the university, sometimes right at the edge of campus. Studios and multi-bedroom floor plans. Usually includes a fitness center and study spaces. Expect higher rent, from $850 per person upwards.
- **The District At Campus West** – Situated in the Campus West neighborhood, a 5–10 minute walk to central campus. Known for larger common areas and proximity to restaurants and bars. Shared apartments often start around $700 per person (indicative).
- **Yugo Fort Collins Grove** – A purpose-built student accommodation about 1.5 miles west of campus. Offers private rooms and shared apartments with all-inclusive rent. Bicycle storage and shuttle service may be available. Indicative pricing: $750–$950 per person per month.
- **Uncommon Ft. Collins** – Located near the intersection of Prospect Road and Shields Street, roughly a 10–15 minute walk from the engineering buildings. Features resort-style amenities (pool, gym, gaming lounge). Rents can be higher, often $900+ per person.

**Shortlisting logic**: Start by determining your walking-distance tolerance. If you want to roll out of bed and be in class in 10 minutes, focus on **State**, **The District At Campus West**, or **Uncommon Ft. Collins**. If you are okay with a 15–20 minute walk or a short bus ride, **The Outpost** and **Yugo Fort Collins Grove** offer better value. If you plan to bike, any of these are viable, but check for bike storage. Also consider whether you want a quieter environment (often further from campus) or a social hub (closer to campus).

Always verify the lease term (typically 12 months starting in August or September) and whether summer storage or subletting is allowed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How far from campus should I live if I don’t want a car?**  
If you do not own a car, living within 1 mile of campus is ideal — you can walk or bike in under 15 minutes. Properties like State, The District At Campus West, and Uncommon Ft. Collins fall into this zone. For those living 1–2 miles away, the bus system works well; the Transfort route 31 covers many student housing areas. A bike can also handle that distance easily.

**What’s the typical rent for a room near CSU in 2026–2027?**  
Indicative rents for a room in a shared apartment within walking distance range from $650 to $950 per month. Studios start at about $950. These figures are indicative — confirm with the operator. Utilities and internet are sometimes included, sometimes extra; always ask.

**Are there safety concerns I should consider when picking housing?**  
Fort Collins is generally safe, with a property crime rate slightly above the national average but violent crime low. Most student housing near campus is well-lit and patrolled. Checking the crime map on the CSU Police website or asking the property manager about security features (key fob entry, cameras) is recommended. Some complexes offer courtesy patrol after dark.
