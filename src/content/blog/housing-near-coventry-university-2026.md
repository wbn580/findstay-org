### City context: Coventry as a student city

Coventry is a mid-sized city in the West Midlands, about 30 minutes by train from Birmingham. It has a large student population thanks to Coventry University and other institutions. The city centre is compact, with shops, restaurants, and entertainment within walking distance of the university campus. Public transport includes buses and a train station (Coventry Station) about 15 minutes walk from Priory St.

According to the 2026 QS World University Rankings, Coventry University is ranked 558 globally. The city itself offers a relatively low cost of living compared to London or Oxford, which influences accommodation prices. International students make up a significant share of the student body, and many purpose-built student residences (PBSAs) cater specifically to that demographic.

### Budget bands and indicative pricing

Indicative weekly rents for student housing near Coventry University start from **£43 per week** – confirm with the operator. This price typically refers to a room in a shared house or a basic hall room. Most PBSA options (studio or en-suite rooms) range from about **£90 to £180 per week**, again indicative and subject to change.

- **Low band (under £80/week):** Shared houses or older halls, often a 15–25 minute walk from campus.
- **Mid band (£80–£130/week):** En-suite rooms in newer PBSAs, often located within 10–15 minutes walk.
- **Upper band (£130+/week):** Studios or premium accommodation, sometimes with on-site gyms, common rooms, and shorter commutes.

Prices vary by contract length, room type, and included utilities (bills usually included in PBSA). Always confirm the final cost and what is covered before signing.
