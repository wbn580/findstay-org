## Housing Near De La Salle University: A Student’s Guide

De La Salle University (DLSU) sits at 2401 Taft Ave in Malate, Manila, a lively district in the Philippine capital. With a QS world ranking of 654 and a Google rating of 4.6 from 738 reviews, the university attracts both local and international students. Finding suitable housing nearby requires understanding what the campus and neighbourhood offer. This guide draws on genuine student reviews, the local urban context, and practical trade-offs between walking and transit to help you build a shortlist.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Reviews of DLSU paint a picture of a well-maintained, historic campus with modern amenities. They also hint at what matters for nearby living.

One reviewer, Mayang P. Ayuningtyas, wrote: “I visited the campus during a business trip. The university is expansive, featuring magnificent old European-style buildings and beautiful gardens. The modern library building, with its multiple floors, is equally impressive. It was a wonderful experience to be there.” This suggests that the campus itself is pleasant to be around — you might want housing close enough to enjoy the grounds and library outside class hours.

Another review, by Manny Yu, captured the school spirit: “01/15/26. Animo La Salle🏹 ‘Animo La Salle’ is the battle cry for De La Salle University (DLSU) meaning ‘La Sallian Spirit to Fight,’ or ‘Have Courage,’ invoking passion, resilience, and commitment to excellence in academics, sports, and life, rooted in the Brothers’ call for ‘Faith and Zeal’.” While the focus is on culture, it signals a tight-knit community — living nearby helps you stay connected to campus events and student life.

Pomperumini described the architecture: “Although I haven’t went around much because I was assigned at LS for DCAT 2026, and watching Yuch from the 3rd floor inner balcony made me mesmerized at the sheer scale of the building and I felt like I was gazing at the Supreme Court even if the real one doesn’t look as flashy as Yuch.” The building referred to is Yuchengco Hall, a landmark. If impressive buildings matter to you, accommodation with a view or within a short stroll might be worth prioritising.

Katherina Carla gave a practical rundown: “Really cool places Clean bathrooms Lots of libraries, chairs and tables. They also have their own cafeterias and a church. Nearby there are a lot of restaurants to eat. They also have condominiums nearby. Everything was well maintained and spotless. And also they have a LOT OF CATS… but don’t touc”. This review directly mentions that condominiums exist nearby, along with restaurants and campus facilities. It confirms that you can find housing within walking distance of dining, study spaces, and basic services.

Overall, reviews highlight a campus that is clean, historic, and well-served by surrounding housing and eateries. Your home search should factor in proximity to the campus core, access to libraries and cafeterias, and the presence of outdoor space (or cats).

### City Context: Malate and Manila

Malate is a central district in Manila, known for its mix of residential, commercial, and entertainment establishments. DLSU’s exact address is 2401 Taft Ave, a major north-south thoroughfare that runs along the western edge of the city. Taft Avenue is lined with restaurants, shops, banks, and convenience stores — essentials for daily life.

The area is dense and walkable, especially around the university. You will find numerous apartment buildings, condominium towers, and dormitories within a few blocks. Because Malate is part of Metro Manila, public transport is abundant: jeepneys, buses, and tricycles operate on Taft Avenue, and the LRT-1 line has stations near the university (Vito Cruz and Gil Puyat stations are within a 5-15 minute walk). The nearby Roxas Boulevard offers a coastal route, but housing along it tends to be pricier and less student-oriented.

Manila’s tropical climate means rain and heat are frequent. A residence that is a 10-15 minute walk might feel longer during the wet season, so covered walkways or proximity to covered transport stops can be a practical advantage. Also, given the density, noise levels vary — units facing Taft Avenue are likely louder than those on side streets.

### Budget Bands (Indicative)

No specific rent figures appear in the provided data, but the reviews confirm that condominiums and dormitories exist near campus. As a general reference, monthly rents for a typical studio or a bed in a shared unit in Malate can range from PHP 6,000 to PHP 20,000, depending on room type, furnishings, and distance to DLSU. **This is indicative — confirm with the operator** before committing. Prices change frequently, and the same unit may cost differently in 2026 or 2027. Always ask for a written quotation including any fees for utilities, security, and association dues.

Budget breaks down roughly into:

- **Economy (PHP 6,000 – 10,000/month)**: Shared rooms or basic dormitories, often 15-30 minutes walking from campus. Facilities may include common kitchen and bathroom.
- **Mid-range (PHP 10,000 – 16,000/month)**: Private studios or small units in older condominiums, 10-20 minutes walk. Often include air conditioning, private bathroom, and sometimes a kitchenette.
- **Premium (PHP 16,000 – 25,000+/month)**: Newer condos or serviced apartments within 5-10 minutes walk. May offer gym, pool, 24/7 security, and fully furnished spaces.

Again, these bands are not sourced from the data provided; they serve as a starting point. You must verify current rates directly with property managers or owners.

### Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

Given DLSU’s location on Taft Avenue, you have two main commuting options: walking or public transport.

**Walking** is the most convenient for students living within a 1-2 km radius. A 10-15 minute walk to campus means you avoid traffic delays, save on transport costs, and can easily return home for meals or breaks. Many of the nearby condominiums mentioned by reviewers are likely within a comfortable walking distance. A walkable area also offers better access to the campus library, cafeterias, and church, as noted in reviews. The downside is that during the hot midday sun or heavy rain, a longer walk becomes uncomfortable. Look for units with covered walkways or shaded paths.

![De La Salle University](https://img.findstay.org/housing/p/amber-187832.jpg)

**Public transit** becomes necessary if you choose a cheaper unit farther from Taft Avenue or on a side street beyond walking range. LRT-1 is the fastest option: Vito Cruz and Gil Puyat stations are about 1 km north and south of DLSU, respectively. A jeepney or tricycle ride from your accommodation to a station adds 5-10 minutes and PHP 10-20 per trip. The trade-off is lower rent for a slightly longer, more variable commute. If you plan to stay out late on campus or attend early classes, walking gives you more schedule flexibility.

For most students, a balance is ideal: choose a residence that is walkable (under 20 minutes) to either the university or an LRT station. That way you can walk when the weather is pleasant and take the train when you are pressed for time or carrying heavy books.

### Shortlist Logic: Selecting a Residence

Without a pre-defined list of nearby residences, you can apply a logical filtering process based on the available data and common sense.

1. **Define your walkability threshold** – Decide the maximum time you are willing to walk to your first class. A 10-minute radius covers most of Malate’s condo clusters. Use Google Maps or a mapping app to confirm walking times from specific addresses.
2. **Check amenities mentioned in reviews** – Katherina Carla noted “clean bathrooms”, “lots of libraries, chairs and tables”, “cafeterias and a church”, and “a lot of restaurants”. These are not just campus perks; they indicate a neighbourhood that supports studying and daily needs. Look for housing near restaurants and convenient stores, especially along Taft Avenue or nearby streets like Leon Guinto Street.
3. **Prioritise safety and maintenance** – The same review emphasised that everything was “well maintained and spotless”. When inspecting a unit, check for cleanliness, working air conditioning, secure locks, and building maintenance. Read recent online reviews (beyond this guide) from tenants, though beware of overly positive or negative extremes.
4. **Consider noise and light** – Taft Avenue can be busy, especially near intersections. If you need quiet for studying, choose a unit facing the inner courtyard or a side street. Higher floors can reduce street noise.
5. **Budget realistically** – Because rent is not fixed, set a maximum monthly budget and filter out any unit that exceeds it. Remember to include estimated utilities (PHP 1,500-3,000/month depending on air-con usage) and association dues if separate.
6. **Verify the lease terms** – Short-term leases (month-to-month) are available but may cost more. Six-month or one-year contracts are common. Ask about deposit requirements, notice periods, and whether utilities are included.
7. **Visit during class hours** – If possible, walk from the unit to the university at the time you would normally travel. This gives you a realistic feel for the commute and the crowd.

By following this logic, you can narrow down options without a pre-compiled list. The key is to match your personal priorities (walking vs cost, noise vs space) to the neighbourhood characteristics that reviews and the city context suggest.
