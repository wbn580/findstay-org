With a rating of 4.6 points (579 reviews) on Google Maps, the score reflects students’ general fondness for this Buddhist-affiliated campus. Before choosing your housing, take a look at the real feedback from current students and alumni—these reviews directly reveal which living details are worth considering in your housing search.

From these reviews, three pieces of practically helpful information for house hunting can be extracted:

1. **Location Core**: Dongguk University is located in downtown Seoul. "center of seoul" means convenient transportation and mature living facilities nearby, but it may also face the generally higher rent in the city center.
2. **Cultural Experience**: There is a temple on campus, and the Lotus Lantern Festival on Buddha's Birthday starts here. If you like to immerse yourself in traditional Korean culture, living near the campus allows you to participate in such activities more conveniently.
3. **Environmental Atmosphere**: The sunset is beautiful—this suggests the campus is near Namsan Mountain, and there may be parks or viewpoints within walking distance. When choosing accommodation, orientation and floor views are also worth noting.

## Living Costs and Budget Ranges in Jung-gu, Seoul

As the core area of the capital of Korea, Jung District's housing costs are at an upper-middle level in Seoul. In the year 2026, the common types of accommodation for students around Dongguk University and their approximate price ranges are as follows (all prices are indicative, please confirm with the operator):

- **Gosiwon（Gosiwon）**：Monthly rent 200,000～400,000 KRW. The room is extremely small (usually 3～5 square meters), shared bathroom and kitchen, low deposit (5～100,000 KRW). Suitable for students on an extremely tight budget who only use the room as a place to sleep.
- **One-room studio（One-room）**：Monthly rent 450,000～700,000 KRW. Private bathroom and small kitchen, area approximately 15～25 square meters, deposit 500～10,000,000 KRW (alternative monthly deposit payment negotiable). This is the most common choice for undergraduate students at Dongguk University.
- **Shared housing（Share house）**：Monthly rent 350,000～550,000 KRW. Has a private bedroom, shared living room, kitchen, and bathroom. Deposit is usually 100～3,000,000 KRW. Suitable for students who want social space but do not want to bear the high rent alone.
- **Double room or multi-bedroom apartment**：Monthly rent per person 300,000～500,000 KRW (whole rent about 700,000～1,200,000 KRW). Suitable for shared housing with friends, but need to handle water, electricity, internet, and other bills yourself.

Note: Rent is higher in areas near Myeongdong and Chungmuro in the central district; the steeper the alley toward Namsan, the slightly lower the rent may be (because walking is a bit tiring). Additionally, the deposit system is mainstream in Korean rentals—the higher the deposit, the lower the monthly rent. For example, with a deposit of 10,000,000 won, the monthly rent may drop to 400,000 won, while with a deposit of 2,000,000 won, the monthly rent may rise to 600,000 won. Before moving in, be sure to clarify the deposit refund conditions with the landlord or agent.

## Walking and Transportation Trade-off

![Dongguk University](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-187750.jpg)

The main campus of Dongguk University is built on the northern foot of Namsan, with a certain slope. The main entrances and exits of the campus are concentrated around Dongguk University Station (동국대입구), where subway Line 3 and Line 4 meet (Chungmuro Station is about 10～12 minutes on foot, and Dongguk University Station is about 8～10 minutes on foot). The school also has shuttle buses between the subway stations and the campus, but with limited frequency.

**Advantages and disadvantages of walking distance:**

- **Walk 5～10 minutes (radius approx. 500 meters): mainly includes the Bidong, Jangchungdong areas. Housing prices are usually highest, but you can save on monthly transportation costs (approx. 5～80,000 won), and you can use the campus library and cafeteria until late. The drawbacks are that some alleyways are steep and slippery when rainy.
- **Walk 15～20 minutes (radius 1 km): extends to the Euljiro, Chungmuro, Sindangdong areas. Rent may be 10%～20% cheaper, but going to and from school each day requires an extra 20～30 minutes of walking time, and the experience is less pleasant in cold winter weather.

**The choice to rely on public transportation:**

- From 1 Station to 3 Station (e.g., Dongdaemun, Seoul Station, Euljiro 4 Street): Monthly rent may be 100,000 to 200,000 won lower than the walking area, but monthly transportation costs about 50,000 won (T-money card fee), plus 10 to 15 minutes each way by car. You need to weigh time costs against money savings.
- Bus direct to campus: Seoul has a dense bus network, but near Dongguk University, the bus stop "Dongguk University Entrance" has trunk buses (e.g., 144, 301, 420). If you live in an area not directly reachable by subway but accessible by bus (e.g., Oksu, Hannam-dong), commuting time is about 20 to 30 minutes, and rent may be lower.

**Suggestion**: If you have a dense course schedule and often stay up late, prioritize choosing accommodation within a 15-minute walk; if you have ample free time outside class or prefer to live in a quieter residential area, you can consider along bus or subway lines, but be sure to actually test the morning rush hour commute time (the Seoul subway is very crowded from around 8 to 9 in the morning).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there 24-hour study spaces near Dongguk University?
The campus library usually closes before 10 pm, but there are several 24-hour study cafes (스터디카페) nearby. Monthly fees range from 50,000 to 100,000 KRW. You can find many along Pildong and Chungmuro within a 10-minute walk. If you often study at your accommodation, choose a room with a separate desk to avoid relying on public spaces.

### How to rent a house without a guarantor for international students?
Many Korean landlords require a guarantor. You can choose a real estate agency that provides "guarantee insurance" or "deposit loan" services. Some platforms (such as this platform) offer a filter for listings without a guarantor. Additionally, the university's international office may have recommended partner accommodations, but this guide does not cover specific institutions. It is recommended to prioritize alternative monthly payment deposit options (higher monthly rent, deposit as low as 500,000 Korean Won) such as Gosiwon or Shared housing, as these contracts usually do not require a guarantor.

### Does Buddhist cultural background affect accommodation choices?
There is a temple on Dongguk University campus, but dormitory life is not directly related to religion. Some landlords may be Buddhists, but contract terms will not involve religious beliefs. The only thing to note is that in Gosiwon or Shared housing, there may be meditation spaces or vegetarian requirements. If you care about this, just ask directly when viewing the room. According to reviews, Buddhist elements are more of a bonus for cultural experience rather than an obstacle to life.
