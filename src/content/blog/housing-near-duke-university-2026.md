## Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

Choosing between walking distance and relying on transit has real implications for time, money, and convenience near Duke.

### Walking

Properties within a 15-minute walk of Duke’s main campus (e.g., near East Campus or West Campus) typically command higher rents. However, you save on parking fees and fuel, and you avoid the time spent commuting. The campus itself is pedestrian-friendly, especially around the Chapel and academic buildings. If you can afford the premium, walking is the most reliable option.

### Transit and Biking

Durham’s public bus system, operated by GoDurham, serves many routes near Duke. Buses run regularly but not always late into the night, so check schedules if you have evening classes. Many students also bike – Duke is bike-friendly with racks and paths. Biking can cut commute times from 30 minutes walking to under 10 minutes.

### Driving

Driving to campus means dealing with restricted parking. As the Google reviews show, permit-only lots are common and enforced even on major event days. Daily parking can be expensive, and a campus parking permit for the year is an additional cost. If you live more than 2–3 miles away, driving may be necessary, but it is not the cheapest or most convenient option.

### Trade-Off Summary

- **Within 1 mile:** Walk or bike – highest rent, lowest transport cost.
- **1–3 miles:** Bike or bus – moderate rent and moderate transport cost.
- **Over 3 miles:** Drive or bus – lower rent, higher transport cost, plus parking challenges.
