## Griffith University Student Housing Guide: What to Know Before You Lease

Griffith University’s Nathan campus sits on the edge of Toohey Forest in Brisbane’s southern suburbs. With a QS World University Ranking of 268 and a Google rating of 4.1 from 433 reviews, the university attracts both domestic and international students. The campus address is 170 Kessels Rd, Nathan QLD 4111. While the institution has its strengths, some student reviews point to significant challenges in academic support and communication. These experiences can influence where you choose to live, especially if you value reliable access to campus resources. This guide covers what recent reviews reveal about student life, the city context, budget bands for accommodation, the walk versus transit trade-off, and how to build a shortlist of housing options near Griffith University’s Nathan campus.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Student feedback on Google is mixed. Out of 433 ratings, the average is 4.1, but several recent one-star reviews highlight serious concerns. A third-year BA student, Sylwia Dziuba, wrote: “Where do I start? No communication from tutors, no unit outlines, marking rubrics provided very late or after the due date for the assessment has passed. PURE INSANITY! The tutors don’t even respond to emails. I am a 3rd year BA student and would never again study at this excuse for a university.” Another reviewer, Jaysson Moggalicious, said: “Found the University to be dedicated to marketing their degrees to students but lacked the ability to teach effectively. It’s basically a MLM scheme in my opinion.” Speck Ozborn, who was in the Pharmacy research program, described “the supervisory environment lacking in fairness, transparency, and adequate student support during conflicts and disputes.”

On the positive side, a five-star review from M. Fadel Pahleva Jacoeb simply stated: “My campus! Shining from the center of Toohey Forrest.”

These reviews suggest that students who rely heavily on face-to-face interaction with tutors and clear assignment guidance may face frustrations. If you are considering studying at Griffith, your housing choice can affect how easily you can attend support sessions, meet with tutors, or access libraries and study spaces during the day. Living closer to campus may give you more flexibility to navigate any communication gaps by being physically present.

### City Context: Brisbane South and Nathan

Nathan is a suburb approximately 10 kilometres south of Brisbane’s central business district. The campus is surrounded by bushland, and public transport links include buses from the city and surrounding suburbs. The area is primarily residential, with a mix of standalone houses and low-rise apartment buildings. Nearby suburbs used by students include Mount Gravatt, Upper Mount Gravatt, Sunnybank, and Holland Park. Brisbane’s climate is subtropical: mild winters and warm, humid summers. You will need to factor in air-conditioning costs if your rental does not include it.

The Greater Brisbane rental market has seen steady demand from students and young professionals. As of 2026, typical rents for a room in a shared house near Griffith Nathan range from approximately AUD 180 to AUD 300 per week, depending on condition and distance. Studio apartments start around AUD 320 per week. These figures are indicative — confirm with the operator or property manager at the time of your search.

### Budget Bands

Your weekly housing budget will determine the type of accommodation and how far from campus you can afford to live. Assume a 40-week academic year (two semesters) if you need a contract aligned with the university calendar; some leases require 52-week commitments.

- **Low budget: under AUD 200 per week**. This usually means a room in a shared house (often with 3–5 housemates) in suburbs like Mount Gravatt East or Robertson. Expect older houses with shared facilities. You will probably need a bus to campus.
- **Mid budget: AUD 200–300 per week**. Shared apartments in newer complexes, or a room in a townhouse closer to Nathan (within 2–3 km). Some options include a private bathroom. Walking or cycling may be feasible.
- **High budget: above AUD 300 per week**. Studio or one-bedroom apartments near the campus gates, or premium shared housing with en-suite and air conditioning. Easy walk to university.

All prices are indicative — confirm with the operator before signing any agreement.

### Walk versus Transit Trade-off

Living within walking distance of Griffith Nathan (say, under 1.5 km) reduces your reliance on public transport. The campus is not a major transit hub like the city centre; buses run along Kessels Road and Logan Road, but frequencies drop outside peak hours. A direct bus from the city to the Nathan campus takes roughly 30–40 minutes. If you choose to live in Sunnybank or Upper Mount Gravatt, you may need one bus or a combination of bus and walking.

Walking to campus has clear advantages: you can attend early morning or late evening classes, access the library and labs outside standard schedules, and avoid bus cancellations. However, Nathan is a hilly area — check the topography of your shortlisted address before committing. A 20-minute walk uphill in summer heat is not pleasant.

If you rely on transit, look for streets near high-frequency bus routes like the 120, 125, or 135. Apartments close to bus stops on Kessels Road or Mains Road give you a decent connection. The trade-off is that you may pay AUD 10–20 per week for a bus pass (concession fares apply). Also, the last bus from campus can be as early as 9:30 pm on some lines, so consider your evening schedule.

For cyclists, dedicated bike paths are limited but present on parts of the campus perimeter. A bicycle can reduce commute time for distances up to 5 km, but secure bike parking is limited.

### Shortlist Logic Using Nearby Residences

![Griffith University](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-3259873.jpg)

When you search for housing near Griffith Nathan, apply a systematic shortlist logic rather than randomly browsing. Use the following criteria with your budget band in mind:

1. **Distance to campus** – Prioritise properties within 2 km radius if you plan to walk or cycle. Use Google Maps to check walking time from the property to the nearest campus gate (closest to your faculty building). For example, the main entrance on Kessels Road serves the majority of lecture theatres.
2. **Transport access** – If walking is not an option, list properties within 400 metres of a bus stop serviced by routes that stop at the Nathan campus interchange. Avoid spots that require a transfer unless you have no choice.
3. **Lease length** – Many student-focused residences offer 40-week contracts. Private rentals typically ask for 6 or 12 months. Align the lease with your academic calendar to avoid paying for empty months.
4. **Utilities included** – In shared accommodation, bills can add AUD 30–50 per week. Some rooms advertise “bills included” — verify the cap on usage during summer when air conditioning runs frequently.
5. **Room configuration** – If you need a quiet place for remote study sessions (especially given the review complaints about tutor communication), avoid rooms adjacent to living areas in small apartments. A room in a larger house with a study desk and good Wi-Fi may be better.
6. **Security and quietness** – Nathan is generally safe, but check street lighting and feedback from previous tenants. Use the university’s student accommodation noticeboard or independent housing platforms (without naming them) to read reviews.
7. **Shortlist size** – Aim for 5–8 options. Visit each in person or request a video tour during peak daylight hours. Pay attention to the condition of shared kitchens and bathrooms, as these are common friction points in shared living.
8. **Price transparency** – Ask upfront for the total weekly cost, including any admin fees, bond (usually 4 weeks’ rent), and whether the bond is lodged with the Residential Tenancies Authority. Do not commit to a place that refuses to provide a written tenancy agreement.

After shortlisting, check whether any residences are university-owned or affiliated — these sometimes offer a more structured support environment, though they can be pricier. The Google reviews you saw for Griffith itself are unrelated to housing, but the academic frustrations reported may motivate you to choose a home that gives you the best possible commute and study environment.

### FAQ

**1. Is it cheaper to live in Nathan or commute from the Brisbane CBD?**  
Living near campus in Nathan tends to be cheaper than a city apartment, but rents vary widely. A room in a shared house in Nathan or Mount Gravatt is typically AUD 180–250 per week, while a studio in the city starts around AUD 400 per week. However, commuting from the city adds AUD 30–40 per week for public transport and takes at least an hour round trip. If your budget is tight, staying close to campus saves both money and time. **2. What should I look for in a lease near Griffith Nathan?**  
Check the lease length (40 weeks vs 52 weeks), whether utilities are included, and the bond amount. Inspect the property for mould, insect screens, and the condition of air conditioning units — Brisbane summers are hot. Also confirm internet speed and availability; some older houses have limited NBN coverage. Avoid signing a lease without seeing the exact room or unit.

**3. How do I find student housing without visiting in person?**  
Request a live video tour focusing on the bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom. Ask for a sample tenancy agreement. Use digital street view to check the neighbourhood and bus stop proximity. Only transfer a holding deposit after you have a signed agreement that clearly states the property address and your rights under Queensland tenancy law. Be cautious of listings that demand payment before a viewing.
