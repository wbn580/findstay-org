## City Context: Bengaluru’s Rental Landscape

IISc is located in Bengaluru (formerly Bangalore), Karnataka. The campus sits in the northern part of the city, bordered by neighbourhoods such as Malleswaram, Basavanagudi, and Sadashivanagar. Bengaluru is India’s IT hub, with a high cost of living relative to other Indian cities. Traffic congestion is a well-known challenge, especially during peak hours and on major arteries like Nandi Durga Road (which runs alongside the campus) and the nearby Outer Ring Road.

Rental prices in areas immediately surrounding IISc reflect a mix of professional demand (many tech workers rent in Malleswaram) and student demand. The presence of other academic institutions in the vicinity (e.g., University of Agricultural Sciences) also influences supply. Because Bengaluru’s real estate market is dynamic, rental rates can vary significantly within a 2–3 km radius. Most student housing options are either individual flats, shared apartments, or paying guest (PG) accommodations. PG accommodations typically include meals and utilities, while independent flats require more self-management.

## Budget Bands for Student Housing

Based on typical Bengaluru rental patterns near IISc, the following are indicative budget bands (all prices are indicative — confirm with the operator):

- **Budget (₹5,000 – ₹10,000 per month)**: This range usually covers a bed in a shared PG or a room in a shared flat in slightly older buildings or areas farther from the main gate, such as deeper parts of Malleswaram or Yeshwanthpur. These options may lack amenities like air-conditioning or high-speed internet, and the commute could be 20–30 minutes by bus or auto-rickshaw. Electricity and water bills are often included in PG rents, but verify this.
- **Mid-range (₹10,000 – ₹20,000 per month)**: A single room in a shared flat or a better PG with attached bathroom, possibly in a newer building within walking distance (1–2 km). Many students choose this band for a good balance of cost and convenience. Wi-Fi and basic furniture are usually included. Some PGs also provide breakfast and dinner.
- **Upper range (₹20,000 – ₹35,000 per month)**: Private studio or 1-bedroom apartment in locations like Sadashivanagar or near the IISc campus gate. These units are often fully furnished, with dedicated parking, and may be in gated societies. Ideal for couples or researchers who need space and quiet. Rent can go higher if you seek premium amenities (gym, pool) or a very short walk to the campus.
- **Premium (₹35,000+ per month)**: Luxury apartments or villas in prime locations like Palace Road or High Grounds. Suitable for visiting faculty or long-term professionals who prioritise comfort and are not constrained by a student budget.

These are rough guides. Actual prices depend on the condition, exact location (how close to the campus entrance), and the current market. Always negotiate and read the contract carefully.

## Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

IISc’s main entrance is on Nandi Durga Road, part of the busy Bellary Road. The campus is large — approximately 400 acres — so internal transport matters if you live far from your department. However, most academic buildings are within a 10–15 minute walk from the gate.

**Walking distance**: Neighbourhoods within 1–1.5 km of the campus gates include parts of Malleswaram (near 6th Cross, 8th Cross) and the areas around the Indian Institute of Science bus stop. Walking is reliable, zero-cost, and allows you to avoid traffic. It also gives you flexibility for late-night lab work. The main trade-off is that rents in the immediate vicinity tend to be higher, and availability may be lower because many students and professionals compete for the same properties.

**Transit options**: If you look slightly farther (2–4 km), rents can drop by 15–30%. The Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) runs buses along Nandi Durga Road and the nearby Outer Ring Road. The nearest metro station is Yeshwanthpur (about 3 km away on the Green Line), but that requires a bus or auto-rickshaw to reach the campus. Renting near a major bus stop (e.g., IISc, Mekhri Circle, or Yeshwanthpur Circle) can keep your commute under 20 minutes by bus. Auto-rickshaws and ride-hailing services are widely available but can add ₹100–₹200 per trip.

**Trade-off summary**:

- Walk: higher rent, lower commute time, no transport cost.
- Transit: lower rent, longer commute (10–25 minutes one-way), variable transport cost, risk of delays in peak traffic.

For students who regularly work late or attend early morning labs, walkable accommodation is strongly advisable. For those who prefer a quieter neighbourhood or lower cost, a short bus ride is manageable.

## How to Shortlist Nearby Residences (General Logic)

Since specific residences are not provided in this dataset, the following logic is based on common student-finding methods around IISc. You can apply these steps when you search:

1. **Define your commute tolerance**: Decide the maximum acceptable travel time (e.g., 15 minutes walking or 30 minutes by bus). Use mapping apps to identify areas within that radius.
2. **Use campus resources**: IISc’s student affairs office or department notice boards sometimes list available accommodations. Talk to current students – they often know which streets are safe and affordable.
3. **Prioritise safety and basic amenities**: Check for 24-hour water supply, reliable electricity, and internet connectivity. In Bengaluru, beware of areas with water scarcity (tankers may be expensive). Malleswaram and Sadashivanagar are relatively well-served.
4. **Evaluate the building type**: PGs usually offer all-inclusive rent but have rules (curfew, guests policy). Flats give more freedom but require deposits and utility payments. Decide based on your lifestyle.
5. **Check public transport links**: If walking isn’t an option, ensure a direct bus line to IISc. The best routes are those running on Nandi Durga Road (e.g., buses from Majestic, Yeshwanthpur, or Hebbal). Avoid locations that require two bus changes.
6. **Visit the area at different times**: Bengaluru traffic varies wildly. A 10-minute auto ride at 9 AM might become 40 minutes at 6 PM. Also check street lighting and crowd after dark – especially important for female students.
7. **Read the rental agreement carefully**: Verify notice period, deposit refund terms, and any hidden charges. Prefer owners who allow a month-to-month or semester lease to avoid long-term commitment.
8. **Budget for deposits**: Most Landlords ask for 2–10 months’ rent as deposit (refundable). This can be a large upfront cost. Some PGs ask for one month’s advance. Factor this into your savings.

If you are looking for ready-to-move options, search for “PG near Indian Institute of Science Bangalore” or “flats for rent in Malleswaram” on property portals. Cross-check with Google Maps to measure walking distance from the campus gate. Also, consider the nearby localities like Yeshwanthpur, Basaveshwaranagar (slightly farther but cheaper), and Sadashivanagar (upmarket, short walk).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Can I find accommodation inside the IISc campus?**  
IISc provides limited on-campus hostel accommodation for its own students (postgraduate and research scholars). Availability for others (short-term visitors, exchange students) is generally not guaranteed. Most students live off-campus in the surrounding neighbourhoods. If you are not formally admitted as a regular student, you will almost certainly need to find private housing.

**2. What is the average rent for a single-bedroom apartment near IISc?**  
Indicative rent for a unfurnished 1BHK (one bedroom, hall, kitchen) in Malleswaram or Sadashivanagar ranges from ₹12,000 to ₹25,000 per month, depending on location, floor, and condition. A furnished option can cost ₹20,000–₹35,000 per month. These prices are indicative — confirm with the operator or landlord before committing. Always compare at least three properties.

**3. How do I verify the landlord’s credentials and avoid fraud?**  
Request a copy of the property tax receipt or a utility bill to confirm ownership. Ask for a formal rent agreement on stamp paper. Avoid paying a large advance without visiting the property. In Bengaluru, online groups (e.g., Facebook communities for Bangalore flats) can be useful, but be cautious about deals that seem too good. Ideally, ask a current IISc student or staff to help verify the address.
