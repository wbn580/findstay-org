## Housing Near IIT Kharagpur: What Students Should Know

Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IITKGP) sits on a sprawling, green campus in the town of Kharagpur, West Bengal. Founded in 1951 as the first IIT, it consistently ranks among India’s top engineering institutions (QS World University Rankings 2025: 215). The campus itself draws strong reactions: one reviewer calls it “vast, green, and filled with an inspiring academic atmosphere,” while another remarks that “the campus perfectly reflects the rich heritage and academic prestige of the institute.” With nearly 5,000 Google reviews and a 4.6 rating, the academic environment clearly impresses—but housing decisions matter just as much for daily life. This guide lays out what campus reviews reveal, the town context, typical budget ranges, walk versus transit trade-offs, and a logical approach to choosing accommodation near IIT Kharagpur. All prices are indicative — confirm with the operator.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Reading recent Google reviews gives a balanced picture. One visitor, **DAFFODIL SALES CORPORATION**, gave five stars and wrote: “I was amazed by its vast greenery, peaceful atmosphere, and well-maintained infrastructure.” Another, **MRUTYUNJAY BALLURGI**, echoed the same: “The campus is vast, green, and filled with an inspiring academic atmosphere.” These comments suggest the surroundings are pleasant, which is a plus for anyone choosing off-campus housing—the neighbourhood around the institute also tends to be relatively green and quiet.

However, **Pankaj Ladwal**, also a five-star reviewer, raised a specific concern: “They need maintenance Hostels. Hostels are in very bad condition. If you are going to Btech and UG program. You have to come along with your own bedding. They don’t provide mat…” This points to a real issue: on-campus hostels, while historically important, may lack modern upkeep. For many students, that makes off-campus housing a practical alternative, especially if you prefer a cleaner, self-managed living space. The fourth review, by **JAYANTA SAHA**, confirms the institute’s prestige but doesn’t address accommodation directly.

Bottom line: the campus environment is highly rated, but hostel conditions can be a problem. Factoring that into your housing decision is key.

### City Context: Kharagpur

Kharagpur is a small city in West Bengal, about 120 km west of Kolkata. Its economy is largely driven by IIT Kharagpur and the nearby railway junction (one of India’s busiest). The town itself is not a major metro, so living costs are generally lower than in Bengaluru, Mumbai, or Delhi. Local amenities include markets, banks, medical clinics, and basic restaurants concentrated in areas like “Old Kharagpur” and near the railway station. The climate is tropical: hot and humid from March to June, heavy monsoon from July to September, and mild winters. For students, this means you may prefer a room with good ventilation or air conditioning (though AC units increase rent).

Transport within Kharagpur relies on auto-rickshaws, cycle-rickshaws, and buses. Ride-hailing apps such as Ola and Uber have limited coverage, so most local trips are arranged on the street. The IIT campus is large—over 2,100 acres—so moving around inside often requires a bicycle or campus shuttle.

### Budget Bands (Indicative — Confirm with the Operator)

Housing costs near IIT Kharagpur are modest compared to metro cities. Below are rough monthly rent ranges for student accommodation in 2026–2027. All prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and are indicative only.

- **Budget (low cost): INR 4,000 – 8,000 per month**  
  Typically a shared room in a paying guest (PG) accommodation or a basic independent room without AC. Shared bathrooms and a common cooking area are common. These options are often located within 1–2 km of the campus gate, in areas like “IIT Kharagpur Road” or “Near New Hall.”
- **Mid-range: INR 8,000 – 15,000 per month**  
  A single room in a PG with attached bathroom, sometimes with AC or a fan, and basic furnishings. Some may include meals (breakfast and dinner). Proximity can be 2–3 km or inside the campus residential quarters (if allocated). This range also covers small one-bedroom apartments in buildings outside the main campus.
- **Premium: INR 15,000 – 25,000 per month**  
  Fully furnished one-bedroom or studio apartments with AC, Wi-Fi, a private kitchen, and possibly a living room. Often located in quieter, well-maintained gated complexes or near the main entrance. Some students share a larger apartment to split costs—per person cost can then fall into the mid-range.

Remember to factor in deposits (usually 2–3 months’ rent) and utility charges (electricity, water, internet). Negotiation is possible, especially for longer leases.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

IIT Kharagpur’s campus is walkable only if you live inside the campus or right at the gate. On-campus hostels and faculty quarters are within walking distance to most academic buildings and dining halls. However, as the review noted, hostel maintenance can be poor, and on-campus accommodation is limited and allocated through a central process (usually for undergraduate students). Graduate students and research scholars often have more flexibility but may still compete for space.

If you live off campus, you face a trade-off:

- **Within walking distance (0–1 km from campus gate)**: You can reach your department in 10–20 minutes on foot. Rent tends to be slightly higher because of convenience. Suitable for students who prefer not to rely on vehicles. However, the immediate vicinity of the campus has limited housing stock, so availability is tight.

![Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (IITKGP)](https://img.unistay.net/housing/p/amber-1223208.jpg)

- **Bicycle or scooter range (1–3 km)**: The most common radius for off-campus students. A bicycle costs INR 2,000–5,000 new; a second-hand scooter can be INR 15,000–30,000. Commute time about 10–15 minutes by bike, 5–10 minutes by scooter. Rent is more affordable here, and you have more options—PGs and apartments along main roads. The downside is that you need to invest in a vehicle and maintain it. Auto-rickshaws are available but can be irregular; a one-way ride costs INR 20–50 depending on distance.
- **Beyond 3 km (e.g., Kharagpur town centre, near railway station)**: Rent can be cheaper (maybe INR 3,000–6,000 for a basic room), but daily commute may take 20–30 minutes by bus or auto. Public buses are infrequent and crowded. This option suits students who are comfortable with a longer commute and want to save money. It’s less common among IIT students unless they have a car or frequent access to the campus shuttle (which runs limited routes).

Most students opt for the 1–3 km radius, as it balances cost and commute. A bicycle is the cheapest transport, and the flat terrain of Kharagpur makes cycling easy.

### Shortlist Logic: How to Choose

When you begin your housing search, follow a shortlist logic based on your priorities:

1. **Determine your budget** (see bands above). Set a monthly rent ceiling, including utilities.
2. **Decide on room type**: shared vs. single, AC vs. non-AC, with or without meals. Meals can save cooking time but limit flexibility.
3. **Choose a transport mode**: If you already have a bicycle or scooter, you can widen your search to 2–3 km. If not, walkable options are more important.
4. **Search by area**: Common student clusters include:
   - **Inside campus (hostel / guest house)** – only if you secure university accommodation.
   - **“IIT Main Road” and “New Hall area”** – walking distance (0.5–1 km).
   - **“Kharagpur Station Road” and “Old Kharagpur”** – 2–3 km, many PGs.
   - **Near “B.C. Roy Hospital” or “Malancha”** – up to 4 km, lower rent.
5. **Inspect the property**: Visit in person to check cleanliness, water supply, security, and neighbours. Read university notice boards and student Facebook groups for leads. Do not rely solely on online listings.
6. **Negotiate lease terms**: Ask for a written agreement, verify owner details, and clarify deposit refund terms. Many landlords accept students with a valid institute ID.

Using this logic, you can shortlist 3–5 options, then compare based on total commute time (door-to-door), cost, and comfort. Always confirm availability for the 2026–2027 academic session.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is it better to live on campus or off campus?**  
A: On-campus housing (hostels) is convenient—you are close to classes, libraries, and mess halls, and it’s easier to participate in campus activities. However, the review above notes that hostel upkeep can be lacking (“very bad condition”, “bring your own bedding”). Off-campus housing often gives you more control over cleanliness and privacy, but you need to handle commuting and may have higher utility costs. If you are an undergraduate, you will likely be required to live in a hostel for the first year; after that, you may choose to move off campus. For graduate students, off-campus is common. Weigh your priorities: convenience versus personal space.

**Q: What is the average rent for a single room near IIT Kharagpur?**  
A: Typical single-room rents (indicative) range from INR 6,000 to INR 12,000 per month, depending on whether the room has AC, attached bathroom, and how close it is to the campus. A basic non-AC room in a shared PG may cost INR 4,000–6,000. Always confirm the exact amount and what is included (water, electricity, internet). Prices can vary by 10–20% between academic years; negotiate for a longer lease.

**Q: How can I find safe accommodation as a student?**  
A: Start with official university resources: the Dean of Students’ office and hostel administration can sometimes provide off-campus listings or vetted contacts. Join student-run social media groups (e.g., “IIT Kharagpur Accommodation” on Facebook). When visiting a property, go during daylight hours, preferably with a senior student. Ask about building security (locks, guards, CCTV). Ensure the landlord is willing to provide a rent receipt or agreement. Avoid paying large upfront amounts without a contract. In case of issues, the institute’s grievance cell can offer guidance.
