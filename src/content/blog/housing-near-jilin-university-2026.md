## City Context: Changchun at a Glance

Changchun is the capital of Jilin Province in northeast China, with a population over 8 million. The city has a continental monsoon climate, meaning cold, dry winters and warm, humid summers. For students, this affects housing choices: insulation and heating become important from November to March. Average winter temperatures hover around -15°C, so a well-heated apartment is a priority.

The Qianwei campus is located in the southwest of Changchun, near the intersection of Qianjin Street and Weishan Road. This area is a student hub, with multiple university campuses nearby (but not named here). Public transport includes bus routes and metro Line 3 (the “light rail”), with stations like Weixing Square and Changchun World Sculpture Park within walking distance. The city center (Renmin Square) is about 8 km northeast, reachable in 30 minutes by metro or 40 minutes by bus. Changchun Longjia International Airport is about 35 km east.

Cost of living in Changchun is relatively low. A meal at a campus canteen may cost 10–20 CNY, and a monthly food budget for a student can range from 1,000 to 1,800 CNY. When it comes to housing, prices are proportionally lower than in Beijing or Shanghai. Students should expect to pay for utilities separately (electricity, water, internet, heating). Heating is often provided centrally in winter and may be included in rent or charged as a flat fee.

## Budget Bands for Student Housing

![Jilin University](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-1671889.jpg)

Based on typical market conditions near JLU’s Qianwei campus (and always cross-checked with operators), here is a rough guide to monthly rent ranges. These are indicative — confirm with the operator before signing any contract.

- **Budget (shared rooms):** 600 – 1,200 CNY per month. This usually means a bed in a shared dormitory-style room (2–4 people per room) in a purpose-built student apartment or a shared flat with other students. Facilities are basic: shared kitchen and bathroom, possibly included utilities.
- **Mid-range (private studio or one-bedroom):** 1,500 – 2,500 CNY per month. A self-contained studio near campus (within 1–2 km). These often have a separate bathroom, a small cooking area, and basic furniture. Heating may be additional (200–400 CNY per month in winter).
- **Comfort (larger apartment):** 2,800 – 4,000 CNY per month. A one-bedroom or two-bedroom apartment with better finishes, possibly in a newer residential compound with amenities like gym, security, and parking. Expect higher utility costs.

Accommodation closer to the main gate (Qianjin Street) tends to be more expensive due to convenience. Apartments 2–3 km away are cheaper but require a bus or metro trip. Students should budget an additional 100–200 CNY per month for public transport if living further out.

## Walk vs Transit: Choosing Your Location

The Qianwei campus is large, with teaching buildings spread across a sprawling site. Living within 15 minutes’ walk of the main teaching blocks can save time, especially in winter when walking outdoors is uncomfortable. The area immediately west of the campus (between Qianjin Street and the Yitong River) has many older residential compounds with small studios. Walking distance: less than 1 km. The trade-off: higher rent per square meter.

Living along the metro Line 3 corridor, for example near Weixing Square (two stops away), offers cheaper rents (budget to mid-range) and a 10-minute metro ride to the campus. The total commute (walk + metro + walk) takes about 25–30 minutes. For those who prefer bus, routes such as 13, 149, and 222 run along Qianjin Street. A bus ride from the southern residential areas (e.g., around Jilin Normal University – not named here) takes 15–20 minutes but may be crowded during peak hours.

Cycling is viable from April to October. Bicycle parking is available on campus. However, the city’s steep winter winds and icy roads make cycling impractical for at least three months a year.

## Shortlist Logic: How to Evaluate Nearby Residences

When creating a shortlist of housing options near JLU, consider these criteria based on the reviews and campus layout. This logic helps filter without needing specific residence names (which are not provided in the data). You can apply it to any listing you find through local platforms.

1. **Proximity to teaching buildings.** The main entrance at Qianjin Street is busy, but many departments are deeper inside the campus. Check which gate your classes are near. Living near the north gate (close to the library and science buildings) may be more convenient than the main gate. Look for apartments within 1 km of your usual entry point.
2. **Heating and insulation.** Winter temperatures can drop below -20°C. Ensure the flat has central heating (暖氣), double-glazed windows, and sealed doors. In shared rooms, check whether heating hours are controlled by a central system or can be adjusted.
3. **Noise and safety.** The area along Qianjin Street can be noisy due to traffic. Apartments set back from the main road are quieter. Check if the building has a security guard (保安) and intercom. Many compounds have a 24/7 guard and CCTV.
4. **Shopping and daily needs.** As one review noted, “shops around it to do good shopping”. The area around Weixing Square (a metro stop) has supermarkets, pharmacies, convenience stores, and restaurants. If you cook, a wet market (菜市场) on Weishan Road is near the campus. Prioritize residences within 10 minutes’ walk of such amenities.
5. **Lease terms and deposit.** Landlords typically ask for a one-month deposit and three months’ rent in advance. Some student-oriented apartments offer flexible terms (e.g., one semester). Confirm the policy for early termination. Many require 30 days’ notice.
6. **Roommate compatibility.** Shared rooms are common for budget accommodation. If you need a quiet study environment, consider a private studio even if it costs more. The extra academic focus may be worth it.
7. **Public transport directness.** If you live far, check whether a direct bus or metro line passes close to your door. A combination of two buses can add 15 minutes each way. The metro Line 3 is the most reliable option.
8. **Internet speed.** JLU offers campus-wide Wi-Fi, but off-campus flats may have slower connections. Check if the building has fiber optic (光纤) internet. Students often need reliable internet for online materials and video calls.
