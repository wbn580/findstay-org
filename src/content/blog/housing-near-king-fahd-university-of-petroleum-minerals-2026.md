### City Context: Dhahran and the Eastern Province

KFUPM’s campus is located on the border between Dhahran and Al Khobar, within the Dammam metropolitan area — a tri-city region of roughly 2 million people. Dhahran is home to Saudi Aramco’s headquarters and has a planned, expatriate-friendly layout with gated compounds, wide roads, and green spaces. Al Khobar, directly south, is a more commercial and residential city with a corniche, shopping centres, and a mix of apartment buildings and villas.

The climate is arid and extremely hot from May to September, with average highs above 40°C. Winters are mild and pleasant. This climate shapes housing choices: air conditioning is a must, and proximity to campus becomes more critical during the summer months when walking even short distances is uncomfortable.

The local population includes a large international community, especially professionals working in oil and gas. Consequently, rental housing is often tailored to expatriate standards: furnished apartments, compound villas with shared amenities (pools, gyms, playgrounds), and serviced residences. Rental contracts are typically annual, though some short-term options exist for conference visitors or exchange students.

**Band 2: Mid-Range Off-Campus Apartments (Al Khobar)**

- Two-bedroom unfurnished apartment: SAR 2,500–3,500 per month.

**Band 3: Shared Accommodation / Room Rentals**

- Room in a shared villa or compound: SAR 800–1,500 per month per person, often including utilities and basic furnishings.
- Common in Dhahran’s gated compounds (e.g., those near Aramco). Requires a car or ride-hailing for campus access.

**Band 4: Luxury Serviced Apartments (Al Khobar Corniche)**

- Two-bedroom serviced apartment: SAR 4,000–6,000 per month, including high-speed internet, gym, pool, and housekeeping. Suitable for families or postgraduates with larger budgets.

Important: Many landlords ask for a full year’s rent upfront or post-dated cheques. Negotiation is possible for longer stays. Students on a tight budget should consider shared compounds or university accommodation.

### Walk vs Transit Trade-off

Walking to KFUPM is feasible only for those living in immediate on-campus residences (KFUPMINN) or in the residential area immediately east of the university (within 1 km). During summer, even a 10-minute walk in the midday heat can be challenging. The campus itself is spacious, so internal walking between buildings is common and air-conditioned walkways exist in some parts.

![King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals](https://img.unistay.net/housing/p/amber-2301860.jpg)

Public transportation in Dhahran–Al Khobar is limited. There is no metro or regular bus service that connects directly to the university gate. Most students and staff rely on private cars, university shuttles (for on-campus residents), or ride-hailing (Uber, Careem). A typical ride from central Al Khobar to KFUPM costs SAR 15–25 and takes 10–15 minutes depending on traffic.

**Trade-off summary:**

- **Walk / short commute:** On-campus housing (KFUPMINN) offers the shortest walk to classes, dining halls, and libraries. Cost is relatively higher, but convenience is unmatched.
- **Drive / commute:** Off-campus apartments in Al Khobar or Dhahran offer more space and lower rent per square metre, but require a reliable vehicle or budget for daily ride-hailing. Driving is generally easy with ample free parking on campus.
- **Transit scarcity:** Without a car, students should prioritise housing within walking distance or in compounds that provide shuttle services. Bicycle use is possible but discouraged during extreme heat.

For the 2026–2027 year, it is realistic to budget an additional SAR 300–600 per month for transport if living off-campus.

**2. Budget vs. Included Services**

- Compare the all-in cost (rent + utilities + internet + transport). KFUPMINN may appear expensive, but it includes utilities, meals (breakfast mentioned in reviews), and zero commute cost.
- Off-campus apartments may have lower base rent but add utility bills, furniture if unfurnished, and transport. Use the indicative bands above to calculate total monthly expenditure.

**3. Lease Flexibility**

- KFUPMINN likely offers semester-based contracts aligned with the academic calendar (confirm with the operator). Off-market apartments usually require annual contracts with early-termination penalties. For short-term stays (exchange students, summer research), serviced apartments or KFUPMINN are safer.

**4. Privacy and Lifestyle**

- Single students may prefer the social environment of a compound with shared amenities. Families often choose two-bedroom apartments in compounds like those on Prince Faisal Bin Fahd Road (Al Khobar).
- KFUPMINN is described as “luxurious” and clean, but it is a managed residence with possibly stricter rules (quiet hours, guest policies). Check these before signing.

**5. Safety and Security**

- Dhahran and Al Khobar are considered very safe. On-campus residences have 24/7 security. Compounds have gate guards. Standard apartments in well-lit streets are also generally safe.
