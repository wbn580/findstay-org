## City Context: Riyadh – A Sprawling Capital

Alfaisal University is located at Al Zahrawi Street, intersecting with Al Takhassousi, in the Al Maather district of Riyadh. Riyadh is a sprawling, car‑centric city with limited public transit compared to many global capitals. The Riyadh Metro is under phased opening, but many areas still rely on taxis, ride‑hailing apps, or private cars.

Al Maather is a central district, near major roads and commercial zones. The area around the university is a mix of residential compounds, apartment buildings, and office towers. Students often choose between:

- **Walkable compounds** directly adjacent to campus – ideal for those who want to avoid traffic.
- **Apartments a few kilometres away** – more affordable but requiring a daily commute.
- **Shared villas** in quieter residential lanes – common for groups of students.

Riyadh has a continental desert climate: very hot summers (above 45°C) and mild winters. Walking in summer is only realistic for very short distances (under 10–15 minutes). During the cooler months (November–February), longer walks become feasible.

## Walk vs. Transit Trade‑Off

### Walking

The university address is on Al Zahrawi Street, near Al Takhassousi and Al Maather. Some residential buildings and apartment towers are within a 10–20 minute walk. Benefits:

- Avoid traffic jams (Riyadh peak hours can double commute times).
- Easier to attend late‑night classes or library sessions.
- No reliance on ride‑hailing apps (Uber, Careem) or taxis, saving money.

Drawbacks:

- Limited availability of affordable units within a comfortable walk (many nearby properties are newer and pricier).
- Summer heat makes walking uncomfortable or even unsafe for distances over 400 metres between June and September.
