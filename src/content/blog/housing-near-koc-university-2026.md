## Housing Near Koç University: What Campus Reviews Reveal

Koç University, ranked 323 in the QS World University Rankings, is located on the northern edge of Istanbul in the Sarıyer district, near the Black Sea coast. The campus itself sits at Rumelifeneri, roughly 25 km north of Taksim Square. With a Google rating of 4.4 from 910 reviews, the university draws consistent praise for its facilities, academic environment, and food options — while a few reviews highlight isolated service issues.

For anyone planning to study at Koç University and looking for accommodation nearby, understanding the campus context, local transport, and realistic budgets is essential. This guide synthesises what current and former students say, and offers a structured approach to shortlisting housing options near the university.

### City Context: Sarıyer and Northern Istanbul

Koç University’s Rumelifeneri campus is not in the heart of Istanbul. It is located on a peninsula at the northern entrance of the Bosphorus, surrounded by forested hills and the Black Sea coast. The nearest neighbourhoods with housing stock are Sarıyer town centre (about 7 km south), Rumelihisarı (12 km), and Bebek (15 km) along the Bosphorus.

Sarıyer is a relatively upmarket area with a mix of apartment blocks and detached houses, known for seafood restaurants and waterfront promenades. Further south, Rumelihisarı and Bebek are more expensive, popular with locals and international students alike. Commuting from central districts like Beşiktaş or Taksim is possible but time-consuming (45–90 minutes each way by bus or minibus).

The climate is mild-to-cool, with winters often rainy and windy (especially near the coast), and summers warm but not as sweltering as central Istanbul due to breezes from the Black Sea.

### Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

Koç University’s campus is a self-contained community. On-campus housing (dormitories) is the most convenient option, especially for first-year students. Walking to classes, library, and food outlets takes minutes.

If you choose off-campus living in Sarıyer centre, walking to campus is not feasible (7 km, mostly uphill). You would rely on the university’s shuttle buses (often free or subsidised for students) or public buses (lines 59R, 59RH, 59K, etc. from Sarıyer to Rumelifeneri). The shuttle journey from Sarıyer takes about 15–20 minutes, while public buses may take 25–35 minutes depending on traffic.

Living further south (Rumelihisarı, Bebek) adds another 10–20 minutes to the commute. The upside is more vibrant neighbourhoods with cafes, shops, and nightlife. The trade-off is that you spend 40–60 minutes each way, which over a week adds up to 7–10 hours of travel. If you value proximity to campus life or need to spend long hours in the library, living within shuttle distance from Sarıyer or on campus is a better fit.

For students with cars, parking on campus is available but limited. Fuel and insurance costs should be factored in.

   - Lower budget (under 10,000 TRY per month): on-campus shared room or a small room in a shared apartment in Sarıyer outskirts.
   - Mid-range (10,000–16,000 TRY): private room in a newer apartment in Sarıyer or a studio in an older building.
   - Higher budget (above 16,000 TRY): studio in Bebek or a larger private apartment in Sarıyer.
3. **Check the review pattern.**

   - From the campus reviews, students appreciate good food and quiet study spots. Ensure your chosen residence has decent kitchen facilities if you plan to cook, or is near food outlets (Sarıyer centre has many restaurants and grocery stores).
   - The negative review about the clinic suggests that on-campus health facilities may not meet everyone’s expectations; if you need reliable medical access, living in a neighbourhood with hospitals or clinics (Sarıyer has both) might be preferable.
4. **Verify transportation connectivity.**

   - Use the university’s official shuttle schedule (often published at the start of each semester) to see if your shortlisted address falls within a shuttle route.    - Student reviews online can help, but always request a virtual tour or, if possible, an in-person visit. Pay attention to heating (winters are damp and cold), internet speed, and hot water reliability.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### **Is it better to live on campus or off campus?**

On-campus housing offers the shortest commute and full immersion in university life. Off-campus housing in Sarıyer centre provides more independence and lower cost (private rooms often cheaper than on-campus dorms) but requires a shuttle or bus ride. If you prefer a quieter environment and prioritise study time, on-campus is ideal. If you value having your own space or cooking your own meals, a Sarıyer apartment may suit you better.

#### **What is the typical commute time from Sarıyer to campus?**

The shuttle from Sarıyer town centre to Koç University takes about 15–20 minutes. Public buses take longer, typically 25–35 minutes, depending on traffic. During peak morning hours, buses can be crowded, so plan accordingly. If you live in Bebek, expect 40–50 minutes one way by bus or shuttle.

#### **What should I consider regarding safety and amenities?**

Sarıyer is generally considered safe, but common-sense precautions apply (lock doors, avoid walking alone late at night in unlit areas). When comparing residences, check for: 24-hour security or doorman; reliable heating and hot water; access to a washing machine; and internet connection quality (essential for online classes and research). On-campus dormitories often have stricter rules (curfew, guests policy), while private apartments offer more freedom but require you to manage utilities and maintenance yourself.
