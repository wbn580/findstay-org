## Student Housing Near L.N. Gumilyov Eurasian National University: A Practical Guide

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Student feedback about L.N. Gumilyov Eurasian National University (ENU) offers useful clues for choosing a place to live. One reviewer, LOONAR, gives a 5‑star rating and writes: “library of this university is so comfortable and beautiful place 🪴💍🤍”. This suggests that the campus provides a pleasant study environment, which matters if you plan to spend long hours in the library. Living close enough to walk there easily would be a clear advantage.

Another reviewer, Otto, rates the university a 3 and notes: “It’s OK, compared to KazNU and other national and state university.” While the comment is about the institution itself, it hints that ENU may not be considered the top choice in Kazakhstan, but it is still a solid option. For a student, this means the surrounding area should offer reasonable amenities and housing options without the premium prices attached to the most prestigious campuses.

Both reviews are several years old, but the physical setting of the university has not changed dramatically. The focus on a “comfortable and beautiful” library indicates that a quiet, student‑friendly neighbourhood near the Satpayev Street campus would match the priorities of many residents.

### City Context: Astana (Nur-Sultan)

ENU is located in Astana (officially Nur-Sultan from 2019 to 2022, now again Astana), the capital of Kazakhstan. The address is Satpayev 2, Almaty District. The city is known for its modern architecture and rapid development, but winters are long and cold (average temperatures in January often drop below –15 °C). Summers are warm, which influences the walk‑versus‑transit decision.

The university sits in the Almaty District, a central area of the city. Nearby you will find residential blocks, shops, and public services. The Ishim River runs through the city centre, and the campus is within a few kilometres of government buildings, museums, and parks. For daily life, this means that a range of housing options exists within walking distance, though prices tend to increase as you move closer to the university.

Public transport in Astana includes buses (routes connect most districts), minibuses, and taxis. A bus journey from a peripheral neighbourhood to the campus can take 20–40 minutes, depending on traffic. In winter, a short walk from a bus stop to the university can be uncomfortable in extreme cold, so living within a 10‑minute walk of the main building is a practical advantage.

### Walk vs Transit Trade‑Off

The main decision for ENU students is whether to live within walking distance or to rely on public transport.

- **Walking zones (0–1.5 km):** Apartments on or near Satpayev Street, as well as adjacent residential blocks, allow a 10‑ to 20‑minute walk to the main entrance. In summer, this is pleasant and free. In winter, you will need heavy coats and boots, but the commute is very short. Rents in this band tend to be higher per square metre.
- **Transit‑dependent (2–5 km):** Cheaper apartments are found in districts like Almaty District fringes, Saryarka, or even further east. A bus ride of 15–30 minutes each way is common. Costs for a monthly bus pass are modest, but you add travel time – and waiting in cold weather reduces comfort significantly.

### Shortlist Logic Using Nearby Residences

Although no specific student‑housing projects were provided for this article, you can apply a logical screening process when you search for apartments in the Satpayev Street area. Here is a simple shortlist method:

1. **Distance first:** Filter by walking time to the campus main gate (Satpayev 2). Use Google Maps or a local app to estimate walking duration – aim for under 20 minutes.
2. **Check the building’s condition:** Look for recent construction or well‑maintained older buildings. Read any tenant reviews online about heating reliability and noise levels.
3. **Visit in person if possible:** The Almaty District includes both modern high‑rises and Soviet‑era blocks. A visit will reveal if the building is safe, clean, and quiet.
4. **Compare two or three options:** Write down the rent, deposit, and included utilities. Factor in the cost of a bus pass if you pick a distant apartment. Choose the one that balances budget and commute time.
5. **Ask about lease terms:** Many landlords in Astana prefer 6‑ or 12‑month contracts. Short‑term rentals (month‑to‑month) are less common and often more expensive.
