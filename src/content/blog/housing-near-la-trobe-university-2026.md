## City Context: Melbourne and Bundoora

Melbourne is Australia’s second‑largest city and a major international student hub. Bundoora is a suburban area with a mix of residential streets, parks, and small shopping precincts. The La Trobe campus sits within walking distance of several supermarkets, cafes, and bus stops. The nearest train station is about three kilometres away at Macleod or Reservoir, but a free university shuttle bus runs between the campus and Macleod station during semester. Many students rely on buses that stop right on Plenty Road. Cycling is also feasible; the campus has bike racks and some dedicated paths.

Living in Bundoora means lower rents compared to inner‑Melbourne suburbs such as Carlton or Fitzroy. Utility costs (electricity, internet) are typical for Victoria. Grocery stores such as Woolworths and Coles are about a 15‑minute walk or a short bus ride from campus. The nearest major shopping centre is Northland Shopping Centre in Preston, a 10‑minute bus ride away.

The academic year at La Trobe runs from late February to November, with a mid‑year break. Many students look for lease terms aligned with the semester calendar, although year‑round leases are common. For the 2026 and 2027 academic years, planning ahead for accommodation is advisable, particularly for February intake.

## Budget Bands for Student Housing (2026‑2027 Estimates)

![La Trobe University](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-1742954.jpg)

Rental prices in Bundoora and surrounding suburbs vary by property type, distance to campus, and inclusions. All figures below are indicative — confirm with the operator.

1. **Shared room (per person per week):** AUD 190 – 260  
   Typically in a multi‑bedroom house or apartment with shared kitchen, bathroom, and living areas. Often includes basic furnishings and sometimes utilities.
2. **Studio or self‑contained unit (per week):** AUD 320 – 450  
   Private studio with own kitchen and bathroom. May include a small desk area. Most are close to public transport or within 1‑2 km of campus.
3. **One‑bedroom apartment (per week):** AUD 400 – 550  
   Separate bedroom, living room, kitchen, and full bathroom. Often located in newer apartment blocks along Plenty Road or near University Hill shopping centre.
4. **On‑campus or managed student accommodation (per week):** AUD 280 – 480  
   Purpose‑built student housing (PBSA) with utilities, internet, and sometimes cleaning included. Contract lengths of 26 or 52 weeks are common. Some residences have communal study rooms, gyms, and social events.
5. **Short‑term or homestay (per week):** AUD 250 – 350  
   Includes meals and bills. Suitable for the first few weeks while searching for long‑term rental. Usually arranged through a homestay platform.

Remember that rental prices fluctuate and depend on lease start dates, property condition, and availability. Always check the exact inclusions (electricity, gas, water, internet) before signing.

## Walk vs. Transit: Choosing Your Location

### Walking Distance (up to 20‑minute walk to campus)

- **Pros:** No transport cost; flexible schedule; easy to go back to campus for evening classes or library access. Nearby streets like Kingsbury Drive, Plenty Road, and Waterdale Road have a mix of houses and units.
- **Cons:** Rent tends to be higher for properties closest to campus. Limited housing stock – popular leases are taken early (October‑November for February start).

### Transit‑Dependent (bus or train + shuttle)

- **Pros:** Wider choice of suburbs such as Reservoir, Preston, Thomastown, or even Brunswick for a more urban lifestyle. Rent can be AUD 30–80 per week cheaper than equivalent properties near campus.
- **Cons:** Requires a myki card and regular top‑ups. Bus frequency is good on weekdays but drops on weekends and late evenings. The free campus shuttle from Macleod station runs until about 6 pm on weekdays only. Travel time from central Melbourne (Flinders Street) to Bundoora is roughly 45–60 minutes by train and bus.

Many students choose a compromise: a 10‑ to 15‑minute walk to a bus stop that goes directly to campus. That reduces walking time while still giving access to more affordable housing.

## How to Shortlist Student Accommodation Near La Trobe

When searching for housing near La Trobe University, a systematic shortlist helps avoid wasted time. Use the following logic as a filter:

1. **Set a maximum walking distance** (e.g., 1 km from campus) or a maximum commute time (e.g., 30 minutes using public transport). Enter this in the platform’s map view.
2. **Define your budget band** from the estimates above. Remember to add AUD 15–25 per week for utilities if not included. For indicative prices, always confirm with the operator.
3. **Check the lease start date and minimum term.** If you arrive in February 2026, look for leases starting in late January or early February. Some purpose‑built student residences offer flexible start dates.
4. **Read recent resident reviews.** On your chosen platform, look for comments about noise, cleanliness, property management responsiveness, and internet speed. Avoid properties with consistent complaints about maintenance.
5. **Verify inclusions:** Ask whether the rent covers electricity, gas, water, and internet. Some PBSA bundles everything; private rentals usually exclude bills.
6. **Inspect virtually or in person.** If you are overseas, request a video tour. Check the condition of appliances, bed, desk, and storage. Ask about pest history.
7. **Compare at least three options.** Write down rent, distance, inclusions, and contract terms. Then rank by your priority (cost, commute, space).

By following this process, you can narrow down to two or three suitable properties before making an application.

## FAQ

### How far is La Trobe University from Melbourne’s city centre?

The Bundoora campus is approximately 16 km north‑east of Melbourne’s CBD. By public transport (train from Flinders Street to Macleod, then free shuttle bus), the journey takes about 50–60 minutes. Driving takes 25–40 minutes depending on traffic.

### What is the typical rent for a studio near La Trobe University in 2026?

An indicative weekly rent for a studio within 1 km of campus is between AUD 320 and 450. Prices vary by inclusions (some include utilities, others do not). Always confirm with the operator before booking.

### Are utilities usually included in student accommodation near La Trobe?

For purpose‑built student accommodation, utilities (electricity, gas, water, internet) are often included in the weekly rent. For private apartments or shared houses, utilities are typically not included and cost an extra AUD 30–50 per week. Ask the operator or landlord for a clear breakdown.
