## City and Campus Overview

Lincoln University College is located in Kota Bharu, the capital of Kelantan, Malaysia, at Jalan Lembah Sireh, Kampung Putih. The school is ranked 638th in the QS rankings, with a Google rating of 4.2 based on 109 reviews. Kota Bharu is the cultural center of the east coast, known for traditional handicrafts, Islamic architecture, and rich Malay cuisine. The pace of life is relatively slow, and the cost of living is lower than in Kuala Lumpur. The area around the campus has relatively dense residential areas, but the public transportation system is less developed than in larger cities; students mainly rely on walking, cycling, or ride-hailing services.

From student reviews, the academic atmosphere at Lincoln University College is recognized by some. For example, Omar Hamwi (5 stars) writes: “My 5 years study journey at Lincoln University has been both fulfilling and valuable. The lecturers bring strong expertise and are very supportive throughout the course, while the administrative team is approachable and very helpful.” Khaled Hamwi (5 stars) also mentions: “Studying at Lincoln has been one of the most rewarding academic journeys. The university is supported by highly qualified and experienced academic staff, along with friendly and helpful administrative personnel.” In addition, Me Ow (5 stars) comments: “Lincoln University College offers a solid MD program. The clinical supervisors are experienced and treat students with a lot of care, and the staff is always helpful and understanding making it a great environment for medical studies.”

However, there are also negative reviews. mohd izuan jamil (1 star) points out: “Very bad management. Don’t waste time to study here. Student welfare not taken care of event you email to the Dean of faculty, nil reply from the Dean as well university itself. Poor management from nursing faculty. Not recommended to study here.” This indicates that the quality of school management and services is controversial. Students may need to allocate more time to handle communications with the school themselves when choosing accommodation.

## Student Accommodation Budget Ranges

Student accommodation prices in Kota Bharu are relatively affordable. Based on the local market (not specific apartment data), for the 2026–2027 academic year, monthly rents for common room types can be roughly divided into three tiers (the following prices are indicative; confirm the final amount with the landlord or operator):

- **Economy (shared room or simple single room)**: 200–400 MYR per month (approx. 320–640 RMB). These are typically in older terrace houses or apartments with basic facilities, possibly shared bathrooms and kitchen. Suitable for students on a very tight budget with low privacy requirements.
- **Standard (private single room/small unit)**: 500–800 MYR per month (approx. 800–1280 RMB). Common in dedicated student dormitories or apartment buildings, with basic furniture (bed, desk, chair), air conditioning, and private bathroom. Usually a 10–20 minute walk to campus.
- **Comfortable (one-bedroom or large apartment)**: 900–1400 MYR per month (approx. 1440–2240 RMB). These units are larger, with a separate living room, kitchen, and bathroom, newer finishes, and some are in gated communities. Suitable for students who want more private space or live with a partner.

The above prices do not include utilities (about 50–100 MYR per month) and internet (30–80 MYR per month). Some landlords require half-year or full-year rent in advance; confirm payment terms in detail before signing.

## Walking vs. Public Transit Trade-offs

![Lincoln University College, Malaysia](https://img.findstay.org/housing/p/amber-1534005.jpg)

The Jalan Lembah Sireh area where Lincoln University College is located is not the city center of Kota Bharu, but there are some small shops and food courts within walking distance. Accommodation near campus is mainly concentrated in the following directions (no specific apartment names):

- **Walking distance (approx. 1 km radius)**: Terrace house areas and standalone houses right next to the school; students can walk 5–15 minutes to classrooms. Advantage: no transportation needed, saving time and money. Disadvantage: limited options, older properties, and potentially noisy (especially near main roads).
- **Walking plus short bus ride (2–3 km radius)**: Kota Bharu's public bus routes are limited and infrequent. More common is cycling (many local students use bicycles) or ride-hailing (Grab, etc.). From about 2 km from campus, cycling takes about 10–15 minutes, one-way ride-hailing costs about 5–8 MYR. This area has more housing options, rents slightly lower than the core walking area, and the environment is quieter.
- **Vehicle-dependent area (over 3 km)**: There are many new apartments in the western and southern parts of Kota Bharu, but they are farther from campus. Students choosing these areas typically use ride-hailing daily or buy a motorcycle (with an international license). Monthly transport costs about 150–300 MYR. Advantage: higher quality accommodation and lower prices (due to distance from campus), but need to consider commute time and unpredictable traffic delays.

Trade-off suggestion: If you are in medical or MD courses with irregular schedules (including night clinicals), the flexibility of walking or cycling is a clear advantage. If you are in management or arts programs with more tolerance for commute time, you can choose cheaper comfortable accommodation further away.

## Practical Logic for Screening Accommodation

Since this data does not provide specific property names and prices in Kota Bharu, the following logic can help students search and filter on their own:

1. **Determine priority needs**: Based on your class schedule, budget, and quietness requirements, list hard conditions (e.g., need for private bathroom, willingness to share, maximum monthly rent).
2. **Set search radius**: Use map tools, set the campus address as center, create three search circles: 1 km, 2 km, 3 km. Check street view to see if walking routes have sidewalks and night lighting.
3. **Verify property conditions**: When contacting the landlord or agent, insist on a video tour or in-person visit. Focus on air conditioning cooling, hot water supply, mold on walls (Kelantan is humid), and door/window security.
4. **Confirm contract details**: Beware of clauses like “no deposit refund.” Require a written contract specifying what rent includes (utilities and internet separate?), maintenance responsibilities, subletting or early termination conditions.
5. **Refer to current students**: Join unofficial student groups of Lincoln University College (e.g., WhatsApp groups, Facebook Groups) and ask for recent accommodation recommendations. Be discerning about reviews – as the negative review above shows, some students are dissatisfied with management, which may also reflect in landlord relationships.
6. **Try short-term rental as transition**: If unsure about long-term choice, first stay in Airbnb or short-term rental for 1–2 weeks, using that time to visit several potential properties. Short-term rental in Kota Bharu costs about 30–60 MYR per day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there on-campus student dorms in Kota Bharu? Does Lincoln University College provide accommodation?

According to available information, it is not clear whether Lincoln University College itself operates on-campus dormitories. Most students rent off-campus housing. It is recommended to contact the university's admissions office or student affairs office directly before enrollment to ask about housing arrangements. If the school does not provide it, you will need to find nearby terrace houses or apartments on your own.

### How long is the typical contract? Can rent be paid monthly?

In the rental market of Kelantan, common contract periods are 6 months or 12 months. A few landlords may accept monthly rent, but the rent will be slightly higher (usually 50–100 MYR more per month). It is advisable to negotiate early termination terms before signing, such as giving one month's notice to move out, to avoid losing the deposit due to schedule changes.

### What documents do international students need to rent a place?

You typically need to provide a copy of your passport, student visa or student ID, a deposit (1–2 months' rent), and the first month's rent. Some landlords may require a guarantor; in that case, you can provide a letter of guarantee from the school (which can be requested from the International Student Office). Ensure all documents are clear and readable, and keep copies.
