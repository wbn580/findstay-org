## City Context: Nanjing

Nanjing, the capital of Jiangsu Province, is a major historical and cultural center in China (Mainland). The university is located at Gulou, Nanjing, Jiangsu, China, 210093. The city is well-connected by an extensive metro system, buses, and taxis. The Gulou campus sits in the old city center, close to attractions like Confucius Temple, Xuanwu Lake, and the Presidential Palace. The Xianlin campus is in the northeastern suburban area of Xianlin University City, about 20–30 kilometers from the center. Rent levels vary significantly between these two zones. The historic core (Gulou, Xinjiekou) tends to have higher rents due to limited supply and proximity to amenities. The Xianlin area, being a purpose-built university district with multiple institutions, offers more purpose-built student housing and shared apartments at lower prices. The local economy is driven by education, technology, and manufacturing, with a large student population creating a stable rental market. Temperate weather with distinct seasons — hot summers and cool winters — should be factored into accommodation choices (e.g., need for air conditioning or heating).

## Walk vs Transit Trade-off

Nanjing University’s two campuses offer very different living options.

**Gulou campus** (main historic campus): Living within walking distance (15–20 minutes) is ideal if you want to be in the heart of the city. The surrounding area has narrow streets, older buildings, and limited parking. Public transit is dense, but walking can be faster than taking a bus or metro for distances under 2 km. Rent premiums exist due to central location. If you are willing to walk 30–40 minutes or take one metro stop, you can find cheaper housing in neighborhoods like Yunnan Road or Caochangmen.

**Xianlin campus**: The Xianlin University City is purposely laid out for students. Most housing is within a 10–30 minute walk from the campus. Bicycle is the most common mode of transport; many students buy a second-hand bike or use shared bikes. Buses and the metro (Line 2) connect Xianlin to central Nanjing in about 40–60 minutes. If you prefer a shorter commute, live in the residential compounds right next to the campus (e.g., Xianlin New Village, Xianlin Shuijingcheng). If you prefer cheaper rent and don’t mind a 15–20 minute bike ride, you can live slightly further out near the 2nd Ring Road.

The trade-off comes down to: **Gulou** offers cultural immersion and walkability but higher rent and older buildings. **Xianlin** offers newer, cheaper housing and a quiet environment, but requires commuting for city-center activities.
