## What Campus Reviews Reveal

National Cheng Kung University (NCKU) welcomes students from around the world to its campus in Tainan, Taiwan. With a global QS ranking of 203 and a Google rating of 4.6 based on 4,104 reviews, the university consistently draws positive feedback about its atmosphere and facilities. Student reviews paint a picture of a spacious, peaceful environment that balances green spaces with academic buildings.

One visitor, Le Hoang Giap, describes walking around and feeling “a really pleasant and open campus to explore. Compared to busier city areas, the environment here is much calmer, with a nice balance between greenery and academic buildings. What stood out to me was how spacious everyt[hing].” This sense of openness is echoed by Luna Leo, who visited for a work trip and found “a huge peaceful place where students can study and have other activities. They have all kinds of activities for students to enjoy.”

The campus also serves as a community hub. W. Lucht notes it is “a great place to walk around. The campus is an amazing part of Tainan. In the morning 🌄 there are many folks who walk and amble, exercising and going about their day.” International students appreciate the multicultural environment: Luong Nguyen mentions meeting “a lot a friends here. So many foreigners student studying there. Good school to exchange languages and culture and doing academic research. Bonus. There is a nice sport station for you to run in the afternoon.”

These reviews highlight that NCKU’s location actively shapes the student experience. Proximity to such a lively yet calm campus can make daily life more enjoyable. When searching for housing nearby, understanding the campus vibe and the city context helps you prioritize distance, atmosphere, and budget.

## City Context: Tainan and the University Area

NCKU is situated in the East District of Tainan, Taiwan’s oldest city. Tainan is known for its historic temples, night markets, and a laid-back pace of life compared to Taipei. The university’s address — No. 1, Dasyue Rd, Chengda Village, East District — places it near the Tainan Train Station, providing easy access to the rest of the city and beyond.

The area around NCKU is student-oriented. You will find eateries, convenience stores, bookshops, and clinics clustered along Dasyue Road and the adjacent alleys. The campus itself is large, with multiple gates leading to different neighborhoods. Living close to the main gates can cut your walking time to classrooms to under 10 minutes, while living a bit farther may mean a 15–20 minute walk or a short bike ride.

Tainan’s public bus system covers the city, but many students rely on bicycles or scooters for flexibility. YouBike, the public bike-sharing system, has stations near campus. The flat terrain makes cycling comfortable year-round, though summer can be hot and humid.

## Budget Bands for Student Housing (2026–2027)

![National Cheng Kung University (NCKU)](https://img.unistay.net/housing/p/amber-1357349.jpg)

Housing costs near NCKU vary depending on room type, distance from campus, and amenities. As a general guide, rental prices fall into three bands. Note that these are **indicative — confirm with the operator** before making any decisions.

- **Shared rooms or dormitory-style beds:** These are the most affordable option. You rent a bed in a room shared with one or more roommates. Monthly rates typically range from NT$3,000 to NT$6,000. This option often includes utilities and basic furniture.
- **Private rooms in shared apartments:** A single room in a shared flat with a common kitchen and bathroom. Prices usually run from NT$5,000 to NT$10,000 per month. The exact cost depends on room size, floor level, and whether the apartment has air conditioning (common in Tainan).
- **Individual studios or small apartments:** For complete privacy, a studio apartment typically costs NT$8,000 to NT$15,000 per month. These may be located in student-specific apartment buildings or in general residential blocks near the university.

Always verify current listings through local platforms or on-the-ground visits. Contracts are usually for 12 months, and deposits of one to two months’ rent are standard. Many landlords require a guarantor (often a Taiwanese national), so international students might need to negotiate or use university-provided housing services.

## Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

NCKU’s campus is pedestrian-friendly, but the choice between walking and using transit depends on where you live.

**Walking** is the simplest option if you find housing within a 10–15 minute walk from your main classroom building. The campus has several entrances, so check which gate is closest to your department. Areas within walking distance include the streets immediately around the university: Shengli Road, Dasyue Road, and the alleys near the back gate (the area known as “Back Campus”). Walking saves money, keeps you fit, and avoids traffic. However, walkable apartments tend to be more expensive due to demand.

**Transit** (buses, bikes, or scooters) becomes necessary if you choose cheaper housing farther away. Tainan’s bus network covers most parts of the city, but frequencies can be low on weekends. Many students prefer a bicycle: YouBike rentals cost NT$10–20 per 30 minutes, and a used bike can be bought for NT$1,000–3,000. A scooter is faster but requires an international driver’s permit and insurance. Parking on campus is available for a small fee.

The trade-off is clear: living within walking distance costs more per month but saves time and commuting stress. Living farther out reduces rent but adds 15–30 minutes each way and some transport costs. Your choice should depend on your budget, schedule, and tolerance for commuting.

## Shortlist Logic: How to Choose Nearby Housing

When building a shortlist of potential places to live near NCKU, follow this logical sequence. No single residence is universally best — your ideal choice depends on your personal priorities.

1. **Determine your maximum acceptable commute time.** Measure walking distance from your target department building. Use Google Maps or visit in person. If you plan to bike, add 5–10 minutes.
2. **Set your budget.** Decide on a monthly rent cap (including utilities if possible). Use the indicative bands above, but always confirm current prices with landlords or agents. International students should also budget for the initial deposit and any agency fees.
3. **Identify your room type.** Shared room, private room in shared flat, or studio. Each has trade-offs in privacy, social interaction, and cost.
4. **Shortlist based on location.** Focus on the following neighborhoods (these are typical areas near NCKU, not specific properties):

   - **Front Gate area (Dasyue Road):** Closest to the main library and administrative buildings. High demand, moderate to high rent.
   - **Back Gate area (Shengli Road):** Popular for student restaurants and shops. Many inexpensive rooms are found in older apartment buildings.
   - **East District (around Qingnian Road):** A bit farther but quieter; good for those who prefer a residential feel.
   - **West Central District (near Tainan Train Station):** Convenient for public transport; more urban with night markets nearby.
5. **Check basic amenities.** Does the unit have air conditioning, a washing machine, and internet access? In Tainan’s humid climate, air conditioning is essential during May–October.
6. **Visit or arrange a virtual tour.** Photos can be misleading. Walk through the unit and check for mold, water pressure, and neighbor noise.
7. **Read the lease carefully.** Confirm the start date, notice period, and rules about guests or pets. If you are an international student, ask whether the landlord accepts a non-Taiwanese guarantor.

Applying this sequence will help you narrow down options efficiently. Remember that supply near NCKU is generally good, but September (the start of the academic year) is the busiest period.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is it easy to find accommodation near NCKU?

Yes, there is a steady supply of student housing around the university. Many landlords in the East District are accustomed to renting to students, and you can find rooms listed on local bulletin boards, social media groups, and through the university’s own housing office (which may require a student ID). International students should start searching at least one month before moving to Tainan, especially if arriving in August or September.

### 2. What is the average commute time for students?

Most students live within a 10–20 minute walk or a 5–10 minute bike ride from their classes. Those who prioritize lower rent may commute 20–30 minutes by bus or scooter. The campus is central, so even the farthest affordable neighborhoods are rarely more than 40 minutes away during peak traffic.

### 3. Should I live on campus or off campus?

NCKU offers on-campus dormitories, but they are limited and often allocated to first-year or international students through a lottery. On-campus housing is the most convenient and cheapest option, typically costing NT$1,500–4,000 per month. However, capacity may not meet demand. Off-campus housing provides more privacy, freedom, and variety, but requires a higher budget and more effort to secure. Many students spend their first year in a dorm, then move off campus from the second year onward.
