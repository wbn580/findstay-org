## City Context: Wenshan District and Taipei

![National Chengchi University](https://img.unistay.net/housing/p/amber-2292597.jpg)

NCCU’s address is No. 64, Section 2, Zhinan Rd, Wanxing Village, Wenshan District, Taipei City. Wenshan is a largely residential and green area at the southern edge of Taipei. The campus sits at the foot of hills, near the Taipei Zoo and the Maokong Gondola.

**Transport links**:

- The closest MRT station is Taipei Zoo Station (Wenhu Line, Brown Line). From there, you can take a bus or walk about 15–20 minutes to campus. Alternatively, the MRT Taipei Zoo Station connects directly to central Taipei stations such as Zhongxiao Fuxing (about 25 minutes) and Taipei Main Station (about 35 minutes).
- Several bus routes serve the university, including those running along Zhinan Road and Section 2. Buses run frequently during peak hours.

**Daily life**:

- Grocery stores, convenience stores, and eateries cluster near the campus main entrance and along the streets leading to the MRT. The Wanxing Village area itself is quiet, with small local markets.
- For larger shopping or entertainment, many students travel to the MRT stations around Zhongxiao Dunhua or Xinyi District. That commute is doable but something to factor into your housing choice.

**Climate and environment**: Taipei has humid subtropical weather. Winters are mild and damp, summers hot and rainy. Living closer to the mountains can mean slightly cooler air but also more fog and occasional mosquitoes. Consider a room with good ventilation and possibly air conditioning.

**Safety**: Wenshan is considered a safe district. Street crime is rare, but as with any city, lock your doors and keep valuables secured.

## Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

Your choice between walking and using public transit depends on your tolerance for daily commute time and your preferred lifestyle.

**Walkability**:

- The campus itself is spread along a hillside. Living within a 10–15 minute walk of the main gate on Zhinan Road means you can easily attend classes, visit the library, and use campus facilities without relying on MRT or buses.
- However, the walk from the MRT station to some parts of campus can be uphill. If you find a room near the top of the hill, you might face a steep climb. Check the exact elevation of your potential residence.
- The area immediately around the university (Wanxing Village, Section 2 of Zhinan Road) is residential and relatively quiet, but options for nightlife or supermarkets are limited. Walking to the MRT for shopping adds about 15–20 minutes.

**Transit advantage**:

- Living closer to Taipei Zoo Station (MRT) gives you faster access to central Taipei. If you have internships or jobs in the city, a transit-oriented location may save you 20–30 minutes each way.
- Buses along Zhinan Road run directly to campus from the station, so you don’t have to walk uphill. The trade-off is less immediate proximity to university facilities and a slightly less calm neighbourhood.

**Recommendation**:
If you plan to spend most of your time on campus for classes and library work, prioritise walkable housing within 1 km of the main gate. If you want to regularly explore Taipei or commute to a part-time job, choose a residence near the MRT station (within 500 m of Taipei Zoo Station). For most students, a compromise — living somewhere between the university and the station — provides both convenience and a reasonable walk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can I find housing outside of Taipei for a lower price, or is living near campus essential?

Living near campus is strongly recommended for NCCU because the university is at the edge of Taipei. Commuting from central Taipei (e.g., Da’an or Zhongzheng districts) adds 40–60 minutes each way on the MRT and bus. The cost difference may not offset the time lost, and the quieter environment in Wenshan is part of the experience many reviewers appreciate. If you value peace and easy access to the mountain campus, choose Wenshan. If you have a specific reason to be in the city centre, budget an extra 2–3 hours per day for travel.

### 2. How do I secure a lease as an international student without a Taiwanese guarantor?

Based on the review mentioning strict financial proof, start by preparing a bank statement showing at least six months of living expenses in Taiwan (including rent). Some landlords accept a higher deposit — for example, three months’ rent instead of two months plus a guarantor. Ask the operator clearly: “Do you accept international students without a guarantor? Can I pay an additional deposit instead?” It is also common to ask a Taiwanese friend or your university’s international office to serve as a guarantor. NCCU itself may provide assistance, but confirm directly with the university’s student affairs office.

### 3. What is the typical contract length, and can I extend for the 2026–2027 year?

Most landlords in Wenshan offer one-year contracts, often starting in July or September to align with the academic year. If you arrive in February (spring semester), you may find half-year leases, but these are less common. For the 2026–2027 academic year, aim to sign a contract from September 2026 to August 2027. Always negotiate the move-in date and early termination clauses in writing. Some operators allow renewal with a short notice period. Confirm months in advance — many popular units near NCCU are taken by June.
