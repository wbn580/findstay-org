## Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

NTUA’s Zografou Campus is served by several bus lines (including 230, 608, and 813) that connect to metro stations (Evangelismos, Katechaki) and central Athens. Walking time from the campus to the surrounding residential streets of Zografou is typically 10 to 20 minutes, making it feasible to live without a car if you choose a flat within the same neighborhood. The trade-off is clear:

- **Walking convenience**: Living in Zografou, Kaisariani, or nearby Ano Ilisia means you can walk to campus in 15–25 minutes. This saves time and avoids transport costs, but rental options in the immediate vicinity are limited and can be pricier for private studios.
- **Transit commute**: Living further out (e.g., downtown Athens, Patisia, or Nea Smyrni) will require a bus ride of 20–40 minutes each way. The advantage is a wider selection of affordable flats and more vibrant urban life, but you’ll need to budget for a monthly transport pass (approximately €30 for students, subject to change) and account for potential delays.

For most students, a hybrid approach works well: choose a flat within a 30-minute bus ride from campus to get both affordability and access to city amenities. Walking is only advisable if you are prepared to live in the immediate vicinity of Zografou, where the atmosphere is relaxed and student-friendly.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. How far is NTUA from Athens city centre?**  
The Zografou Campus is about 4 km east of Syntagma Square, the central hub of Athens. By bus, the journey takes approximately 20–30 minutes; by car or taxi, about 15 minutes depending on traffic.

**2. Which neighborhoods are safest and most convenient for NTUA students?**  
Zografou and Kaisariani are the two most popular student enclaves directly adjacent to campus. Both are residential, relatively quiet, and have basic shops, groceries, and cafes. Ano Ilisia is a short walk away and also safe. Avoid isolated streets late at night – standard city caution applies.

**3. What is the typical rental contract term for student housing in Athens?**  
Most private landlords offer 12-month contracts, but some may agree to 9-month terms aligned with the academic year. Always confirm with the operator; deposits typically equal one to two months’ rent. As a rule, start your search at least two months before your course begins.
