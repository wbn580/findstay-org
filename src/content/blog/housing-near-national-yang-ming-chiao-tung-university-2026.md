## Hsinchu City Context and Housing Market

Hsinchu is a technology hub in northern Taiwan, home to the Hsinchu Science Park and a large population of engineers, researchers, and students. The city has a moderate cost of living compared to Taipei, but rental prices near NYCU reflect strong demand from both students and park employees.

**Key neighbourhoods near NYCU:**

- **Guangming Village & East District:** The campus itself is in Guangming Village, surrounded by residential streets, eateries, and convenience stores. Many apartments here are older buildings but offer short walking distances to classes.
- **Near Hsinchu Station (about 6 km away):** The city centre has more shopping, night markets, and transport links. Commute by bus or scooter takes 15–20 minutes.
- **Science Park area:** Some students prefer living closer to the park for internship convenience, but rental prices there can be higher.

**Typical housing types:**

- University dormitories (limited availability, usually for first-year undergraduates or some graduates)
- Private apartments (套房, suites) – most common for singles/couples
- Shared flats (分租) – more economical
- Homestay with local families – less common but available for language immersion

**Lease terms:** Most contracts are for one year, with a deposit of two months’ rent. Utilities (electricity, water, internet) are usually paid separately. For international students, landlords may require a guarantor or a higher deposit unless you use a student housing service.

## Walking vs. Transit Trade-offs

**Walking to campus** – The ideal choice if you value convenience, time savings, and zero commuting cost. NYCU’s main campus is compact; most lecture halls, libraries, and labs are reachable within 10–15 minutes on foot from nearby rental buildings on Daxue Road, Gongyuan Road, or Guangming Village streets. You can easily pop home for lunch, nap, or grab a snack from the canteen. However, apartments very close to campus tend to be priced at the higher end of the budget and can be noisy during semester events.

**Using public transport or scooter** – If you choose to live further out (e.g., near Hsinchu Station or along Zhongzheng Road), you can save on rent (mid-range prices drop NT$1,000–2,000 per month) and enjoy a more spacious flat. Hsinchu City Bus routes 1, 2, and 31 connect the station area to NYCU; a one-way ride costs about NT$15. Scooter rental (around NT$3,000–4,000 per month) gives you flexibility but requires a valid international driving permit and adds fuel/maintenance costs. Commute time expands to 20–30 minutes each way, which over a year amounts to many hours lost.

**Trade-off summary:**

- Walk: higher rent, zero commute, more campus life immersion.
- Ride: lower rent, longer commute, more independence and space.
- Scooter: moderate upfront cost but fast and flexible; useful if you also work in the Science Park.

Many students compromise by living within a 10–15 minute walk while using a bicycle (bike parking is free on campus) to cover the remaining distance.

## How to Shortlist Nearby Housing

When you search for accommodation near NYCU, apply this shortlist logic based on your priorities:

**1. Determine your maximum walking distance.** Use the campus boundary (Daxue Road) as a reference. A 10-minute walk radius covers most of Guangming Village and parts of Gongyuan Road. A 20-minute walk extends to the eastern end of East District.

**2. Match your budget to the band above.** If your allowance is NT$10,000, you can likely find a mid-range suite within 10–15 minutes’ walk. If you need to go lower, you will have to accept a shared room or a location further out.

**3. Check the transportation option.** If you plan to rely on the university shuttle or public bus, verify that the route stops near your apartment. For scooter users, check for free or affordable parking near campus (parking spaces are limited).

**4. Visit the neighbourhood yourself (or via virtual tour).** Walk around during different times of the day. Is the street well lit at night? Are there convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart) within 200 metres? Is there a laundry shop nearby? These small factors affect daily life more than the square footage.

**5. Read recent reviews of the building or landlord on local forums (e.g., Dcard, PTT student boards).** Avoid any landlord with consistent complaints about deposits or maintenance.

**6. Consider roommates.** Splitting a larger flat can cut costs dramatically. Many international students find flatmates through university Facebook groups or the NYCU International Student Office noticeboard.

**7. Confirm contract details.** Check whether utilities are included, what the policy is for early termination, and whether the deposit is returned in full. Some landlords ask for a Taiwanese guarantor; if you cannot provide one, use a reputable student housing platform (the one you are reading this guide on) that acts as intermediary.

**Sample shortlist (not exhaustive – always cross-check):**

- **Within 5 min walk:** Suites on Daxue Road near the main gate. Expect NT$12,000–15,000. Very convenient but higher price.
- **Within 10 min walk:** Flats on Gongyuan Road or Lane 68, Guangming Village. Mid-range NT$9,000–12,000.
- **Within 15 min walk by bike:** Zhonghua Road area. Lower rent NT$7,000–9,000, but you need a bike.
- **Bus commute (~20 min):** Near Hsinchu Station (e.g., Zhongzheng Road). Budget NT$6,000–9,000 for a private suite.

Always request to see actual photos of the room and check for mold, air conditioner condition, and internet speed (important for research work). For the 2026/2027 academic year, start your search at least two months before arrival, because good units go quickly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is it better to live on campus or off campus for a master’s student in 2027?**  
A: It depends on your preference for quiet study and social life. On-campus dormitories are the cheapest option (indicative NT$15,000–25,000 per semester) and put you in the centre of activities. However, many graduate students find dorms noisy and prefer private off-campus suites. Off-campus rental prices for a private suite are around NT$10,000–14,000 per month (indicative). If you value uninterrupted research, off-campus is usually better.

**Q: How can I find a rental that is within walking distance to campus without paying too much?**  
A: The trade-off is space. Look for a slightly older building (not newly renovated) within 10–15 minutes’ walk. Some landlords rent small suites on the upper floors of mixed-use buildings near Guangming Village. Also consider sharing a larger apartment with 1–2 roommates to split rent. Typical shared rooms in that radius cost NT$6,000–8,000 per person (indicative).

**Q: Are utilities included in the rental price in Hsinchu?**  
A: Usually not. Electricity, water, gas, and internet are billed separately, except for some fully managed student residences. Electricity rates can be high in summer due to air conditioning. Ask the landlord for average monthly utility costs before signing. For a single person, budget around NT$1,500–2,500 per month extra (indicative).
