## Housing Near Oregon State University: A 2026 Guide

Located in Corvallis, Oregon, Oregon State University (OSU) is a mid-sized public research university with a campus that consistently earns high marks from visitors and students. Based on Google reviews (4.5 stars, 732 reviews), the campus is described as walkable, well-maintained, and integrated into the city. For students looking for housing near OSU in 2026–2027, understanding the campus environment, local rental market, and transportation options is essential. Below is a factual, data-driven overview to help you compare areas and properties.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

The campus itself sets the tone for where students choose to live. Reviewers highlight the ease of getting around on foot and the pleasant mix of old and new buildings. One reviewer, Stephane Maes, noted:

> “One of the best mid sized campuses in the country! Easy to walk everywhere and very well laid out with a great mix of old and new architecture. Not many public schools where it’s that easy to walk to the football stadium, basketball arena and baseball field from any part of campus.”

This walkability means that living within a 10–15 minute walk of campus can give you direct access to academic buildings, sports facilities, and the downtown core. Another reviewer, Mark Vogel, commented on the grounds:

> “What a beautiful campus. Drove over from spokane to visit my daughter. She gave us the full walking tour of the campus. Grounds were so clean, fun walking through the buildings.”

For online students, Jay Bee shared a positive experience with OSU’s eCampus program, suggesting that even if you are not physically on campus daily, you might still want to be near university facilities for events, libraries, or study spaces.

These reviews indicate that the campus is compact and pedestrian-friendly, which reduces the need for a car if you live nearby. However, housing options vary in distance and price.

### City Context: Corvallis

Corvallis is a college town in western Oregon, with a population of around 60,000. It is largely shaped by Oregon State University, meaning many businesses and services cater to students. The downtown area is adjacent to campus and offers grocery stores, cafes, and restaurants within walking distance.

The rental market in Corvallis is competitive, especially around the start of the fall term. In 2026, the price range for student-oriented apartments near campus begins around **USD 769 per month** for a room in a shared unit (indicative — confirm with the operator). Options include both purpose-built student housing and private rentals. Since Corvallis is not a large city, the overall cost of living is lower than in Portland or Seattle, but rents have been rising in recent years.

### Budget Bands

Based on available listings for 2026–2027, housing near OSU can be grouped into rough budget bands. All prices are indicative and should be confirmed directly with the operator before signing a lease.

- **USD 769 – 900 per month**: Typically a room in a shared apartment or a studio in an older building within 1–2 miles of campus. Examples include some units at **Yugo Corvallis Domain** (formerly known as The Domain) or older private apartments.
- **USD 900 – 1,200 per month**: A standard one-bedroom or a larger shared unit in a newer complex, often closer to campus or with amenities like gym, pool, and study lounges. Properties like **The Vicinity** and **Easton** fall into this range.
- **USD 1,200+ per month**: Premium studios or one-bedroom apartments in the most convenient locations, such as directly across from the university or with high-end finishes.

Prices vary by lease length (12-month vs. month-to-month) and whether utilities are included. Always ask about additional fees for parking, trash, or maintenance.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-off

OSU’s compact campus makes walking a realistic option if you live within about 1 mile. Most academic buildings, the library, and the student union are less than a 20-minute walk from the main residential neighborhoods north and west of campus. Many student housing complexes are located on or near Monroe Avenue, which is a direct pedestrian route to campus.

If you choose housing farther away — say, 2 miles or more — you will need to rely on the Corvallis Transit System (CTS), which is free for OSU students with a valid ID. Buses run on several routes, including the “Beaver Bus” that circles campus and connects to off-campus apartments. However, service frequency can be limited on weekends and evenings. Biking is also popular: the city has bike lanes and relatively flat terrain.

The trade-off is straightforward: walking saves time and money but usually costs more in rent. Transit or biking widens your search area and can lower monthly costs, but adds commute time and requires you to coordinate schedules.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Are the prices mentioned for 2026–2027 guaranteed?**
A: No, all prices listed are indicative and based on early 2026 listings. Rent amounts, availability, and lease terms change frequently. Always confirm the final total cost (including utilities, fees, and deposits) directly with the property operator before signing.

**Q: How do I apply for housing if I am an international student?**
A: Most purpose-built student housing near OSU accepts international students without a U.S. co-signer if you can provide proof of enrollment and pay the deposit. You can usually apply online via the property’s website. Leases may require a guarantor or a deposit equal to 1–2 months’ rent. Contact the property manager for their specific policy.

**Q: Can I live off-campus and still use OSU’s bus system?**
A: Yes, Oregon State University provides a free bus pass (the “Beaver Bus” through CTS) to all enrolled students. This covers all Corvallis Transit System routes. Additionally, many student housing complexes are on shuttle routes operated by the properties themselves. Check with each residence for its own shuttle schedule.
