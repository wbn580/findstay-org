## Housing Near Palacký University in Olomouc: What Campus Reviews Reveal

**Data date: January 2026**

Palacký University in Olomouc (PU) is a public research university ranked 668 in the QS World University Rankings. Located at Křížkovského 511/8 in the historic centre of Olomouc, Czechia, the university holds a Google rating of 4.7 from 316 reviews. Student feedback often highlights its architecture and atmosphere, while also pointing out challenges for international learners — particularly in the medical programmes. Below, we draw on these reviews, city context, and practical logic to help you choose where to live near the university.

### What Campus Reviews Tell Us About Living in Olomouc

The university’s physical environment draws strong praise. Abdullah Arif writes: *“Palacký University has really beautiful buildings. The architecture is very impressive and has a classic, historical feel. Walking around the campus feels special because the buildings are well-designed and full of character.”* Dariusz Prokopowicz describes it as *“the undisputed spiritual and scientific heart of Moravia, where a majestic 16th-century legacy meets a strikingly modern, high-stakes research ambition.”*

However, international medical students should note the experience of reviewer “rashitka”, who gave a 1-star rating: *“AVOID. Not a good place for international med students. I found the teaching and exam methods are outdated, especially in Anatomy and Chemistry. I personally felt limited support from some faculty and the vice dean, and at times noticed unequal treatment toward students with brown skin.”* This suggests that housing decisions may be influenced not only by proximity but also by the need to build a strong support network outside the faculty.

### City Context: Olomouc’s Student-Friendly Character

Olomouc is a medium-sized city (about 100,000 inhabitants) with a large student population — roughly one in seven residents is a university student. The city centre is compact, walkable, and filled with baroque architecture, parks, and affordable cafés. The tram and bus network is efficient, and the main railway station connects to Prague (roughly 2 hours) and other Czech cities.

PU’s main campus is spread across several historic buildings in the old town, while the Faculty of Medicine and Dentistry is located near the Olomouc University Hospital, about 2 km north of the centre. Knowing which faculty you belong to is essential for choosing the right neighbourhood.

### Budget Bands for Housing

Rents in Olomouc are generally lower than in Prague or Brno, but prices vary significantly by housing type and location. All figures below are **indicative — confirm with the operator**.

- **Low budget** – Shared rooms or dormitory places in university-managed halls. These are typically the most affordable option, often located within walking distance or a short tram ride from campus. Expect basic furnishings and shared kitchens/bathrooms.
- **Mid-range** – Private studio flats or shared apartments (2–3 people) within a 20-minute walk or 15-minute tram ride from the university. These offer more privacy and often include a kitchenette and private bathroom.
- **Higher budget** – One-bedroom or larger apartments in the historic centre or near the hospital. Suitable for couples, families, or students who prioritise space and quiet.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

Because PU’s faculties are not all in one location, the choice between walking and using public transport depends on your faculty.

- **Centre-based faculties** (Arts, Science, Law, Education) – most teaching takes place in buildings around the old town. Walking from anywhere in the central district to a classroom usually takes 10–20 minutes. A flat near the main square or the university’s Křížkovského address is ideal.
- **Medical and health faculties** – located near the hospital on Hněvotínská street, about 2 km from the centre. Walking from the old town takes 25–30 minutes. Many students use trams (lines 1, 4, 6) or buses (lines 11, 20) from the centre to the hospital stop; the ride is about 10 minutes. Living along those transit lines or in the Nová Ulice neighbourhood can save time.

### Shortlist Logic: How to Choose a Residence

When evaluating housing near Palacký University, consider the following criteria in order:

1. **Faculty location** – First, identify which campus buildings you will use most. If you are in the medical programme, favour the area around the hospital. For other programmes, the old town is your hub.
2. **Walkability vs. transit** – If you prefer walking, limit your search to a roughly 1 km radius from your main faculty. If you are fine with a short tram ride, expand to neighbourhoods such as Neředín, Povel, or Lazce.
3. **Budget** – Determine whether you can afford a private studio or need a shared room. University dormitories (e.g., halls on třída 17. listopadu or in the Povel area) are often the cheapest but may have limited availability.
4. **Reviews and condition** – Visit the flat in person or request a video tour. Check for heating adequacy (winters are cold) and internet reliability. Read reviews of the building management or landlord if available on student forums.
5. **Contract flexibility** – Many landlords require a one-year lease, but some offer 10-month contracts aligned with the academic year. Clarify early departure policies.

**Q: Is it better to live in the city centre or near the hospital for medical students?**

Living near the hospital means a quieter neighbourhood with shorter commutes to classes and the hospital (useful for clinical placements). Some students split the difference by choosing an apartment along tram lines connecting the two areas.
