## Housing near Savitribai Phule Pune University: A Practical Guide

Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) sits on a large, green campus in the Ganeshkhind area of Pune, Maharashtra. With a QS World University ranking of 566 and a Google rating of 4.1 from over 2,300 reviews, the university attracts both domestic and international students. Finding suitable accommodation is a common concern, especially for out-of-state and international students. This guide uses real student reviews to highlight what to expect, combined with practical advice on budget, commuting, and how to shortlist housing options.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Student feedback offers a realistic picture of life at SPPU, including the housing situation.

One reviewer, Shubham Shinde, gave a 5-star rating and noted: **“Savitribai Phule Pune University has one of the most beautiful campuses in Pune. The historic main building is stunning, especially in the evening when the lights turn on and the whole place looks very peaceful. Walking around the campus feels refreshing because of the greenery and well-maintained g…”** The emphasis on greenery and a peaceful atmosphere is echoed by many. SUPRIYA GODASE, also a 5-star reviewer, called it **“one of the most prestigious universities in India, known for its vast campus, rich heritage, and academic excellence. The greenery and peaceful environment create an ideal atmosphere for study and research.”** For students who value a calm, study-friendly environment, the campus itself is a major asset.

However, housing difficulties are a real concern, particularly for students from outside Maharashtra. Ashma Akter gave a 4-star rating but pointed out: **“Good environment large campus all round quite good but the students from non-maharashtra didn’t get hostel easily infact we completed our 1 year of course but still didn’t get it …but otherwise staffs behaviour was good.”** This review highlights that university hostel accommodation is limited and not guaranteed, even after a year of study. Students should not rely solely on on-campus housing.

Abhijeet Sonawane added: **“It is popularly known as the ‘Oxford of the East’. It has about 307 recognized research institutes and 612 affiliated colleges offering graduate and under-graduate courses. The university attracts many foreign students due to its excellent facilities. It offers good accommodation facility. The main …”** While he mentions “good accommodation facility,” the context suggests the availability may not meet demand, as Ashma Akter’s experience shows.

**Key takeaway from reviews:** The campus is beautiful and conducive to study, but university-managed housing is insufficient for all students. Most will need to find private accommodation nearby.

### City Context: Pune as a Student Hub

Pune is one of India’s most important education and IT cities. The presence of numerous colleges, research institutes, and technology parks means a large student and young professional population. The university is located in the Aundh area, close to the Pune-Mumbai highway and relatively near the Hinjawadi IT hub. This location offers both academic and employment opportunities.

The city has a well-developed public transport system including buses (PMPML), auto-rickshaws, and ride-hailing services. The Pune Metro is expanding, with the Aundh area served by existing and planned stations. The nearest railway station is Pune Junction (about 7–8 km), and the airport is about 12–13 km away.

The student community in Pune is diverse, with many people from across India and other countries. This means a wide range of cuisines, cultural events, and lifestyle options. However, the cost of living has risen in recent years, especially in popular areas near IT parks and universities.

### Budget Bands

Exact prices vary greatly based on property type, location, facilities, and season. All figures below are **indicative — confirm with the operator**. For the academic years 2026 or 2027, you can generally expect the following ranges:

- **Budget (shared accommodation / PG accommodation)**  
  Shared rooms in paying guest (PG) accommodations or student hostels near the campus often start from around INR 6,000 to INR 10,000 per person per month. These typically include basic furniture, meals (sometimes), and Wi-Fi. Shared bathrooms and limited privacy are common.
- **Mid-range (private room in a shared apartment / studio PG)**  
  A private bedroom in a shared apartment or a higher-quality PG with attached bathroom may cost INR 12,000 to INR 18,000 per month. These usually offer better privacy, possibly a study desk, and access to a shared kitchen or common area.
- **Premium (1 BHK or 2 BHK independent apartment)**  
  Entire apartments – one-bedroom or two-bedroom – in Aundh, Baner, or nearby localities typically range from INR 20,000 to INR 35,000 per month for a 1 BHK, and INR 30,000 to INR 50,000 for a 2 BHK, depending on furnishing, amenities, and proximity to the university.

Many students split a 2 BHK apartment between 2–3 people to bring per-person costs closer to the mid-range bracket.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-off

The university campus itself is large, and some academic buildings, libraries, and canteens are within walking distance of each other. However, most private housing options are not directly on campus. Here is the trade-off:

- **Walking distance (within 1–2 km)**  
  Properties in Ganeshkhind itself or the immediate Aundh area (near the main gate) allow you to walk to the university. This saves commute time and money. Rentals in this zone are in higher demand and may be pricier. The benefit: you can attend early classes easily, use campus facilities in the evening, and avoid traffic.

![Savitribai Phule Pune University](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-1297181.jpg)

- **Short transit (3–6 km, 10–20 minutes by auto/bus)**  
  Areas like Baner, Pashan, and parts of Hinjawadi are within a quick auto-rickshaw or bus ride. Rent here may be slightly lower, but you spend time and money on commuting. Buses are affordable (INR 10–25 per trip), while auto-rickshaws may cost INR 50–150 depending on distance.
- **Longer commute (7–15 km, 30–60 minutes)**  
  Cheaper housing exists farther out (e.g., Wakad, Kharadi, or along the Mumbai highway). However, Pune traffic can be heavy during peak hours, making a short distance into a long journey. This option is not recommended unless you have a private vehicle or are willing to rely on crowded buses.

**Recommendation:** For students who value time and convenience, living within a 3 km radius of the university is ideal. If budget is tight, consider a shared apartment at the mid-range level closer to campus rather than a cheaper option far away.

### Shortlist Logic: How to Choose a Residence

Given that specific nearby residential properties are not listed here, this logic applies to any housing search. Use these criteria to evaluate options:

1. **Proximity to university (walking or short auto ride)**  
   First, filter by distance. Use Google Maps or local platforms to check the travel time to SPPU main gate. A walkable location (≤1.5 km) or a 10-minute auto ride is preferred by most students.
2. **Budget alignment**  
   Decide on a per-person monthly rent (including utilities if possible). Match with the budget bands above. If the cost seems too low, ask about hidden charges (maintenance, electricity, cooking gas deposits).
3. **Safety and security**  
   Verify that the building has functional locks on main doors, good lighting at night, nearby shops and people around. Female students especially should check if the area feels safe after dark.
4. **Tenure and deposit**  
   Most landlords ask for a refundable deposit of 2–6 months’ rent. Check the lease terms – some require a 11-month minimum. Ensure you can break the lease early (with notice) if needed.
5. **Amenities and restrictions**  
   Ask about Wi-Fi (speed and monthly cost), meals (if it’s a PG), guest policy (whether friends can visit), curfew timings, and cooking permissions. Some PGs do not allow non-vegetarian food or require early entry.
6. **Roommates and community**  
   If you are sharing, look for flatmates with similar schedules and habits. Some student housing operators offer matched roommate services.

By systematically applying these filters, you can narrow down a manageable shortlist. Visit the property in person or do a video tour if possible. Ask current tenants about their experience – especially regarding landlord responsiveness and water/electricity reliability.

### FAQs

**1. How can I apply for on-campus hostel accommodation?**  
University hostels are limited. Visit the official SPPU website (not linked here) for hostel application procedures and deadlines. Contact the university accommodation office directly. Based on reviews, expect high demand; have a backup plan with private housing.

**2. What is the best area to live in for a first-year student?**  
Aundh and Ganeshkhind are the most convenient due to walking distance. Baner (2–3 km) is also popular with students and has many PGs and apartments. Both areas have grocery stores, eateries, and good bus connectivity. For lower rent, consider Pashan but verify the commute.

**3. How much does it cost to commute daily?**  
If you live within 3 km, an auto-rickshaw trip one-way may cost INR 40–80. PMPML bus fare is around INR 10–30. A monthly bus pass is about INR 800–1,500. Owning a bicycle is a good option for distances under 5 km, though Pune has some hilly areas.
