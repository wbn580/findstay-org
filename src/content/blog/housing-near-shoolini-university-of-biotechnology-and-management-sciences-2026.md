## Campus Life and What Reviews Reveal

Shoolini University of Biotechnology and Management Sciences, located on the Solan-Oachghat-Kumarhatti Highway in Bajhol, Himachal Pradesh, holds a QS World University Ranking of 503 and a Google rating of 4.2 based on 1,679 reviews. Student feedback consistently points to a supportive academic environment set against a backdrop of hills and greenery. A second-year B.Sc. Psychology student, Ayushi Kumari, describes her experience: “The campus is peaceful, beautiful, and equipped with good facilities that create a great learning environment. The faculty members are supportive, approachable, and always ready to guide students.” Another student, Shivani Singh, notes that the university is “known for its research-driven education” and that “the campus is surrounded by beautiful hills, providing a peaceful and motivating learning environment.”

Vani Nag, a second-year CSE student, adds: “I have had a really lovely time here so far. It has been an overall wonderful journey with a great environment, good teachers and lot of opportunities for personal development apart from academics.” Riya Thakur emphasises the broader culture: “Faculty members are approachable and always ready to help students academically and personally. The university promotes research, entrepreneurship, and innovation through various initiatives and competitions.” These quotes paint a picture of a place where academics and personal growth go hand in hand, which often shapes where students choose to live.

## Understanding the Location: Solan and Bajhol

The university sits in Bajhol, a locality on the outskirts of Solan city in Himachal Pradesh. Solan is known as the “Mushroom City of India” and sits at an altitude of about 1,350 metres, offering a cool climate year-round. The campus is roughly 8–10 kilometres from Solan’s main bus stand and railway station. Many students coming from outside the state first arrive in Solan and then take a shared taxi or bus to reach the university. The surrounding area is semi-urban, with a mix of agricultural land, small shops, and residential colonies.

Given the hilly terrain, distances that appear short on a map can take longer to cover due to winding roads. Most daily essentials can be found within walking distance of the campus gate, but for more extensive shopping, banking, or entertainment, students often head to Solan town. This context matters when choosing accommodation: proximity to campus often means less time commuting, but living closer to Solan town offers better access to services and transport links.

## Housing Options: On-Campus vs Off-Campus

![Shoolini University of Biotechnology and Management Sciences](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/uh-s01518022.jpg)

Shoolini University provides on-campus hostel accommodation for both boys and girls. Hostels are typically the first choice for first-year students because they offer convenience, meals, and a built-in social network. University-run hostels are usually located within walking distance of academic buildings, which aligns with the peaceful, study-oriented environment described in reviews.

Off-campus options include private hostels, paying guest (PG) accommodations, and rental apartments in nearby villages such as Bajhol itself, or in the Solan suburbs like Oachghat and Kumarhatti. Off-campus housing gives students more independence, often cheaper rent for shared rooms, and the possibility of cooking their own meals. However, distances can be a factor – some PGs are a 10–15 minute walk from the campus, while others may be a 20–30 minute bus ride away. It is important to inspect the property, check for reliable water and electricity supply (common issues in hill areas), and confirm the safety of the neighbourhood, especially for female students.

## Budget Considerations

Housing costs near Shoolini University vary depending on room type, location, and amenities. On-campus hostel fees are set by the university and usually include rent, meals, and basic utilities – these are announced annually and are indicative — confirm with the operator. Off-campus PGs and rented rooms typically fall into a range that depends on whether the room is single or shared, whether it is furnished, and whether meals are provided. As a rule of thumb, living further from campus tends to be more affordable, but commuting costs and time must be factored in. Always ask about deposits, electricity charges (often billed separately), and any maintenance fees before signing a contract. Prices in the area have been relatively stable, but it is wise to confirm current rates directly with the landlord or operator, as they can change between semesters.

## Walk vs Transit: Choosing Your Commute

Because the campus is located on a highway, walking from accommodation in Bajhol village is feasible if you are within a 1–2 kilometre radius. Many PGs and hostels in Bajhol are reachable on foot in 10–20 minutes. Walking is the most economical option and allows you to enjoy the fresh air and mountain views.

For students living farther out – for example, in Solan town or Oachghat – public transport is necessary. Shared minibuses and “tempos” (three-wheelers) ply the route between Solan bus stand and the university throughout the day. The ride takes about 20–30 minutes during non-peak hours. A few students also use private bicycles or scooters, though the hilly roads require caution. Relying on transit means you need to factor in schedule flexibility; classes may start early in the morning, and buses become less frequent after 8 p.m. If you plan to spend time on campus for extracurricular activities or study groups, living within walking distance gives you more freedom.

## How to Shortlist Nearby Residences

When evaluating housing near Shoolini University, start with a clear checklist based on what matters most to you:

- **Distance to campus**: If you value time and convenience, prioritise accommodation in Bajhol or within a 15-minute walk. Check Google Maps walking time – but also physically walk the route, as hills can make the actual effort greater than the distance suggests.
- **Safety and security**: Look for well-lit streets, secure door locks, and preferably a neighbourhood with other students. Female students should ask about CCTV or guard presence, especially in PG accommodations.
- **Amenities included**: Confirm whether rent covers water, electricity (or a fixed monthly limit), internet, and meals. Many PGs in the area offer Wi-Fi, but speed and reliability vary.
- **Contract flexibility**: Some landlords require a full-semester commitment, while others allow monthly payments. Read the fine print regarding notice periods and deposit refunds.
- **Noise and privacy**: The campus itself is quiet, but roadside properties on the highway can be noisy. If you need quiet for studying, choose a room at the back of the building.
- **Reviews from current students**: Ask seniors or check student forums (not provided here, but local word-of-mouth is valuable). The university administration may also maintain a list of verified off-campus accommodations.

Compile a shortlist of three to five options that check most of your boxes. Visit each in person during a weekday to see the actual environment and talk to current tenants. Because the housing market here is relatively small, good options can be snapped up within a week of being advertised, so act quickly.
