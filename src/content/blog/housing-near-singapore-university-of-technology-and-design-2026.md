## The Trade-off Between Walking and Public Transport

The SUTD campus is at 8 Somapah Rd. The nearest bus stop is about a 5-minute walk, from which buses can reach Upper Changi MRT station (East West Line) or Expo station (East West Line and Circle Line). Residential projects within walking distance are very limited (mainly the “SUTD Hostel” near SUTD and some private condominiums), so most students need to rely on public transport.

- **Walking (0–1 km)**: Almost no regular rental listings. The area around campus is mostly industrial or undeveloped land. A few private condominiums like “The Glades” or “Casa Clementi” are about 2–3 km away, requiring a walk of over 30 minutes, which is impractical.
- **Bus + MRT (mainstream choice)**: From the city center (e.g., Orchard Road, Bugis) to SUTD by bus or MRT, a one-way trip takes about 40–50 minutes; from the east (Bedok, Tampines) about 20–30 minutes. Bus routes such as 2, 24, 31, 35, and 38 can reach near the campus directly.
- **Driving/taxi**: From the city center to the school takes about 20–30 minutes, but the Certificate of Entitlement is expensive in Singapore, so most students choose public transport.

Therefore, commute time is a core filtering factor. If frequent commuting is needed, it is advisable to prioritize listings in the eastern or northeastern areas, such as Changi, Bedok, Simei, and Tampines. Rents in these areas are slightly lower than the city center, and commute times are manageable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How long is the approximate commute from these listings to SUTD?

Most listings are in eastern or central Singapore, requiring multiple bus or MRT transfers to reach SUTD. Typical commute times are as follows:

- Eastern listings (e.g., Bedok, Changi): 20–30 minutes
- Central listings (e.g., Lavender, Orchard Road): 40–50 minutes
- Northeastern listings (e.g., Tampines, Hougang): 30–40 minutes  
  All times are peak-hour estimates; it is advisable to test the route in advance.

### 2. What fees are usually included in the rent? What extra payments are needed?

Rent generally does not include utilities (water, electricity, gas) and internet fees, though some apartments or hostels may include them. A refundable security deposit equivalent to one month’s rent is usually required. Additionally, the lease may specify air-conditioner cleaning fees, property management fees, or furniture damage costs. Everything is subject to the contract.

### 3. How can I verify the authenticity and reliability of a listing?

When contacting the operator, you can request to see a lease template, real photos or videos of the property, and verify the other party’s identity (such as a property license). In Singapore, private rentals must comply with the Residential Tenancies Act. It is recommended to arrange viewings through a licensed agent or student accommodation platform and avoid paying large deposits in advance. Do not remit any money before viewing the property in person.
