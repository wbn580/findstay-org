### City Context: Nanjing and the Jiangning District

Nanjing, the capital of Jiangsu Province, is a historic city with a population of over 8 million. It is a major educational hub in eastern China, home to several universities. The Jiangning District lies south of the city centre and has become a fast-growing residential and industrial zone. The metro Line 1 (the southern extension) and Line 3 pass through the area, with stations like “Southeast University Chengxian Campus” (on the older city-centre campus) and “NJUST” or “Shengtai Road” near the main Jiangning campus. However, the main SEU campus in Jiangning is about 20 km from the city centre, so commuting to downtown takes approximately 40–60 minutes by metro.

Within Jiangning, the area immediately surrounding SEU includes a mix of:

- **Student apartments**: Purpose-built dormitories run by the university or private operators, often offering shared rooms.
- **Residential compounds (xiaoqu)**: Gated communities with studios to three-bedroom flats, popular among graduate students and faculty.
- **Village housing**: Older multi-storey buildings near the south gate, usually cheaper but less regulated.

The local economy is driven by manufacturing, logistics, and a growing tech park. For students, daily needs (groceries, restaurants, clinics) are well covered within walking distance of the campus.

### Budget Bands (Indicative — Confirm with the Operator)

Housing costs in Jiangning are generally lower than in Nanjing’s city centre but have risen in recent years. The following bands are based on typical market rates in 2025-2026 (all figures are indicative and may change; always confirm with the operator or landlord):

- **Budget (shared room in student apartment or older community)**: Approximately 800–1,500 CNY per month per person. These are usually rooms in a 2–4 person dormitory-style flat with shared bathroom and kitchen. Best for students who prioritise low cost and don’t mind less privacy.
- **Mid-range (private studio or 1-bedroom in a newer compound)**: Approximately 2,500–4,000 CNY per month for a self-contained unit. These flats often have a separate bathroom, kitchenette, and basic furniture. Suitable for single students or couples.
- **Upper-range (2-bedroom or larger apartment for sharing)**: Approximately 3,500–5,500 CNY per month for a whole flat. Splitting between two or three roommates brings the per-person cost down to 1,200–2,000 CNY, which is a common arrangement among graduates.

Note that utility costs (electricity, water, internet, gas) are usually not included and can add 300–600 CNY per month depending on usage. Some operators also charge a management fee. Always clarify what is included in the listed rent.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

Choosing a place based on walking distance to campus versus reliance on public transit depends on your schedule, budget, and tolerance for commuting.

**Walking zone (0–15 minutes on foot)**:

- Properties within 1 km of the campus gates are convenient for early classes, library access, and social activities. They are often slightly more expensive due to demand.
- The downside: Older buildings close to the campus may lack modern insulation, reliable air conditioning, or security. Noise from student life can be a factor.
- Typical options: Dormitories inside the campus (if available to international postgraduates) or small flats in the “students’ village” just south of the east gate.

3. **Inspect the property in person or via a trusted friend**: Online photos can be misleading. Pay attention to water pressure, air conditioning/heating condition, and cleanliness of shared areas.

5. **Consider roommate matching services**: If you plan to share, use university bulletin boards or trusted social media groups (e.g., WeChat groups for SEU students) rather than unknown platforms.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Can I live on campus as an international student?**  
On-campus dormitories are available for degree-seeking international students, but numbers are limited. Allocation is usually based on application order and programme type. Contact the SEU International Students Office directly (via the university website) for the latest policies. Off-campus housing is a common alternative for postgraduates and short-term exchange students.

**Q2: What is the typical rental contract duration?**  
Most private landlords in Jiangning ask for a one-year lease, paid quarterly or up-front. Some will accept a six-month lease but may charge a slightly higher monthly rate. Short-term Airbnb-style stays are possible but can cost 30–50% more. Always negotiate the deposit (usually one month’s rent) and get a receipt.

**Q3: How safe is the neighbourhood around Southeast University?**  
Jiangning is generally safe, with a visible police presence and well-lit streets. Petty theft (like leaving a bicycle unlocked) can happen, but violent crime is rare. Stick to main roads after dark, and avoid renting from unregistered agents. Use a reputable real estate platform (e.g., Beike, Lianjia) with verified listings.
