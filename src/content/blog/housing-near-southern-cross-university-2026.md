## City Context: Lismore

Southern Cross University’s main campus is at Military Road, East Lismore, New South Wales. Lismore is a regional city in the Northern Rivers region, approximately 800 km north of Sydney and 200 km south of Brisbane. The city has a population of around 30,000 and serves as a commercial and educational hub for the area.

Lismore has experienced significant flooding events in recent years, particularly in 2022 and 2024. Many residential areas and university facilities were affected. When searching for accommodation, it is advisable to check flood risk maps and discuss history with property managers. The university itself has invested in recovery, but the broader community is still adapting.

The local rental market is relatively small. For the 2026 academic year, the indicative starting weekly rent for student-oriented properties is AUD 235. This price is based on current listings and should be confirmed directly with the operator. Most purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA) is concentrated near the campus or in the central Lismore area.

## Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

The SCU Lismore campus is located in East Lismore, about 2 km east of the city centre. Walking from the city centre to campus takes roughly 25 minutes. From the residential areas in central Lismore, a walk of 15–20 minutes is typical. Many student residences are purpose-built within a 10-minute walk of the main lecture buildings.

Public transport in Lismore consists primarily of buses. The Lismore City Bus network runs routes connecting the campus to the CBD and outer suburbs. However, services are infrequent, especially on weekends and evenings. A car or bicycle can be more convenient if you plan to explore the region. Bicycle infrastructure is limited but improving; many students cycle between campus and nearby accommodation.

If you live in one of the UniLodge properties, you are likely within walking distance of the university library, food outlets, and student services. The trade-off: if you prefer a quieter environment or a larger apartment, you may need to live farther out and rely on a car. Flood risk is also a factor – properties closer to the river or in low-lying areas may carry higher insurance costs and greater disruption during heavy rain.
