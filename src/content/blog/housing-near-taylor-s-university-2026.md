## Housing Near Taylor’s University: Campus Reviews and Neighbourhood Guide (2026)

Taylor’s University is located at 1, Jln Taylors, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor, Malaysia. The campus holds a Google rating of 4.1 from 1,192 reviews, reflecting mixed but generally positive feedback from students and visitors. Understanding what current reviewers say about the campus and its surroundings can help you decide where to live.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Several reviewers highlight the campus environment. Five-star reviewer Mandy Kok wrote: “Taylor’s University has one of the most beautiful and sustainable campuses in Malaysia. The greenery and the lakeside environment make it a perfect place to unwind especially taking a walk around the lake at night to relax after a long day.” Another five-star reviewer, asishkumar sahoo, noted: “Attending Taylor’s University Open Day was a truly worthwhile experience. The moment I arrived at the Lakeside Campus, the atmosphere felt warm, organised, and genuinely student-friendly.”

A one-star review from N AR points to an operational issue: “It’s amazing what the priorities are. It a Sunday. There is no valet and the parking lots are empty. It’s raining and the security guard first refused and then reluctantly removed the cone so the student could get in without getting wet.” This suggests that while the campus itself is appealing, logistical inconsistencies can occur, which may influence your choice of housing – especially if you plan to drive or rely on valet services.

Another five-star reviewer, CHIT SNOW, chose Taylor’s University “because of its strong reputation, especially in the field of Education.” The reviewer also mentioned the Bachelor of Education (Honours) with a minor in Psychology, noting that “not many universities offer such a solid education programme.”

Overall, the reviews paint a picture of a pleasant campus with green spaces and a lakeside setting, while also hinting that parking and security can be unpredictable. When looking for housing, you may want to prioritise locations that allow you to walk or take reliable transit, minimising reliance on campus parking.

### City Context: Subang Jaya

Subang Jaya is a major suburban city in Selangor, located about 20 kilometres west of Kuala Lumpur city centre. It is well connected by the Kelana Jaya LRT line (putra LRT) and the MRT Kajang line, with several stations serving the area. The city has a large student population due to the presence of Taylor’s University and other educational institutions. Shopping malls, supermarkets, restaurants, and healthcare facilities are abundant, especially around the USJ and SS15 areas.

For students, Subang Jaya offers a convenient blend of urban amenities and suburban quietness. The area around the Lakeside Campus is relatively green, with residential condominiums, apartments, and landed houses within walking distance. Public transport links to the campus include buses and ride-hailing services, though some students prefer private vehicles.

### Budget Bands for Student Housing

Accommodation options near Taylor’s University can be grouped into three broad budget bands. All prices listed below are **indicative – confirm with the operator** before making any commitment.

- **Economy (shared living):** Rooms in shared apartments or budget hostels typically cost less per person. This option suits students who prioritise low rent and are comfortable with common facilities. Locations further from campus (requiring a bus or LRT ride) tend to be more affordable. Expect shared bathrooms and a basic kitchen.
- **Mid‑range (private studio/ensuite):** Studio apartments or ensuite rooms in purpose‑built student accommodation or condominiums. These offer more privacy and often include a small kitchenette, private bathroom, and utilities. Prices are moderate, and many are within a 15‑to‑20‑minute walk or a short bus ride from the campus.
- **Premium (serviced apartment/condo):** High‑end serviced apartments or luxury condos with facilities such as swimming pools, gyms, and 24‑hour security. These are ideal for students who want a quiet, comfortable living environment with minimal commuting. Premium options are usually close to the campus or in prime areas like USJ.

Because rental rates fluctuate based on lease duration, season, and furnishing, always check with the property manager for current rates. The presence of many mid‑range options makes Subang Jaya a balanced market for student housing.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade‑Off

The choice between walking and using public transport depends on your budget, schedule, and tolerance for walking in tropical weather.

**Walking distance:** Properties within a 10‑to‑15‑minute walk from the Lakeside Campus are in high demand. These are mainly in the immediate vicinity of Jln Taylors, such as condominiums along Persiaran Taylor’s. Walking saves time and money, but the heat and occasional rain can be uncomfortable. If you choose walking, look for sheltered pathways or shaded routes.

**Transit:** If you live further away (e.g., SS15, USJ, or Bandar Sunway), the LRT and MRT lines can get you to the campus area. The closest LRT station to Taylor’s is **Taipan** (on the Kelana Jaya line), which is about 2.5 km from campus. From there, you can take a feeder bus or ride‑hailing service for the last mile. Buses operated by RapidKL and local shuttle services also serve the campus. Transit commutes typically take 20–45 minutes one‑way, depending on the distance and waiting time. This trade‑off can reduce rent but adds daily travel costs and schedule planning.

### Shortlist Logic Using Nearby Residences

When shortlisting housing options near Taylor’s University, use the following criteria:

1. **Distance to campus gate:** Measure walking time from the property to the main entrance (not just Google Maps distance). Use the Lakeside Campus address provided above as your destination.
2. **Safety and security:** Look for gated communities, 24‑hour security, well‑lit streets, and reliable locks. Check Google reviews for the residence (if available) and ask current students about the neighbourhood.
3. **Utilities and furniture:** Many mid‑range and premium units are fully furnished. Decide if you need a furnished unit or if you can bring your own. Electricity and water bills may or may not be included in the rent.
4. **Public transport access:** If you rely on transit, check the nearest bus stop or LRT station and the frequency of services. The Kelana Jaya LRT line connects Subang Jaya to KL city centre, which is useful for weekend trips.
5. **Grocery and dining:** Proximity to a supermarket, 24‑hour convenience store, and eateries is important for daily life. Areas like SS15 and USJ have many food courts and minimarkets.
6. **Lease terms:** Short‑term leases (3–6 months) are common for student housing, but some landlords require a 12‑month commitment. Clarify the deposit, notice period, and any penalties for early termination.
7. **Roommate matching:** If you plan to share, consider using university noticeboards or verified housing platforms. Avoid sharing with strangers without a written agreement covering bills, cleaning, and quiet hours.

**Example shortlist process (generic):**

- If your budget is economy, prioritise shared apartments near LRT stations (e.g., Taipan or USJ stations) for lower rent and good transit connection.
- If your budget is mid‑range, shortlist condos within a 1‑km radius of campus. Verify that the walking route is safe and shaded.
- If your budget is premium, look at serviced apartments in the USJ or Bandar Sunway area, which offer high standards but require a short drive or bus ride.

Remember that no single residence suits everyone – your choice depends on your priorities for cost, convenience, and comfort.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How far is Taylor’s University from Kuala Lumpur city centre?**  
The Lakeside Campus is approximately 20 km from KL city centre. By car, the journey takes about 25–40 minutes depending on traffic. Public transport via LRT and bus takes around 60–90 minutes. Many students prefer to live in Subang Jaya itself rather than commute from KL.

**Is it safe to walk to campus from nearby condos at night?**  
Most residential areas immediately around the campus are generally safe, with security guards at condo entrances and well‑lit streets. However, reviews mention occasional issues with parking and security protocols. It is advisable to walk in groups or use a ride‑hailing app at night, especially if you are unfamiliar with the area. Check with current students about the specific routes you plan to use.

**What utilities and amenities are typically included in student housing?**  
In the Subang Jaya market, many mid‑range and premium units include water and electricity in the rent, but always confirm. Broadband internet is usually not included; you will need to arrange your own plan. Some purpose‑built student accommodations offer all‑inclusive packages covering utilities, internet, and even weekly cleaning. Read the lease carefully to avoid unexpected bills.
