## Housing Near The Education University of Hong Kong: What You Need to Know

The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK), ranked 530th in the QS World University Rankings, is located at 10 Lo Ping Road in Ting Kok, Tai Po. While the campus offers a tranquil, green setting with mountain views, its distance from Hong Kong’s main urban centres means that choosing the right accommodation demands careful thought. This guide distils what current students say about the commute, breaks down budget expectations, and provides a clear shortlisting logic so you can weigh your options for 2026 or 2027.

### What Campus Reviews Reveal

Student reviews on Google (overall rating 4.3 out of 5, with 146 reviews) paint a consistent picture: EdUHK’s location is both its greatest asset and its main drawback.

> “I need around 1.5 hours to commute from the city centre by public transport. A comfy campus with natural scenery surrounded this area. It offers shuttle bus from and to University station.” – Nandyan Ayu, 4 stars, 11 months ago

This confirms that living far from campus, especially in Kowloon or Hong Kong Island, will cost you significant time. The shuttle bus mentioned runs between the University MTR station (East Rail Line) and the campus, bridging the last leg of the journey. If you prefer a shorter total commute, living in Tai Po or along the East Rail corridor makes sense.

> “Located in the Tai Po, EdUHK is one of the local UGC funded institutions that is reputed for training future teachers for nursery, primary and secondary schools in Hong Kong. It has a spacious and green campus and overlooks a breathtaking mountain views, providing students with open space and tranqu” – Ivan Chui (ivchui7), 4 stars, a year ago

The “spacious and green campus” is a recurring theme. Those who value a quiet, nature-oriented environment often find the location rewarding. However, the same remoteness means fewer dining and entertainment options within walking distance compared to city-centre campuses.

> “Famous university with tremendous scenery, exceptonal academic papers made by world class professionals is uprising into world class standard.” – Simon PK CHOW, 5 stars, 3 years ago

While the academic praise is notable, the key takeaway for housing is that the scenery and tranquillity come with a trade-off in accessibility. Your decision on where to live will directly affect how much you enjoy that scenery versus how long you spend commuting.

### City Context: Tai Po and Beyond

EdUHK sits in the Tai Po district, on the northeastern coast of the New Territories. The nearest MTR station is **Tai Po Market** (East Rail Line), about a 15-minute bus or minibus ride from campus – or you can take the school’s shuttle bus from **University Station** (one stop north of Tai Po Market). The area is primarily residential, with local markets, shops, and eateries concentrated around Tai Po Centre and Tai Po Plaza.

Living in Tai Po itself gives you a short commute to campus and access to basic amenities. For more nightlife, shopping, and dining variety, students often look toward Sha Tin (20–30 minutes by MTR) or Kowloon Tong (35–40 minutes). However, as the review noted, a full journey from Central Hong Kong can take 1.5 hours each way.

### Budget Bands (Indicative – Confirm with the Operator)

Rental costs in Hong Kong vary dramatically by location and room type. Below are approximate 2026/2027 bands for student accommodation near EdUHK. All figures are indicative – always verify with the landlord or operator before committing.

- **Shared room in Tai Po (walk-up apartment)**: HK$3,500 – HK$5,000 per month. Typically a bed space in a 2–3 bedroom flat. Lowest cost, but shared facilities and often older buildings.
- **Private room in Tai Po or Tai Wo (private flat)**: HK$5,000 – HK$8,000 per month. You get your own room in a shared flat. More privacy, but still communal living.
- **Studio or small apartment in Tai Po Market area**: HK$8,000 – HK$12,000 per month. Self-contained units, usually in newer buildings. Suitable for couples or those who prefer solitude.
- **Room in Sha Tin or Kowloon Tong**: HK$6,000 – HK$10,000 per month. Longer commute but closer to central Hong Kong for internships or social life.
- **On-campus or off-campus purpose-built student housing**: EdUHK operates limited on-campus halls, but they are usually allocated to first-year and exchange students. Off-campus student apartments (e.g., in Tai Po Mega Mall area) may charge HK$6,000 – HK$9,000 per month for a single room. All prices are indicative – confirm with the operator.

### Walk vs. Transit Trade-off

EdUHK’s campus is on a hillside, and walking from the nearest bus stop or shuttle drop-off point requires some uphill effort. Most students rely on the free shuttle bus that runs between campus and University Station (East Rail Line). The shuttle operates on a fixed schedule during term time; outside those hours, public buses (e.g., KMB route 74K or minibus 26) serve the area.

![The Education University of Hong Kong](https://img.unistay.net/housing/p/amber-2337442.jpg)

**Walking to campus** is realistically only an option if you live in Ting Kok or along Lo Ping Road, where a few village houses and low-rise apartments exist. Even then, the walk can take 15–25 minutes uphill. Most residents in Tai Po town centre take a bus or minibus (5–10 minutes) to the campus entrance.

**Transit from farther away**:

- From Tai Po Market MTR: 15 minutes by bus to campus.
- From University Station: shuttle bus (5 minutes) or bus (10–15 minutes).
- From Sha Tin: 30–40 minutes total (MTR + shuttle).
- From Kowloon (e.g., Kowloon Tong): 50–60 minutes.
- From Hong Kong Island (e.g., Admiralty): 70–90 minutes.

If you have early classes or late study sessions, a shorter commute is a genuine quality-of-life advantage. Conversely, if your social life and part-time jobs are in Kowloon or Hong Kong Island, you may accept a longer ride in exchange for more opportunities.

### Shortlist Logic for Choosing a Residence

Without a standardised university-managed housing list, students must rely on private rentals and online platforms. Here is a logical shortlisting approach for 2026/2027:

1. **Define your maximum acceptable commute time**. If you want under 30 minutes door-to-door, focus on Tai Po and Ting Kok. If 45–60 minutes is fine, Sha Tin and Kowloon Tong open up.
2. **Check shuttle bus schedule compatibility**. EdUHK’s shuttle runs from University Station. Living near that station (e.g., in Ma On Shan or Fo Tan) can give you a fast connection, but you’ll need to transfer at Tai Po Market or University.
3. **Prioritise areas with direct bus routes to campus**. Buses 74K, 26, and 28K serve the university. Properties near bus stops on Ting Kok Road (between Tai Po Market and the campus) are especially convenient.
4. **Compare rent per square foot**. Tai Po offers lower rent than Sha Tin or Kowloon for similar space. A typical 500–600 sq ft flat in Tai Po costs around HK$11,000–HK$15,000 total; dividing among 2–3 tenants can keep individual rent under HK$5,000. In Kowloon Tong, the same budget might only cover a shared room.
5. **Visit the neighbourhood at night**. Some areas of Tai Po (e.g., near the Old Town) can be quiet after 10 pm. If you need evening access to shops or study spots, choose a location near Tai Po Plaza or the MTR station.
6. **Verify lease terms and utilities**. Many Hong Kong leases are for 12 months with a two-month deposit. Check whether water, electricity, and internet are included. For student housing operators, confirm all fees and cancellation policies in writing.
