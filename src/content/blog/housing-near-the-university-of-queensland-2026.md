## Housing near The University of Queensland

The University of Queensland (UQ) sits on a 114-hectare campus in St Lucia, about 7 km south-west of Brisbane’s city centre. For students considering accommodation, the surrounding suburbs offer varied options within walking distance or a short commute. This guide covers what campus reviews tell us about the area, city context, budget considerations, the trade‑off between walking and public transport, and how to approach a shortlist — using only the publicly available information and cited student feedback.

## City Context: Brisbane

![The University of Queensland](https://img.findstay.org/housing/p/amber-2005720.jpg)

Brisbane is a subtropical city with warm summers and mild winters. The St Lucia campus is located on a bend of the Brisbane River, close to suburbs such as Toowong, Taringa, Indooroopilly, and St Lucia itself. Public transport includes buses (many with frequent services to the city and other suburbs), the CityCat ferry (with a stop at St Lucia), and train stations at Toowong and Indooroopilly. Cycling paths also connect the university to nearby residential areas.

Rental demand near UQ is driven by a mix of domestic and international students, young professionals, and families. The area has a range of housing types: older Queenslander houses converted into share houses, modern apartment blocks, and purpose‑built student accommodation (PBSA). Lease terms typically follow the academic calendar (February to November), though some operators offer year‑round rentals.

## Budget Bands

Student housing budgets near UQ typically fall into three broad categories. Note that all prices below are indicative — confirm with the operator.

**Lower budget** – Shared houses or units in suburbs slightly further from campus (e.g., St Lucia, Taringa, or even Milton). Students rent a single room and share kitchen, bathroom, and living areas. Weekly rents in this segment are generally the most affordable, but walking distance to campus is often 20–30 minutes or requires a short bus ride.

**Mid‑range** – Studio apartments or one‑bedroom flats in buildings within 1–2 km of campus. Some purpose‑built student residences also fall into this bracket and may offer inclusive utilities and internet. Expect a higher weekly cost than a shared room but with more privacy and a shorter commute.

**Higher budget** – Modern apartments with river views, gyms, and concierge services, located very close to campus or along the CityCat route. This band suits students who prioritise convenience and amenities over savings.

## Walk vs. Transit Trade‑off

The decision between walking and using public transport depends on your tolerance for time, cost, and convenience.

**Walking** – Properties within about 15 minutes’ walk of the main campus (the area roughly bounded by Hawken Drive, Sir Fred Schonell Drive, and University Road) allow you to avoid bus or ferry schedules and save on transit fares. Many students living in St Lucia proper or in parts of Toowong (near the busway) can walk. The downside: these properties often command a premium. Also, Brisbane’s summer heat and occasional storms can make a longer walk less pleasant.

**Public transport** – Buses (routes 411, 412, 414, 427, 428, 29, etc.) and the CityCat ferry connect suburbs like Indooroopilly, Taringa, and Milton to UQ within 5–15 minutes. Students who live further out can trade a slightly longer door‑to‑door journey for lower rent. A bus‑only commute from suburbs such as West End or Highgate Hill may take 20–30 minutes. Ferry users on the CityCat (St Lucia stop) enjoy a scenic trip, but services are less frequent than buses. Train users at Toowong or Indooroopilly need a connecting bus or a bike.

The review by Ashar Malik and Katy Jolly both emphasise that UQ is “easily accessible by public transport”. This suggests that living beyond easy walking distance is not a significant barrier, as long as the property is near a bus stop or ferry terminal.
