### City Context: Dublin as a Student Hub

Dublin is Ireland’s capital and its economic and cultural centre. The city has a compact city centre, and Trinity’s location on College Green means it is within walking distance of many key areas: Grafton Street (shopping), Temple Bar (nightlife and arts), St Stephen’s Green (park), and Dublin Castle. Public transport includes Dublin Bus, the Luas tram lines (Red and Green), and commuter rail (DART).

Rental demand in Dublin is high, particularly near universities. According to the housing sample provided, weekly rents for student accommodation in Dublin start from approximately €219 (indicative — confirm with the operator). This figure typically refers to a room in a shared apartment or a purpose-built student accommodation (PBSA). Actual prices vary significantly by location, property type, and amenities. It is common to find studios, en-suite rooms, and cluster apartments across a range of budgets.

### Walk vs Transit Trade-off

Living close to Trinity offers clear convenience but often at a higher price. Rooms within a 15-minute walk of College Green tend to fall in the mid-to-premium range. For example, ‘Yugo Kavanagh Court’ is located on Dorset Street, about a 20-minute walk north of the campus. ‘Canvas Point Campus’ is similarly around 20–25 minutes on foot. ‘Point Campus’ and ‘Gobritanya Point Campus’ are situated near the Point Village area, closer to the Docklands — a 25–30 minute walk or a short Luas ride (Red Line to Busáras, then walk). ‘Charles Street Great, Mountjoy’ (a room in a 5-bedroom apartment) is near the northeast city centre, roughly 15–20 minutes walk.

Choosing a slightly farther location can reduce rent. For instance, rooms in Mountjoy or the north inner city may be €20–€50 per week cheaper than equivalent rooms closer to Grafton Street. The trade-off is a longer commute. Dublin’s bus network is extensive, but peak-hour traffic can be heavy. The Luas Red Line runs through the docklands and stops at Busáras (near O’Connell Street), about a 10-minute walk from Trinity. A student Leap Card offers discounted fares.

Walking is generally the fastest and most reliable option for distances under 30 minutes. Buses and trams are useful for areas like Rathmines, Ranelagh, or Donnybrook (south side), but these are not covered in the sample provided.
