## What Campus Reviews Reveal

Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF) is a public university founded in 1990 and named after the Catalan linguist Pompeu Fabra. Its main campus sits in the Ciutat Vella district of Barcelona, specifically in the Vila Olímpica neighbourhood near Ciutadella Park and the Barcelona Zoo. With a QS World University Ranking of 265 and a Google rating of 3.8 based on 176 reviews, the institution draws a mix of local and international students. The reviews present a mixed picture: the campus architecture and location are widely praised, and the economics programme has a strong reputation. However, some students question the depth of coursework, especially in the Master in Management. As you look for housing near UPF, consider how the campus’s central position in Barcelona’s Olympic Village gives you access to green spaces, the beach, and the city’s public transport network.

## City Context: Barcelona and the UPF Campus

Barcelona is one of Europe’s most popular student cities, offering a Mediterranean climate, a vibrant cultural scene, and extensive public transport. UPF’s main address is Carrer de la Mercè, 12, Ciutat Vella, 08002 Barcelona, Spain. This puts you in the historic centre, within walking distance of the Gothic Quarter, Barceloneta beach, and the port. The Vila Olímpica area, where the campus’s lecture halls and library are concentrated, was redeveloped for the 1992 Olympics and today features modern buildings, wide promenades, and a relaxed atmosphere.

For students, the key advantage is connectivity. The Barcelona metro (L4 line at Ciutadella – Vila Olímpica station) and multiple bus routes link the campus to the rest of the city. Biking is also popular, with dedicated lanes along the seafront. The main challenge is that demand for housing in the city centre is high, and prices have risen steadily over the past decade.

## Walk vs. Transit Trade-Off

Your decision about where to live will largely hinge on how you value time versus cost. Here is a breakdown:

- **Walking distance (≤15 minutes)**: Properties around Vila Olímpica, Barceloneta, and the eastern edge of Ciutat Vella. You save on transport costs and have the flexibility to go home between classes. The trade-off is higher rent and potentially more noise from the tourist-heavy areas.
- **Short transit (15–25 minutes by metro/bus)**: Districts such as Poblenou, Sant Antoni, or parts of Gràcia. Rent tends to be 10–20% lower than in the immediate campus zone, and you still have a quick trip. A monthly metro pass (T-Jove or T-Usual) costs around EUR 40–60 for unlimited travel within the city.
- **Longer commute (25–40 minutes)**: Outer districts like Les Corts, Sants-Montjuïc, or L’Hospitalet de Llobregat. Rent can be significantly cheaper (EUR 250–350/week range), but you must account for travel time and the fact that public transport can become crowded during peak hours. If your classes start early or end late, consider the frequency of night buses.

For most UPF students, the sweet spot is a 20-minute metro ride, balancing affordability with convenience. Many choose to live along the L4 metro line (yellow) that runs directly to Ciutadella – Vila Olímpica station.

### How to Choose

1. **Compile a list of your deal-breakers** – walking time, maximum rent per week, need for an en-suite bathroom, access to a gym or study room.
2. **Filter by district** – If you value short commutes, focus on properties within 2 km of UPF (e.g., Diagonal 1, Rosselló 2, Sant Pau). If you need lower rent, expand to Sants-Montjuïc or Les Corts.
3. **Compare transport options** – Check the nearest metro line and bus frequency. The L4 (yellow) line is the most direct for UPF, so any residence with easy access to L4 will save time.
4. **Visit or video-call** – Always confirm the actual condition of the property, as online photos can be misleading. Ask about utilities included (water, electricity, internet) and the contract length (often 9–11 months).
5. **Read the lease carefully** – Typical Spanish rental contracts require a deposit of one or two months’ rent. Additional fees (agency or booking) should be clarified upfront. For international students, some operators offer flexible move-in dates aligned with the academic calendar.
