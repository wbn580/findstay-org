## What Campus Reviews Reveal

University of California, Berkeley (UCB) ranked 17 in the QS World University Rankings in 2026, with a Google overall rating of 3.8 (over 2100 reviews). These reviews reflect the genuine feelings of students and parents about the campus experience, encompassing both high recognition and specific criticism.

**Positive voices**: A 5-star user, Guadalupe Morales Garfias, wrote: “Very significantly historic university. There is many good stories to everything from the floor to the structure of the buildings. Students here achieve academic excellence and fight for what they believe. Students here fight for justice and come together to be able to do so.” This passage summarizes UCB’s core traits—a historically rich campus, an atmosphere of academic excellence, and a tradition of students actively engaging in social justice. Another 5-star user, I State Bald Facts, humorously wrote: “Fire. I spend my entire family’s saving on this 3-course meal. The food was awful, they however handed me a certification of degree completion. That I am happy with.” Although exaggerating about the food, ultimately recognizing the value of the degree.

**Negative feedback**: 5-star user Mirtha Hernandez pointed out issues with commencement organization: “Great school. Commencement needs to be better organized. If we pay $21 to park, there should be a shuttle there, not 3 blocks away. No one to help find seats, numbers are faded away. Long list of forbidden items and then I see people carrying items on that list.” Such complaints about administrative details are worth noting for relatives planning to attend graduation. More concerning is the experience of 1-star user zey ka: “I had a negative experience with the representative Bill during my application process for the Data Science Master’s program. He asked several irrelevant and uncomfortable questions, and his attitude made me feel discriminated against and racially profiled.” This reminds prospective applicants to be vigilant when communicating with admissions or administrative staff, and to provide feedback through official channels if necessary.

Overall, UCB’s academic reputation and student activity vitality are recognized, but administrative processes and individual staff attitudes may affect the experience. It is recommended that prospective students obtain first-hand impressions through campus visits, student forums, and direct communication with current students, rather than relying solely on online ratings.

## Berkeley City Environment and Accommodation Budget

Berkeley is a quintessential college town, located on the eastern shore of the San Francisco Bay Area with a mild climate and diverse culture. The area around campus is filled with bookstores, cafes, restaurants, and various student services. However, the cost of living in Berkeley is relatively high across the United States—especially for apartments close to campus, where rent is steep.

According to data from early 2026, there are a total of 13 accommodation options open to students, with starting prices around 595 USD/month (indicative — confirm with the operator). This price typically corresponds to a single bed in a shared apartment or a small studio, and may be within commuting distance. Actual budget distribution is roughly as follows:

- **Economy (600–1,000 USD/month)**: Usually requires a longer commute (bus or BART), or choosing a multi-person shared arrangement. These options are limited and require early application.
- **Mid-range (1,000–1,800 USD/month)**: A single room or studio within walking or short biking distance to campus, or a shared unit in a more central location.
- **High budget (1,800 USD/month and above)**: Fully equipped independent apartments or newly built student apartments near the campus center, offering more private space.

Please note that all prices are indicative; actual rent fluctuates with seasonality, supply and demand, and lease variations. It is recommended to start searching as soon as enrollment is confirmed and to contact the apartment operators directly to confirm current prices and availability.

## Walking and Transit Trade-offs

![University of California, Berkeley (UCB)](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-290730.jpg)

The UCB campus is centralized and compact; walking from one end to the other usually takes no more than 15 minutes. Ideally, living within walking distance of campus (within 15 minutes) maximizes time savings and campus life enjoyment. However, rents in central Berkeley are often 30%–50% higher than in the surrounding areas. Typical monthly rent for a place within walking distance is usually above 1,200 USD.

**Choosing public transit** is a primary way to reduce living costs. The Downtown Berkeley station of BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit) is right next to the south side of campus, providing quick connections to Oakland and San Francisco. Students living in Oakland or Downtown San Francisco have a one-way commute of about 30–45 minutes, but can choose cheaper or larger accommodations. For example, some student apartments in San Francisco may start around 600 USD (with shared housing), but living costs (food, transportation) must also be considered.

**Cycling** is a very popular and flexible option. Berkeley's terrain has slight undulations but gentle slopes, and cycling for 15 minutes can cover a large area. The campus has many bike racks, and BART trains allow folding bikes.

**Trade-off advice**: If your budget is below 900 USD/month and you don't mind commuting, prioritize locations along BART (such as near Oakland's 19th Street or 12th Street stations). If social convenience and academic atmosphere are more important and you are willing to pay above 1,200 USD, choose within walking distance of campus. For mid-term planning (junior, senior, or graduate students), try living near campus for one semester to get to know the city, then adjust based on actual needs.
