### City Context: Irvine

Irvine is a planned city in Orange County, California, known for its low crime rates, extensive cycling paths, and master-planned communities. The median household income is among the highest in the U.S., which is reflected in housing costs. The city is approximately 40 miles south of Los Angeles and a 15-minute drive to the Pacific coast. Public transportation includes the OC Bus system and the Irvine Station for Metrolink trains, but many residents rely on cars.
