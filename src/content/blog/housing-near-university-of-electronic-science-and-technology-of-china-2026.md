### Walk vs Transit Trade-Off

The campus is situated on a main road (Jian She Bei Lu) and is served by multiple public transport options. Walking distance to the campus is generally 1–2 km for most residential blocks in the immediate vicinity. Students who choose to live within a 1 km radius can walk to classes in 10–15 minutes, saving on transport costs and time. The trade-off is that apartments closer to the campus often command higher rents, especially if they are in newer buildings.

Public transit in Chenghua District includes several bus lines and the Chengdu Metro. The nearest metro stations are approximately 1–1.5 km from the main campus gate, a 15–20 minute walk or a 5-minute bus ride. Taking the metro expands the housing search to areas like Donghu or Shawan, where rents may be lower for equivalent space. However, the daily commute including waiting and transfer time can add 30–40 minutes per trip. Bicycles and electric scooters are also popular; rental or purchase of a bike can shorten commute time and provide flexibility. For those who prioritise budget over convenience, living 3–4 metro stops away and combining a short bus ride with walking is a common strategy.
