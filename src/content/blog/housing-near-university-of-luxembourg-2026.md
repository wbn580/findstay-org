### Housing Near University of Luxembourg: A Practical Guide

The University of Luxembourg sits at the heart of the Belval district in Esch-sur-Alzette, a purpose-built scientific and cultural hub about 20 minutes by train from Luxembourg City. With a QS World University Ranking of 381 and a Google rating of 4.3 based on 449 reviews, the university draws a diverse, multilingual student body. Finding accommodation nearby requires understanding the campus environment, local housing costs, and the trade-offs between walking and commuting. This guide walks through what students say, the city’s housing landscape, budget bands, transit options, and a logical shortlisting approach for the 2026 and 2027 academic years.

#### City Context: Luxembourg’s Housing Landscape

Luxembourg has a tight rental market, especially near the university. The country’s high income levels and limited housing supply push rents up. In Esch-sur-Alzette and the Belval area, studios and one-bedroom apartments are the most common options for students, though shared flats (colocations) are also available. Many students live in purpose-built student residences run by the Luxembourg Student Housing Office (known as SEVE) or private operators.

The city of Luxembourg itself is a 15–20 minute train ride from the Belval campus, costing roughly €2–4 per trip with student discounts. Some students choose to live in the city centre for access to more nightlife, restaurants, and shops, trading commuting time for convenience. Others prefer staying in Esch or even smaller surrounding towns like Belvaux or Soleuvre to keep rent lower and walk or bike to campus.

Important for 2026 and 2027 planning: Luxembourg’s population continues to grow, and new residential projects are under way in the Belval area. However, supply remains constrained. Starting your search early (6–8 months before move-in) is strongly advised.

#### Budget Bands for Student Housing

Rents vary significantly depending on location, room type, and whether utilities are included. All figures below are indicative — confirm with the operator.

- **Budget-friendly (€400–€600 per month)**: A room in a shared flat (colocation) in Esch-sur-Alzette or a nearby suburb. Typically includes internet and basic utilities. These rooms are often smaller (10–15 m²) and may require a shared kitchen and bathroom.
- **Mid-range (€600–€900 per month)**: A studio or a larger private room in a purpose-built student residence in the Belval area or central Esch. Many of these are within a 10–15 minute walk of campus. Utilities are usually included.
- **Upper range (€900–€1,300+ per month)**: A one-bedroom apartment in Luxembourg City or in a brand-new residence near Belval. These offer more space and privacy but come at a premium.

Shared flats remain the most economical choice. According to student forums, a typical colocation in Esch costs around €500–€650 per month including charges, while in Luxembourg City the same type of room can reach €700–€900. Purpose-built studios in Belval start at about €650–€750.

#### Walk vs Transit: How to Choose Your Location

The Belval campus is in a pedestrian-friendly zone. Many academic buildings, the university library, and the Rockhal music venue are within a 10-minute walk of one another. If you live in Belval itself, you can walk to class in under 15 minutes. That’s a major advantage — no commuting costs, no schedule dependency, and more time for study or social activities.

If you live in Esch-sur-Alzette centre, the walk to campus takes about 25–30 minutes, or you can take bus lines that run every 10–15 minutes. A monthly bus pass for students costs approximately €30–€40. Living in Luxembourg City means a 15–20 minute train ride plus a 5–10 minute walk on either end. The train is reliable and frequent (every 10–20 minutes), but the round-trip commute adds about an hour daily.

![University of Luxembourg](https://img.unistay.cn/housing/p/amber-258006.jpg)

The trade-off is simple: walkability saves time and money but often comes with a higher rent because Belval and central Esch are in higher demand. Transit access gives you more affordable options further out but adds daily travel time and expense. For students with early classes or heavy workloads, walking is usually the better choice. For those on a tighter budget, living further out and commuting can bring monthly rent down by €100–€200.

#### Shortlist Logic: How to Evaluate Your Options

When shortlisting housing for the 2026 or 2027 academic year, work through these five filters:

1. **Distance to campus** – Ideally within a 20-minute walk. If not, check the bus or train frequency and travel time. Google Maps transit directions are reliable.
2. **Rent all-in vs. plus utilities** – Many student flats list rent “all charges included.” Verify what’s covered (water, electricity, internet, heating). Some require a separate utility bill.
3. **Lease duration** – Most student residences offer 12-month contracts. If you only need 10 months (e.g., from September to June), ask about early termination or subletting options.
4. **Furnished vs. unfurnished** – International students almost always need furnished accommodation. Confirm that the unit includes bed, desk, chair, kitchen appliances, and basic storage.
5. **Deposit and contract terms** – Expect to pay a deposit equal to 1–3 months’ rent. Check the notice period (typically 1–3 months) and any penalties for breaking the lease early.

If you are a first-year student, consider applying for a room in a recognised student residence first. These often include social events, laundry facilities, and a sense of community. Private colocations are more variable — inspect the flat or request a video tour.

For the 2027 intake, especially if applying to the MSc in Data Analytics mentioned in the reviews, start your housing search as soon as you receive your admission letter. Popular residences fill up by March or April for a September start.

### FAQ: Housing Near University of Luxembourg

**1. How early should I start looking for student housing in Luxembourg for the 2026–2027 academic year?**

Begin your search at least six months before your planned move-in date. For example, if you are arriving in September 2026, start browsing listings and contacting residences in March or April 2026. Many purpose-built student residences open waiting lists in spring. Late applications (after May) will have limited choices, especially for the more affordable options near the Belval campus.

**2. Is it better to live in Luxembourg City or in Esch-sur-Alzette as a student at the University of Luxembourg?**

It depends on your priorities. Live in Esch-sur-Alzette or Belval if you want to walk to campus and keep your daily schedule flexible. You’ll also save on commuting costs. Live in Luxembourg City if you prefer a larger variety of restaurants, cultural events, and social life, and are willing to spend about an hour each day commuting. For Master’s students with intense coursework, the walkable option is often wise. For PhD students or those with lighter timetables, city life can be worth the commute.

**3. What is the average rent for a studio apartment near the university, and are utilities included?**

A studio in the Belval area or central Esch typically costs between **€600 and €900 per month**. Many student residences list rent as “all charges included” (water, electricity, internet). Confirm this in writing. Private studios with separate utility bills may appear cheaper but can end up costing more during winter months. Always ask for a breakdown before signing.
