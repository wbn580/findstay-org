### City context: Dubai Academic City and surroundings

Zayed University’s address is Al Rowaiyah Second – Academic City – Dubai. The area is a dense cluster of colleges and university campuses, roughly 30 minutes by car from Dubai International Airport and about 20 minutes from the Dubai Mall. Public transport options are limited compared to central Dubai; the nearest metro station (Dubai Silicon Oasis or Al Rashidiya) requires a bus or taxi transfer. Most students rely on university shuttles, private cars, or ride-hailing apps.

The broader Dubai housing market offers a wide price spectrum. For student budgets, the indicative weekly rents start from 220 AED (approximately 60 USD) for shared accommodations in areas like Al Sufouh or Dubailand, and can rise to 600–800 AED per week for standalone studios near Academic City. ### Walk vs transit trade-off

Walking is not a practical option for most students. The university sits within a car-oriented zone with few pedestrian-friendly paths connecting residential compounds. On-campus housing is limited to a few dormitory blocks, which are usually reserved for first-year students or certain programmes. If you live off campus, you will need to arrange transport.

![Zayed University](https://img.findstay.org/housing/p/amber-2265510.jpg)

- **Walking:** Only feasible if you live inside Academic City itself. A handful of apartment buildings (e.g., Makaan, a nearby residence) are within a 10–15 minute walk. Check if your potential housing is inside the same security gated area.
- **Public transport:** Dubai’s Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) buses operate along Academic City Road (lines 367, 369). The nearest metro station is about 8 km away. A bus trip combined with a metro journey can take 45–60 minutes one way. Monthly passes cost about 300 AED (indicative).
- **University shuttles:** Many students use Zayed University’s own shuttle service, which runs between the campus and key residential hubs. Schedules vary by semester; verify with the student affairs office.
- **Private car or ride-hailing:** A typical trip from Dubailand to campus costs 30–50 AED by Uber. Carpool groups are common among students.

**Trade-off advice:** If you have early morning classes or evening exams, paying a bit more for a shuttle-friendly residence (like Yugo Dubailand) may reduce stress. If you have a flexible schedule, a cheaper shared room further out (e.g., Al Sufouh) can work, as long as you account for travel time.
