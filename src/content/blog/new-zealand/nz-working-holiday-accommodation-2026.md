New Zealand's Working Holiday Visa (WHV) is one of the world's best programs — 12 months (or 23 for UK/Canada citizens) of work and travel in one of the most beautiful countries on Earth. But the first month's accommodation can be tricky, especially if you're chasing seasonal work.

## First Week: Where to Land

Most WHV holders arrive in Auckland or Christchurch. Book 4–7 nights at a hostel:

- **Auckland**: YHA Auckland City, Haka Lodge, Attic Backpackers. NZD 30–40/night for a dorm bed.
- **Christchurch**: Jailhouse Accommodation (yes, a former prison — a classic WHV experience). NZD 28–38/night.
- **Wellington**: The Dwellington, Trek Global. NZD 30–40/night.

Use your first week to: get an IRD number (tax), open a bank account, buy a SIM card, and decide where you're actually going.

## Seasonal Worker Housing

### Fruit Picking & Horticulture

The main WHV work sectors. Regions and seasons:

- **Hawke's Bay**: Jan–May · Apples, grapes · Hostels NZD 150–250/week; some orchards provide worker dorms
- **Central Otago**: Dec–Mar · Cherries, apricots · Very tight — book early. Dorm-style accommodation from NZD 180/week
- **Bay of Plenty**: Mar–Jun · Kiwifruit · Te Puke is the center. Hostels and orchard-provided housing from NZD 160/week
- **Marlborough**: Feb–Apr · Grapes (vintage) · Blenheim has hostel and camping options. Vintage season is intense — 12-hour days, 6 days/week


### Ski Season (June–October)

Queenstown and Wanaka are the main ski towns. Accommodation is EXPENSIVE and SCARCE during winter. Staff housing from NZD 180–250/week if you work for the ski field. Otherwise, shared houses in Queenstown run NZD 250–400/week per room.

**Pro tip**: Apply for jobs that include accommodation. Cardrona, Treble Cone, and The Remarkables all offer staff housing — though it's often dorm-style and basic.

## Long-Term Flatting

After you've settled into a region, flatting is the way to go. The NZ flatting culture is very WHV-friendly — flats are used to people coming and going. Find flats on:
- **Trade Me Property** (tradeMe.co.nz)
- **NZ Flatmates** Facebook groups (region-specific)
- **Backpacker Board** (hostel noticeboards — seriously, still a thing in NZ)

## FAQ

**Do I need a car for a WHV?** For city-based work, no. For fruit picking in rural areas, yes — or at least make friends with someone who has one. The "buy a cheap car, road trip, sell it" is a classic WHV move.
**Can I extend my WHV?** UK and Canadian citizens can get 23 or 36 months depending on the scheme. Other nationalities: 12 months, extension possible if you do 3 months of specified regional work.
**What's the minimum I should arrive with?** Immigration suggests NZD 4,200 for living costs for the first few months. Realistically, NZD 5,000–7,000 is safer given housing deposits.
