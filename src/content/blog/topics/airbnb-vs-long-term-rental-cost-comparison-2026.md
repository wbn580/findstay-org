The calculus between booking an Airbnb and signing a long-term lease has shifted. In 2026, with rental prices up 20–40% in most student cities and Airbnb adding service fees, the math isn't always obvious. Here's a city-by-city breakdown.

## Sydney, Australia

- **Studio CBD**: AUD 3,200–4,500 · AUD 2,600–3,200
- **Shared room**: AUD 1,400–2,000 · AUD 1,100–1,500
- **Utilities included?**: ✅ Yes · ❌ Add AUD 150–250
- **Furniture included?**: ✅ Yes · Often ❌ (add AUD 500–1,500 upfront)


**Verdict**: Airbnb is 50–80% more expensive. Only use for 2–4 weeks of temporary accommodation while you find a lease.

## London, UK

- **Studio Zone 1**: £2,500–3,800 · £1,500–2,400
- **Room in flatshare**: £1,200–1,800 · £700–1,200


**Verdict**: Airbnb costs roughly double. The gap is widening as London's new short-term rental regulations (90-night annual cap) reduce supply.

## New York City

- **Studio Manhattan**: USD 4,500–7,000 · USD 2,800–3,800
- **Room in Brooklyn**: USD 2,000–3,000 · USD 1,200–1,800


**Verdict**: NYC effectively banned short-term rentals under 30 days unless the host is present (Local Law 18). Monthly Airbnb in NYC barely exists legally. Long-term lease is the only practical option.

## The "Medium-Term" Sweet Spot

For stays of 1–3 months (semester exchange, internship, short course):

- **Coliving operators** (Common, Ollie, Quarters) offer furnished rooms with flexible 1–6 month leases. USD 900–1,800/month depending on city.
- **University summer housing** — many universities rent dorm rooms to non-students during summer. AUD 250–400/week in Australia, £150–250/week in the UK. Incredible value.
- **Sublets** — Find students leaving for exchange or internship. Often furnished, often discounted. Check university housing Facebook groups.

## When Airbnb Makes Sense

1. **First 2–4 weeks in a new country** — use it as a base while you attend inspections
2. **Short courses under 1 month** — the transaction costs of a lease (bond, agency fees, furniture) don't justify a short stay
3. **Group bookings** — a 3-bedroom Airbnb split 3 ways can beat 3 individual hostel/hotel rooms

## Hidden Costs of Long-Term Leasing

Where Airbnb's all-inclusive pricing can close the gap:

- **Furniture**: Furnishing a studio from scratch costs USD 800–2,000 (IKEA). Over 12 months, that's USD 70–170/month.
- **Utilities setup**: Connection fees, deposits, and the time cost of setting up accounts
- **Bond cash flow**: 4–6 weeks' rent locked up in bond until lease end
- **Early termination**: Breaking a lease can cost 1–2 months' rent

## The Bottom Line

For stays under 1 month: Airbnb or hostel.
For stays 1–3 months: Target coliving, sublets, or university summer housing.
For stays 3+ months: Long-term lease significantly cheaper. Use Airbnb/Hostel for the first 2–4 weeks.
