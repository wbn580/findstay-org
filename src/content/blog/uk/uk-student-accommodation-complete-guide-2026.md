The UK hosts over 600,000 international students across 160+ universities. The student housing landscape varies dramatically between London and the rest of the country — a room near LSE costs £250/week, while a similar room near the University of Sheffield might be £85/week.

## Accommodation Types

### University Halls of Residence
First-year international students are typically guaranteed a place. Halls are either catered (meals included) or self-catered (shared kitchen). £120–280/week depending on city and room type.

### Private Purpose-Built Student Accommodation (PBSA)
Unite Students, iQ Student, Student Roost, and Chapter dominate the market. En-suite rooms in cluster flats (5–8 people sharing a kitchen) or self-contained studios. £140–380/week.

### Private Rented Sector (PRS)
Renting directly from a landlord or through a letting agent. Typically a 12-month assured shorthold tenancy (AST). You'll need a UK guarantor or 6–12 months' rent upfront.

## Tenancy Rights

Since 2019, most fees for tenants have been banned (Tenant Fees Act):
- ❌ No letting agent fees
- ❌ No credit check fees
- ❌ No inventory fees
- ✅ Holding deposit capped at 1 week's rent
- ✅ Security deposit capped at 5 weeks' rent

Your deposit must be protected in a government-approved scheme (TDS, MyDeposits, or DPS) within 30 days. If it isn't, you can claim compensation.

## Council Tax

Full-time students are exempt from council tax. You need a council tax exemption certificate from your university — usually downloadable from your student portal. If you live with non-students, the household still gets a 25% discount if only one non-student lives there, but you lose the exemption if there are two or more non-students.

## FAQ

**Do I need a UK guarantor?** Most private landlords require one — a UK resident who agrees to pay your rent if you can't. Housing Hand and YourGuarantor offer guarantor services for international students (for a fee, typically 4–6% of annual rent).
**Is PBSA worth the premium?** For your first year, yes. It's the lowest-stress option — no guarantor needed, all-inclusive bills, on-site management, and a built-in social network. After year one, many students move to private rentals with friends.
**What about IHS and health cover?** The Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) gives NHS access. For travel outside the UK, [SafetyWing](https://safetywing.com/?affiliate_id=17632) covers gap periods.
