Chicago is America's best-value major city for students. World-class universities, incredible architecture, and rent that's 50–70% cheaper than NYC or San Francisco. The L trains connect everything efficiently, and Lake Michigan provides a beach lifestyle that surprises most newcomers.

### UChicago (Hyde Park)

**Hyde Park**: The neighborhood surrounding UChicago. Tree-lined streets, Obama's house, the Museum of Science and Industry. Rooms USD 700–1,200/month. Way below other top-10 university neighborhoods.

**Kenwood**: North of Hyde Park. Mix of historic mansions and apartment buildings. Rooms USD 650–1,100/month.

**South Loop**: 15 min Metra train or 20 min on the #6 bus. Downtown living at lower prices than the North Side. Rooms USD 800–1,400/month.

### Northwestern (Evanston)

**Evanston**: The university's own city just north of Chicago. Walkable downtown, lakefront parks. Purple Line L to Chicago in 45 min. Rooms USD 700–1,300/month.

**Rogers Park**: Chicago's northernmost neighborhood on the Red Line, bordering Evanston. Extremely diverse — 80+ languages spoken. 15 min to NU campus. Rooms USD 600–1,100/month.

### DePaul & UIC (City)

**Lincoln Park**: Near DePaul's main campus. Affluent, beautiful, near the lake and the Lincoln Park Zoo. Rooms USD 800–1,500/month.

**Wicker Park & Logan Square**: The hipster corridor along the Blue Line. Great for DePaul loop campus students. Rooms USD 700–1,300/month.

**Pilsen**: Near UIC. Chicago's Mexican-American cultural heart. Murals everywhere, incredible tacos. 5 min Pink Line to UIC. Rooms USD 600–1,100/month.

### FAQ

**Is Chicago dangerous?** Hyde Park, Lincoln Park, and Evanston are very safe. Some South and West Side neighborhoods have higher crime rates. Students almost never end up in those areas.
**Can I manage without a car?** Absolutely. The CTA (L trains + buses) is one of America's best transit systems.
**What's the winter like?** Brutal. January average -6°C with wind chill much worse. Lake effect snow is real. Invest in a serious winter coat.
