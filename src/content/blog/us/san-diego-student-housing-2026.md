San Diego is America's finest city in terms of weather — 22°C and sunny, 263 days per year. The University of California San Diego (one of the country's top STEM schools), San Diego State University, and the University of San Diego make it a major student hub. Housing is expensive (California) but you're paying for perfect weather, beaches, and a famously chill lifestyle.

### UCSD (La Jolla)

**UTC (University Towne Centre)**: The main off-campus housing zone for UCSD. Modern apartment complexes around the Westfield UTC mall. 5–10 min shuttle to campus. Rooms USD 900–1,600/month. The shuttle bus (free with student ID) is reliable.

**La Jolla Village**: Walking distance to campus. Extremely expensive — La Jolla is one of America's wealthiest zip codes. Rooms USD 1,200–2,200/month. Some students share 1-bedrooms to make it work.

**Mira Mesa**: 10–15 min drive north, significantly cheaper. Large Asian community with excellent Vietnamese, Korean, and Filipino food along Mira Mesa Boulevard. Rooms USD 800–1,300/month.

**Pacific Beach (PB)**: 15 min drive south. The surf-and-party neighborhood. Boardwalk bars, breakfast burritos (Kono's Cafe), and a permanent vacation vibe. Rooms USD 900–1,600/month.

### SDSU (College Area)

**College Area**: The neighborhoods immediately surrounding SDSU. Older single-family homes converted to student rentals. Rooms USD 800–1,400/month. The trolley (Green Line) connects to downtown in 25 minutes.

**La Mesa**: East of campus on the Green Line. More suburban, slightly cheaper. La Mesa Village has a walkable downtown with restaurants and a farmers market. Rooms USD 700–1,200/month.

**North Park & Normal Heights**: Between SDSU and downtown. San Diego's hipster central — craft beer, vintage shops, and a genuinely diverse food scene. 10 min drive to SDSU. Rooms USD 800–1,500/month.

### USD (Linda Vista)

**Linda Vista**: The neighborhood around USD. Hilltop campus with bay views. Rooms USD 800–1,500/month.

**Ocean Beach (OB)**: 10 min from USD. The anti-PB — a 1960s hippie surf town that never changed. Dog Beach, the OB Pier, and Hodad's burgers. Rooms USD 900–1,500/month.

### Transport Reality

San Diego is a driving city. The trolley connects SDSU and downtown but misses UCSD (until the new Blue Line extension opens). Most UCSD students drive or shuttle. Budget for a car or at least carpool/Uber.

### FAQ

**Is San Diego as expensive as LA?** Roughly equal for housing. SD is slightly cheaper for going out. The difference is lifestyle — San Diego is more outdoorsy and relaxed, LA is more industry-driven.
**Can I surf before class?** At UCSD — yes. Black's Beach is a 15-minute walk from campus.
**Monthly budget?** USD 1,500–2,500 depending on neighborhood and whether you share a room.
