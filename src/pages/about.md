# About UniStay

**UniStay** is a comprehensive accommodation resource for anyone moving abroad — whether you're an international student starting university, an expat relocating for work, or a digital nomad looking for your next base.

## What We Cover

- **Student Accommodation** — Purpose-built student housing (PBSA), university dorms, private rentals near campuses in Australia, UK, US, Canada, NZ, Europe & Asia
- **Expat & Long-Stay Housing** — Apartments, co-living spaces, and rental market guides for professionals relocating overseas
- **Short-Term & Flexible Stays** — Airbnb alternatives, serviced apartments, and nomad-friendly housing
- **Health Insurance** — OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover) comparison for Australia, international student health plans, and travel insurance via SafetyWing

## Why UniStay?

Finding a place to live in a foreign country is one of the hardest parts of moving abroad. Rental scams, confusing lease terms, visa restrictions on housing — we break it all down in plain English, with real data and practical advice.

## How We Make Money

UniStay is reader-supported. When you purchase health insurance or book accommodation through links on our site, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This doesn't influence our recommendations — we only feature services we'd use ourselves. Read our [full disclosure](/disclaimer/).
